# Reference-led reset

A clean reset preserves the outcome and accepted truth, not the rejected solution. Restless must be
able to prove the producer received a fresh workspace and a bounded input set; asking an actor to
forget visible material is not a reset.

## Accountable lead: decide whether to reset

Use this protocol when the owner explicitly requests a fresh start, the same consequential quality
gap survives two revisions, or the candidate contradicts an authoritative product/brand source.
Prefer an ordinary revision when the current direction remains valid and the required change is local.

Before commissioning production, read
[the Reset Capsule contract](references/reset-capsule.md) and form one concise capsule. Resolve
contradictory instructions by recency and authority. A product's current rendered experience and
source outrank an external inspiration; external references calibrate maturity and component sources
supply mechanics.

## Create a genuinely fresh production boundary

1. Create **new producer Work**, not a new revision of the rejected Work. Same-Work successor Attempts
   intentionally consume prior output artifacts and prefer their source commit; that is continuity,
   not clean-room execution.
2. Bind the new Work to the accepted repository and an exact clean `--base-ref`. Give it a new
   bounded `--worktree` slug. Do not add `--requires` or `--revises` edges to rejected production.
3. Attach only a completed reference-pack Work when the producer needs material beyond the repository.
   That Work must have no repository coordinate and its output must be the Reset Capsule plus frozen
   authoritative references. Do not attach producer rationale, prior source, rejected screenshots or
   a transcript unless the capsule explicitly retains one as necessary factual evidence.
4. Use a fresh Staff producer. If the company mission contains stale task-specific implementation
   direction that conflicts with the capsule, do not pretend the context is clean: use a clean
   dogfood company or have the owner revise the mission before launch.
5. Declare build, type, route and native-review gates in the new Work. These prove integrity, never
   taste.

Before launch, prove the intended model route is usable rather than merely named. The company
default, Exec actor and commissioned Staff must agree on the model unless a deliberate override is
recorded. Confirm the provider-specific credential exists and is admitted by the running Runtime;
configured-but-disabled credentials and silent provider fallback invalidate the dogfood.

The producer may know concise rejected **observations** such as “the page felt text-heavy.” It must not
receive the rejected solution as a repair template. State what failed at the outcome level, then let
the authoritative references and current source determine the new expression.

## Producer: work reference-first

Read only the capsule, its attached references, the clean repository and applicable project skills
before choosing a direction. Inspect the authoritative product source before any external inspiration.
Do not open the rejected candidate, its branch or its rationale.

For visual work, render the smallest meaningful slice early. Compare the native result with the
reference packet before expanding scope. Repeat after each consequential slice. A successful build,
route response or clean layout manifest cannot approve visual quality.

Record the exact final commit and prepare factual native-review evidence. Producer Work must set
`owner_review_required=false`: it is an input to independent criticism, not an owner decision. Do
not declare or mutate an integration branch from producer Work. Keep publication or another external
effect outside this protocol unless separately authorised.

## Independent critic: compare before accepting

Create fresh critic Work that requires the new producer Work and may revise it. Give the critic the
outcome contract, Reset Capsule, exact live candidate and factual captures. Withhold producer
rationale and private reasoning.

Critic Work also sets `owner_review_required=false`. The critic may inspect but does not edit the
candidate. If it requests changes, commission repairs through new producer-owned Work and repeat the
independent check; do not turn praise or stage-transition messages into feedback on completed
producer Work because that can reopen it.

The critic must identify:

- where the candidate visibly follows each authoritative source;
- where it still drifts from product truth or maturity calibration;
- the three weakest rendered moments, even when accepting;
- whether copy is clear to a visitor without internal company knowledge; and
- whether desktop, mobile, reduced-motion, complete-page and closing/footer quality hold together.

Return `accept`, `changes_requested` or `reject`. Repairs remain producer-owned.

## Owner-facing admission

Only after an independent `accept`, create a third, final delivery/admission Work. It must:

1. start from the exact accepted commit and preserve its source tree unless a newly discovered
   integrity defect makes re-production necessary;
2. declare the candidate integration branch only at this stage;
3. set `owner_review_required=true` and link exactly one live native `ReviewTarget` plus its probe;
4. use the Runtime's ordinary supervised loopback HTTP ReviewTarget inside the company, then require
   the owner-facing cockpit to project that exact target through its ticketed isolated review origin;
   never present the internal loopback URI as the owner's review link; and
5. prohibit publishing, pushing or deployment unless the owner separately authorised it.

The accountable lead inspects the exact admitted outcome, uses `prepare-owner-brief`, then
`escalate-handoff`. Do not use `resolve-handoff` for a judgement that belongs to the owner. The team
must not announce completion until the owner attention item contains an openable ReviewTarget.

## Completion

Escalate one bounded owner decision only after the candidate is live, the clean provenance is
observable, deterministic gates pass, and the critic's consequential findings are resolved or
explicitly disagreed with from rendered evidence. Team acceptance and owner acceptance are distinct
states; a critic verdict may admit a candidate for owner review but cannot spend owner authority.

After the owner verdict, retain small reusable observations and authority choices in OrgIntel. Do not
turn the rejected implementation or full transcript into future default context.
