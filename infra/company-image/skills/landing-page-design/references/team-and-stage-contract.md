# Team and stage contract

## Coverage matrix

The lead records one accountable actor for each row before production.

| Responsibility | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Product/offer truth | Audience, page job, factual claims and conversion outcome |
| Art direction | Reference synthesis, tokens, layout idea and memorable signature |
| Copy/IA | Hero promise, section logic, route map and CTA vocabulary |
| Design engineering | Native implementation, motion, responsive and accessible states |
| Comparative criticism | Side-by-side rendered verdict independent of producer reasoning |

The smallest credible formation is often three Staff: an art director who also owns copy, a design
engineer, and an independent critic. Add a dedicated copy/IA specialist when the product is difficult
to explain or the site includes substantial publication content.

## Work stages

Freeze the complete producer acceptance-gate contract before scheduling begins. Do not mutate those
gates around an active Work node; express newly discovered taste concerns as an independent downstream
review or create a bounded continuation from the exact produced commit.

1. **Reference capture:** exact incumbent and rendered product at matched desktop/mobile viewports.
   Gate the artifacts on nonblank pixels, nonzero body text, successful critical resources, and no
   consequential console/page errors. File existence is not rendered evidence.
2. **Baseline synthesis:** product truth, mature reference, measurable incumbent defects, and an
   explicit improvement thesis. Consume the selected capture artifacts in a fresh Work when browser
   output is large; do not mix an unbounded capture transcript and the full narrative synthesis in
   one provider session.
3. **Direction study:** three compact directions; no owner review and no integration branch.
4. **Selection:** accountable lead chooses one direction from rendered evidence.
5. **Production:** complete routes and content using selected direction and approved assets.
6. **Independent comparison:** critic judges candidate versus incumbent and product.
7. **Revision:** producer-owned, limited to consequential findings; repeat comparison.
8. **Admission:** exact accepted commit, one live ReviewTarget and bounded owner decision.

Do not attach rejected producer rationale to later execution. Do retain factual observations,
incumbent captures and accepted reusable assets.

## Preflight

Before direction work starts, prove:

- the intended model and provider credential are actually admitted;
- the actor can access applicable shared, company and project skills;
- the repository contains or receives the product-design reference and incumbent;
- approved component sources are reachable or frozen locally;
- the browser/capture tool can render the incumbent; and
- review Work can create the ordinary supervised loopback target later projected by the owner
  cockpit.

The final ReviewTarget is admissible only when it identifies the exact commit and tree, is backed by
a supervised process, passes a live HTTP probe at its reserved loopback endpoint, and projects to an
owner-openable URL. A URL written into a report is not proof that the outcome can be opened.

Resolve preflight failures before spending creative Attempts.

Treat the model's nominal context size as an upper bound, not the provider or gateway request limit.
Keep browser manifests compact, link large screenshots as artifacts, and split capture from synthesis
before a session accumulates substantial tool output.

The accountable lead is subject to the same limit. After baseline acceptance and again after
direction acceptance, checkpoint the decision state and rotate into a reconstructed, single-purpose
wake for selection/commissioning or admission. Do not keep the entire discovery and browser-history
transcript resident in a long-lived lead session merely because coordination is ongoing.
