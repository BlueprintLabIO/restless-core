# Landing-page design

A landing page is a commercial and visual outcome, not a collection of valid components. The live
browser result is primary evidence. A green build can reject broken work but cannot approve taste.

## Accountable lead: form a team that fits the quality target

Before commissioning code, name how the team covers these responsibilities:

- product and offer truth;
- art direction and typography;
- plain-language copy and information architecture;
- interaction/motion design engineering; and
- independent comparative criticism.

Actors may combine adjacent responsibilities when their role and evidence justify it. Keep the
critic independent. If a material responsibility is uncovered, add or reassign Staff before
production instead of asking one generalist to discover strategy, design, copy, implementation and
taste simultaneously.

Read [the team and stage contract](references/team-and-stage-contract.md) when forming or correcting
the team.

## Establish the quality floor

When an incumbent exists, capture it at desktop and mobile and treat it as the minimum quality floor.
Preserve its successful assets, interaction mechanics, content and product truth unless the outcome
explicitly rejects them. A clean-room direction means fresh composition and judgement; it does not
mean asset amnesia.

Order references explicitly:

1. the real product and its design system define identity and factual truth;
2. the best accepted incumbent defines the minimum public-site quality;
3. mature external sites calibrate narrative depth, confidence and finish; and
4. component libraries provide implementation mechanics, never brand identity.

State what the candidate must improve. If the only requirement is “different,” do not infer that
change itself is success.

## Explore before expanding

For a taste-critical or open-ended direction, commission at least three small, materially different
visual directions before building the full site. Each direction must show a real hero thesis, one
representative product encounter, a compact copy voice sample, desktop/mobile intent, and its single
signature interaction. It may be a live route or a rendered prototype.

The art/creative lead compares the directions against product truth and the incumbent, chooses one,
and records why. Do not average the directions together. Do not spend full-site production time on
an unselected concept.

## Build the selected system

The design engineer and copy owner receive the selected direction, incumbent assets, product facts,
approved component registry and route contract. They should:

- use the repository's native framework and existing design system;
- reuse mature code when it advances the selected direction;
- make the hero a concrete thesis rather than a decorated headline;
- spend visual boldness on one orchestrated signature and keep supporting effects disciplined;
- write short, concrete, value-led public copy from the visitor's side of the screen;
- give every primary route and section a visual or informational reason to exist;
- make articles standalone, readable and generous rather than narrow repository annotations; and
- complete mobile navigation, reduced motion, closing conversion and professional footer states.

Render after each consequential slice. Preserve meaningful time for browser comparison and revision;
do not let orchestration or gate repair consume the entire quality budget.

## Compare, do not merely inspect

Use the native web-review skill for deterministic browser facts, then apply
[the comparative review contract](references/comparative-review.md). The critic receives the live
candidate, incumbent and product reference at the same viewports, but not producer rationale.

The verdict is one of:

- `better` — materially improves the outcome without regressing product truth or complete-site
  quality;
- `changes_requested` — a plausible winning direction with consequential visible defects; or
- `weaker` — does not beat the incumbent and should not replace it.

“Acceptable,” “clean,” “coherent,” or “passes checks” are not sufficient when the outcome is to
improve an incumbent. If no candidate is better, retain the incumbent and say so.

## Owner-facing completion

The accountable lead inspects the exact post-critique site. Escalate one live ReviewTarget only after
the candidate is proven better, every promised primary route is present, the strongest critic issues
are resolved, and the comparison evidence is attached. Publication remains a separate consequence.
