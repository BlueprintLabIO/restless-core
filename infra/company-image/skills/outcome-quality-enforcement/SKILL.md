# Outcome quality enforcement

The accountable lead owns the accepted outcome. Staff own production. A completed Attempt is evidence
to judge, not proof that the outcome is good.

## Form the quality contract

Before expensive production, retrieve rather than guess:

- the real product, operating environment, repository, data, and constraints;
- authoritative product or brand truth;
- the strongest accepted incumbent or gold-standard reference;
- prior failures as compact observations, without inheriting rejected solutions; and
- the specialist skill or evaluator that matches the outcome.

Resolve any ambiguity that changes the solution class, cost, authority, or acceptance bar. Otherwise
make a reversible assumption and record it. Keep one concise durable contract containing:

1. the observable outcome and whose reality must change;
2. priorities and explicit trade-offs;
3. references ordered by authority;
4. the incumbent or minimum quality floor;
5. exclusions and authority boundaries;
6. native evidence required for acceptance;
7. the independent evaluator and verdict language;
8. the owner-attention envelope; and
9. the convergence and blocker conditions.

Give producers the minimum sufficient context: the contract, exact inputs, environment, and relevant
examples. Do not replay the full lead conversation or make them reverse-engineer the outcome from a
checklist.

## Run the evidence loop

Use the smallest Work graph that preserves real accountability:

1. Commission Staff to produce a coherent end-to-end candidate or one high-information slice.
2. Operate the exact candidate in its real medium. Capture native evidence before interpreting it.
3. Commission a fresh-context independent evaluator when judgement matters. Give it the contract,
   ordered references, exact candidate, and factual evidence—but not producer rationale.
4. Require a decisive verdict and ranked consequential gaps. Check that the evaluator actually tested
   the promised outcome; a weak verifier creates false confidence.
5. Commission the smallest root-cause repair with a credible improvement hypothesis, then evaluate the
   new exact candidate again.

Budget evidence context deliberately. Keep large screenshot, trace, log, and capture corpora as durable
linked outputs; give the evaluator compact manifests, representative native views, and commands or
locators for retrieving the rest. When capture and judgement together would saturate one session,
separate evidence production from a fresh synthesis pass rather than carrying an ever-growing
transcript. Verification commands must be non-interactive and must not install packages, rewrite lock
files, or otherwise change the candidate they purport to judge.

Continue while a consequential gap and credible improvement path remain. Attempt limits bound local
execution failure; they do not lower the team outcome. Create sparse revision or replacement Work as
needed. After the same important failure twice, change the approach, context, skill, model, evaluator,
or team seam. Use a clean reset when the trajectory is anchored to rejected assumptions or form.

Prefer one coherent root fix over accumulated patches. Complexity must buy observable quality,
reliability, or future reasoning leverage. Convert repeated failures into a reusable skill, test,
evaluation, tool, or compact durable observation.

## Converge honestly

Accept only when:

- the contract is proven against the exact native artifact;
- the result clears the stated floor and ordered references;
- independent review accepts it when required;
- the verifier and evidence are themselves credible;
- no consequential unresolved gap remains; and
- the owner is asked only for authority or irreducible judgement, with a ready-to-use review target.

Do not stop because a build is green, a producer is confident, an arbitrary loop count elapsed, or the
lead is tired of iterating. Stop when quality converges or when a concrete external constraint makes
further machine progress impossible. State residual uncertainty and disagreement plainly.
