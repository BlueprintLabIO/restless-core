A final reviewer can detect a stale outcome. It cannot prevent one that has already closed. The role
of an accountable lead is most visible in the narrow interval between a material change and an
irreversible consequence.

## What we expected

The obvious way to supervise a queue is to inspect it when workers finish. That can be enough when
the world stays still. It becomes late when a policy, source or safety condition changes while
production is active.

## What happened

In a support experiment, two workers were handling 96 cases when the refund policy changed. In the
causal arm, the event reached the responsible non-producing lead with the affected responsibility.
The lead redirected the live Attempts and the company closed 96 of 96 under the changed policy.

In the terminal-only comparison, the same information waited for the end. Sixteen stale cases
remained. The final validator could name the error, but there was no active decision-maker left to
prevent it. That arm closed 80 of 96 and reached semantic deadlock.

The causal lead also made one real judgement error. Independent evaluation caught a duplicate-case
contamination. Availability did not make the lead correct. It made correction possible before the
wrong outcome became final.

## What changed

Restless keeps the lead separate from planned production. The lead owns the mission, sees material
facts, redirects work and judges the promoted outcome. Ordinary unit completion does not need to wake
it. Harm, contradiction, failed evidence or a changed requirement can.

The implementation implication is small: a factual event should reach the nearest accountable owner
with the affected Work and evidence. A calendar of status messages does not create the same decision
window.

## Where the conclusion stops

The policy change and cases were controlled. This result does not prove that a live provider event
will survive authentication, recovery and owner review. It does show why a supervisor should remain
available, and why final review cannot be treated as a substitute for causal accountability.
