A non-producing lead looks expensive when work proceeds normally. The lead writes none of the final
units. It adds another model call, another handoff and another judgment boundary.

The support experiment tested the part that normal operation hides: a material policy change while
workers were already handling cases.

## The intervention window

Two workers owned 96 support cases. During production, the refund policy changed. In the causal arm,
the event reached the accountable lead with the affected responsibility. The lead redirected two live
Attempts, prevented the stale policy from being accepted and closed all 96 cases under the new rule.

In the terminal-only arm, the change waited for the end. Sixteen cases remained stale. The final
validator could report the problem, but no active decision-maker could prevent it. The result closed
80 of 96 and entered semantic deadlock.

The difference was not another review step. It was the ability to act before the consequence became
final.

## Availability is part of the role

This is why the lead cannot quietly become a producer. A lead absorbed in drafting has the same blind
spot as a worker. The organisation loses the actor that still holds the mission, can see the changed
fact and has authority to redirect the affected work.

The role earns its cost when three conditions meet:

- a material fact can change active work;
- the lead can be addressed with the exact Work and evidence;
- the lead has enough attention left to act inside the consequence window.

Routine success does not need that attention. Ordinary unit completions can accumulate. Harm,
blockage, contradiction, changed policy and exact gate failure should wake the lead immediately.

## A supervisor can still be wrong

The causal lead repaired the stale policy and introduced a separate judgment defect in one case. That
matters. Availability gave the organisation a chance to correct course. It did not guarantee that the
new direction was correct.

Exact population checks and independent semantic review remain necessary. The lead needs the actual
changed fact, the affected responsibility and the candidate outcome. Status summaries and confidence
language cannot replace those inputs.

The experiment therefore supports a narrower claim than “management works.” Causal supervision can
prevent stale completion. Supervisory judgment still needs evidence and review.

## What Restless should preserve

The product does not need a meeting cadence or a workflow engine. It needs a small factual path:

1. record the material event;
2. correlate it with current responsibility;
3. wake the nearest accountable lead;
4. preserve or supersede the exact affected Attempt;
5. inspect the revised native outcome.

That path is enough to let intelligence respond. More status machinery would consume the attention
the role exists to preserve.

## Boundary of the finding

The policy event was controlled and the cases were fictional. The run proves a causal coordination
mechanism in a test world. It does not prove that a real provider event will survive authentication,
projection, restart and native owner review.

That is the next dogfood. One real inbound test event should enter through its provider boundary,
reach the right lead and alter a prepared outcome without polling or owner rescue.
