An architecture that expects to be corrected.

Restless is a clean-slate rebuild and an active research programme. Product intent is explicit. Coordination claims earn permanence through controlled dogfood and real company work.

What endures

The company does machine-doable work. The owner supplies mission, judgement, taste and sign-off. Authority makes the arrangement accountable.

These are product commitments, not an experimental preference. The owner should receive prepared native outcomes. Every executable request should have one accountable lead and attributable production. Capabilities must be probed, never assumed.

What remains provisional

  • Default team shapeOne worker per lead currently wins often enough to be the prior, not a universal rule.
  • Elastic queuesRepeated independent units can repay more workers, but demand shape and tail quality change the answer.
  • Lead supervisionIt remains an invariant in the current architecture even though experiments show that presence alone is not correctness.
  • Completion signalsArtifacts and material events currently beat fixed timers. Generalisation beyond code and research still needs proof.

How findings change the system

We branch competing explanations, run them against the same bounded work, then purge to one current canon. A test can reject a mechanism, expose a harness defect or leave the comparison inconclusive. All three outcomes are useful when they are labelled honestly.

One site benchmark, for example, produced two passing candidates and split blind reviewers. It said something about coordination on an existing codebase. It did not establish independent creative advantage. The correct response was a greenfield run, not a larger claim.

Open questions

  • What signal predicts that one coherent outcome exceeds a strong worker’s effective session?
  • When does a bounded parallel hand become attributable Staff work?
  • Which artifact and event boundaries generalise outside software?
  • Can Exec return to availability before lead orientation completes?
  • When do two accountable leads beat one broader lead?
  • Can inbound real-world signals preserve authority, idempotency and owner attention?

Findings, not victory laps.
