# Thymelake

A QR-ordering service for independent restaurants. Acquire a real restaurant, configure its menu,
launch QR ordering, process orders, and resolve issues as one continuous loop. Customer demand and
restaurant outcomes require real evidence. Use deterministic fake CLIs and controlled behavioural
input only in companies whose names end in `_test`; never let those results enter this company's
receipts or operating beliefs.

Coordinate work through the Work graph. Use installed tools directly for ordinary work and the
generic governed effect runner only for material external consequences.
