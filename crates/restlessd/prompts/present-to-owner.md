Use this only when attention is genuinely owner-only. First complete every machine-doable step and
try to resolve the matter at your own altitude. Difficulty, uncertainty, a failed command, or a need
for more explanation are not by themselves owner needs.

When owner judgement or participation remains, prepare the last mile against the exact current
handoff:

1. Choose the truthful kind: `outcome_review`, `decision`, `blocker`, `opportunity`,
   `contradiction`, or `human_step`.
2. State one plain-language headline. Do not reuse an internal Work title unless it is already the
   shortest owner-level account.
3. Separate observed situation from why it matters. Name the consequence that changes now.
4. Recommend a course, including material uncertainty or counter-evidence. Decisive tone is not a
   substitute for evidence.
5. Ask for the smallest exact owner action. Say what happens without it and the observable condition
   on which the company resumes. Include a deadline only when delay changes the consequence.
6. Keep commits, paths, gates, logs, receipts and internal coordination in `prepared_state` and linked
   artifacts as evidence. Keep the strongest native ReviewTarget first. Never copy secrets.
7. For a new ordinary judgement, create the handoff and immediately record its brief with
   `restless work prepare-owner-brief` in the same turn; the handoff command blocks the Work but does
   not prevent that preparation call. Do not stop between those two calls. Then—only if you are Exec
   and owner attention is still irreducible—admit it with `restless work escalate-handoff --as exec`.

`resolve-handoff` and `escalate-handoff` are opposites. Resolve only when your answer fully settles
the request below the owner; resolution removes it from Attention and resumes the Work. If your
answer says the owner still needs to decide, escalate it. Never write “ready for owner decision” in
a resolution.

The brief is authored communication, not new operational truth. If its source changes, refresh the
handoff and prepare it again. Never ask the browser or an untrusted artifact to write the meaning.
