Use this only when attention is genuinely owner-only. First complete every machine-doable step and
try to resolve the matter at your own altitude. Difficulty, uncertainty, a failed command, or a need
for more explanation are not by themselves owner needs.

When owner judgement or participation appears to remain, prepare the last mile against the exact
current handoff:

1. Choose the truthful kind: `outcome_review`, `decision`, `blocker`, `opportunity`,
   `contradiction`, or `human_step`.
2. Before escalating, name every machine-doable probe, repair or preparation that could materially
   change the choice. Complete it. If any remains, keep the item below the owner and say what the
   company will do next.
3. When owner judgement is irreducible, work backwards from the smallest exact answer that releases
   the company. Headline the course you recommend in language the owner can act on; do not reuse an
   internal Work title, headline a project status or present a menu. If the headline does not make
   the recommended direction clear on its own, rewrite it.
4. State only the observed change that makes the decision necessary now, then the business
   consequence and material trade-off. Recommend one course. Do not hand the judgement back as an
   options list or explain several implementation methods in the primary brief.
5. Ask for the smallest exact owner action, normally approval or adjustment of the recommended
   course. Do not make the owner compare the alternatives again. Say only the source-supported
   consequence of waiting and the observable condition on which the company resumes. Include a
   deadline only when delay changes the consequence.
6. Keep commits, paths, gates, logs, receipts and internal coordination in `prepared_state` and linked
   artifacts as evidence. Keep the strongest native ReviewTarget first. Never copy secrets.
7. Privately audit the draft clause by clause against the source. For each factual clause, identify
   the exact observation that supports it; remove or explicitly label anything that has no source.
   A no-action consequence is not permission to invent urgency, lost momentum or hypothetical harm.
   Preserve uncertainty and counter-evidence that could change the recommendation. An uncertainty is
   an unknown exposed by the source, not a place to invent possible upside, downside or a causal story
   merely to fill the field.
8. Rehearse a ten-second owner read: what changed, why it matters, what the company recommends, what
   exact action is needed, and what happens after acting or waiting. Delete anything that does not
   change one of those answers. Write plain text, not Markdown; the cockpit supplies hierarchy.
9. For a new ordinary judgement, create the handoff and immediately record its brief with
   `restless work prepare-owner-brief` in the same turn; the handoff command blocks the Work but does
   not prevent that preparation call. Do not stop between those two calls. Then—only if you are Exec
   and owner attention is still irreducible—admit it with `restless work escalate-handoff --as exec`.

`resolve-handoff` and `escalate-handoff` are opposites. Resolve only when your answer fully settles
the request below the owner; resolution removes it from Attention and resumes the Work. If your
answer says the owner still needs to decide, escalate it. Never write “ready for owner decision” in
a resolution.

The brief is authored communication, not new operational truth. If its source changes, refresh the
handoff and prepare it again. Never ask the browser or an untrusted artifact to write the meaning.

Assume the reader has no technical context, little time and will not infer a missing connection. Give
each field one job:

- `headline`: the recommended decision or concrete outcome, using words the owner already knows;
- `situation`: only the observed change that made this item appear now;
- `impact`: the direct business consequence, without repeating the situation;
- `recommendation`: one course, beginning with a plain action verb;
- `no-action`: the literal state that remains if the owner waits; and
- `uncertainty`: only an unknown that could change the recommendation.

Use short direct sentences. Name the person, company or object before saying what happened to it.
Expand an acronym on first use. Prefer “send the four emails” to “execute the outreach operation” and
“the website stays unpublished” to “the release remains in a non-terminal state.” Do not make one
field understandable only by reading another, and do not submit the same sentence under two labels.
