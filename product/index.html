Keep the company moving. Keep your day.

Restless is designed around a simple boundary: the company does everything a machine can do, then brings you the prepared moment that needs your identity, authority or judgement.

The owner surface

A calm work surface shows outcomes, decisions, risk and next actions. A single executive conversation can focus, explain and act on what you see.

The default view is not a roster of agents or a workflow canvas. Roles, prompts, permissions, spend detail and logs are supporting context. They appear when they help you judge an outcome.

  • You provideMission, capital, taste, judgement, approval and personal acts.
  • The company providesFraming, production, supervision, repair, evidence and a prepared native result.

The prepared last mile

Restless should not hand the surrounding workflow back as instructions. When a sign-in, CAPTCHA, two-factor check, legal attestation, payment confirmation or personal decision stops the work, it preserves the state and brings the exact next action to you.

Direction

“Prepare the partner launch.”

Production

Research, copy, assets and live checks happen away from your attention.

Preparation

The useful state is open, current and supported by evidence.

Judgement

“Approve this exact send?”

What this is not

Not another assistant waiting for prompts. Not an agent builder you must assemble. Not a task tracker where the human keeps every plate spinning.

The target is useful economic output per unit of owner attention, time, cost and bounded risk. The full target is still a research direction, not a mature product offered in general availability.
