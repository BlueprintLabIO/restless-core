## Decision to test in production

Swift Arrival keeps stable network, physics and mission foundations while exposing fun as a
data-driven search surface. At least 80% of ordinary feel findings should close through tuning,
content or presentation. Core refactoring is allowed only when evidence shows that the available
parameter space cannot make player intention legible, multiplayer state reliable or the central
activity enjoyable.

This campaign tests the decision by building the first complete vertical slice, not by constructing a
generic tuning framework in isolation.

## Native outcome

A fresh pair of players can start at the depot and complete one coherent 15–20 minute workday in a
recognisable Swift Arrival game:

1. choose and load contracts;
2. inhabit one continuous walk-through truck with responsive first-person movement and visible hands;
3. drive a short obstacle route while managing cargo and recoverable mistakes;
4. encounter one robber event and one police inspection;
5. deliver across three materially different destinations;
6. finish with an acknowledged result that reflects cargo state and player actions; and
7. restart or replay without manual developer repair.

The slice contains six data-defined cargo types and at least one incompatible combination. It supports
six network slots, proves two simultaneous rendered players, and mechanically exercises four connected
peers. It has a coherent low-poly visual, audio and interface identity. It is a vertical slice, not a
large open world, campaign, progression tree, dedicated-server product or Steam release.

## What counts as one loop

A loop is one frozen candidate or preset evaluated against one player-visible hypothesis:

`observation -> smallest causal hypothesis -> bounded change -> cheap gates -> native scenario -> decision`

It records candidate identity, preset, seed, hypothesis, native evidence, result, cost and disposition.
One code edit, model turn, screenshot, test retry or agent handoff is not a loop. A failed hypothesis is
a valid loop when its negative evidence changes the next decision. Duplicate runs and infrastructure
retries are friction, not product loops.

The campaign runs at least 30 material loops. It stops before 60 when every acceptance gate is met. If
acceptance arrives before loop 30, the remaining loops test robustness, materially different tuning
presets and ordinary recovery rather than adding speculative features.

## Campaign stages

### Stage A — executable baseline and seams (loops 1–6)

- Prove the EXP-14 positive, rejection and recovery baseline on the copied candidate.
- Split the monolithic prototype only along demonstrated ownership seams: game configuration, scenario
  data, stable authority/simulation and presentation.
- Add one validated tuning schema, named presets, fixed seeds and live local reload.
- Preserve a playable candidate after every accepted loop.

### Stage B — central physical toy (loops 7–18)

- Establish satisfying camera-relative walking and driving, acceleration and blocked movement.
- Make visible hands, pickup, carry, throw, straps, deliberate drop and recovery readable and forgiving.
- Replace placeholder route motion with collision-bearing truck and obstacle behaviour sufficient for
  the slice; test the relevant state under 100–150 ms simulated latency.
- Compare at least three distinct feel presets before choosing one canon.

### Stage C — complete workday grammar (loops 19–36)

- Add depot contract/load start, three destinations and workday result.
- Add six data-defined cargo types, including one incompatible pair, without changing core authority for
  each type.
- Add one robber encounter, one police inspection, calm intervals and fail-forward escalation.
- Make cargo history alter visible acknowledgement and result.

### Stage D — identity, onboarding and robust play (loops 37–50)

- Establish a coherent low-poly palette, lighting, silhouettes, spatial labels, sound and restrained UI.
- Make first-run controls and current intent discoverable without debug prose dominating the game.
- Exercise four peers, restart, reconnect-at-lobby if supported, ordinary drops, early exits, failed
  inspection and partial cargo loss without soft locks.
- Compare at least two pacing presets and retain one canonical workday.

### Stage E — acceptance and reserve (loops 51–60, only as needed)

- Run cheap deterministic and network gates on every final candidate.
- Run sequential fresh source-blind Terra journeys through the exact rendered target.
- Use the reserve only for evidence-backed repairs, regression and tuning. Do not broaden content.

## Acceptance gates

### G1 — foundation

- Godot project parses and starts from one documented command.
- Host authority, join, movement, cargo, truck, delivery and clean shutdown pass focused gates.
- Positive, shortcut-rejection, drop-recovery and seat-re-entry EXP-14 behaviours remain green.

### G2 — tuning architecture

- Player, hand, truck, cargo, route, encounter and recovery settings are data-defined and validated.
- Three materially different feel presets run against one fixed scenario without edits to stable
  networking, authority or mission code.
- Every retained playtest names exact preset and seed.

### G3 — full slice content

- One depot, one obstacle route, three destinations, six cargo types, one incompatible combination,
  one robber encounter and one police inspection are present in the executable workday.
- A normal workday lasts 15–20 minutes at the canonical preset without waiting padding.
- Failure escalates or recovers; it does not silently soft-lock the run.

### G4 — multiplayer and robustness

- Two fresh rendered clients can complete the canonical workday with host-owned results.
- Four peers connect and complete a focused mechanical scenario under simulated 100–150 ms latency.
- A second clean launch can complete without manual state repair.

### G5 — independent experience

- Two sequential source-blind Terra players complete the final candidate from public controls and
  objective only.
- One source-blind negative/recovery player obtains the intended visible response.
- No evaluator pass label outranks the pixels, state trace and accountable lead review.

### G6 — founder review target

- The founder receives a prepared native build, one command, controls, canonical preset/seed and known
  limitations.
- Automated and agent evidence may establish coherence and independent playability. Only founder
  judgement may establish that the slice is fun enough to expand.

## Organisation and loop control

- Exec delegates once to one accountable non-producing Game Product lead and remains available.
- One end-to-end Gameplay Staff worker owns coherent production by default.
- Runtime/tooling or independent art/audio Staff is added only for a stable, independently useful seam.
- Independent players receive no source, producer history, logs, tests or prior player evidence.
- The lead selects the next loop from material game evidence, not a pre-expanded 60-item backlog.
- Each loop changes the smallest surface capable of testing its hypothesis. One conclusive failure stops
  the candidate and yields one failure packet.
- Mechanical checks are frequent and cheap. Terra vision is sparse: stage transitions, semantic defects
  and final acceptance. Founder taste is last.

## Budgets and stop rules

- Aggregate model ceiling: USD 180 for the 30–60-loop campaign.
- No single loop may silently exceed USD 8; the lead narrows or stops first.
- At most 12 decisive frames survive each vision run; raw exploratory captures are deleted after compact
  synthesis.
- Provider refusal produces one shared cooldown. No retry storm, polling loop or timeout cascade.
- Stop immediately for unapproved spend/effects, evidence contamination, unrecoverable source loss,
  three repeated instances of the same infrastructure blocker, or loop 60 without all gates.
- A product failure is not an infrastructure blocker. It becomes the next bounded loop while evidence
  continues to discriminate.

`fun enough` decision is supported by current-candidate evidence. If founder review remains pending,
the honest label is `prepared vertical-slice candidate`, not accepted game.
