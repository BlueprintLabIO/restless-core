# T3 — Primary bottleneck screen

## Observed friction

Ordinary delegation can lose through callback ceremony, cognitive duplication, context transfer or
communication narration. Building all proposed remedies before observing which failure occurred would
recreate the coordination churn under study.

## Outcome

E01/E02 failure codes activate at most two of W07, W04, W01 or W09. Each activated candidate receives
the cheapest falsifying probe and, when mechanically viable, one matched GPT-5.6 live screen.

## Ownership

Experiment mechanism below the accountable lead; no production default changes here.

## Acceptance

- Selection cites observed V3, I3/E5, C1/C4 or M1 evidence.
- Untriggered candidates remain unimplemented and later receive `not-activated`.
- First live loss stops the candidate; a win records its exact complexity and falsifier.
- At most two candidates are screened for the same bottleneck.

## Deletion

Delete losing adapters and duplicate paths immediately after evidence capture. Preserve the smallest
reproducible probe and report.
