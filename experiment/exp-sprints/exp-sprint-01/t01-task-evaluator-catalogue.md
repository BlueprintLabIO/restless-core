# T1 — Task and evaluator catalogue

## Observed friction

Earlier runs repeatedly tuned one game feature, misclassified nominal size as lead saturation and let
evaluator proxies manufacture or miss outcomes. The wider churn question needs frozen tasks selected by
work shape and native proof, not feature variety alone.

## Outcome

E01 and E02 have immutable scenario bytes, seed locators, workload classifications, native review
targets, external evaluators and negative controls. Reserve game/non-code tasks are catalogued only far
enough to activate when a later evidence gate requires their shape.

## Ownership

Experiment evidence. Candidate game/source fixtures stay isolated from Restless and Cosmon production
history.

## Acceptance

- Two fresh pre-run classifications record saturation, separability, coupling, volatility,
  uncertainty and tool density for each active task.
- Scenario and evaluator hashes are frozen before arm order is allocated.
- Each negative control fails for the intended absent behaviour without mutating the candidate.
- Blind review sees the native artifact before actor narration.
- Task wording cannot change an arm's structural role without invalidating the matched set.

## Deletion

Discard candidate tasks or evaluators whose native signal is ambiguous. Do not accumulate a universal
scenario catalogue.
