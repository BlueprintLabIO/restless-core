# Experiment Sprint 09 - Continuous mission frontier

**Status:** Complete; current primitives suffice after thin continuity and Runtime repairs

**Decision owner:** Founder

**Date:** 26 August 2026

**Original dependency:** Sprint 20's terminal research-publication report. The founder explicitly
activated EXP-09 first on 26 August 2026. The first cell is therefore narrowed to an isolated
editorial-continuity fixture derived only from completed EXP-05 evidence. It tests continuous
architecture, not the finished public publication. Sprint 20 remains required before Dogfood 5.

If EXP-08 later promotes a web-production playbook, both later EXP-09 arms receive the same frozen
version; playbook presence is not the treatment.

## Decision this sprint must produce

Determine whether Restless can sustain useful work across multiple real or controlled signals with
materially less owner prompting than repeated bounded requests, while remaining correctly idle when
nothing worth doing has changed.

The experiment must identify whether the current substrate - Goals, accountable responsibility,
Work/Attempt, messages, schedules, inbound events, ordinary files/Git and process callbacks - is
already sufficient. A new `Mission` entity, workflow engine, polling daemon or autonomous-improvement
state machine is not assumed.

## Central hypothesis

> One standing mandate held by an accountable non-producing lead can turn material signals into a
> sequence of bounded, evaluated Staff outcomes without per-cycle owner prompting, duplicate work or
> unbounded polishing, and can decline action when expected value is absent.

The credible failure is not merely that one cycle is weak. It is that continuity amplifies stale
beliefs, keeps actors busy without new evidence, duplicates effects, degrades artifact quality,
occupies Exec, or silently converts schedules into semantic completion.

## Working definition

A **continuous mission** is a working convention, not a new product entity:

```text
standing mandate and measurable objective
  -> material signal or scheduled opportunity check
  -> accountable lead judges act / investigate / no-op
  -> bounded Staff Work and Attempt if useful
  -> native outcome and independent acceptance evidence
  -> retain, publish, prepare, revert or discard
  -> lesson updates the next cycle
  -> responsibility returns to idle
```

The mission is continuous because responsibility and objective persist. Individual Work and Attempts
remain bounded and close semantically. An actor process need not remain sampling, and a quiet company
is correct when there is no useful work.

## Matched treatment

Use the current Restless product path, GPT-5.6 Sol, matched tools, effort, budgets, authority and
signals.

```text
Arm R - repeated request baseline
  the owner receives each material signal and issues the frozen next-cycle directive

Arm M - standing mission
  the owner supplies the mandate once
  signals reach the accountable responsibility directly
  the lead decides whether to commission bounded Staff work
```

Both arms preserve continuously available Exec, a non-producing lead, Staff production and the same
independent outcome review. Arm R's owner directives are frozen relays, not bespoke coaching. Their
active time is counted because the eliminated owner relay is the product claim.

## Workload A - editorial continuity

Feed both arms the same versioned sequence derived from the research-publication domain:

- a valid new experimental result that warrants a new article;
- a correction that requires updating an existing claim;
- a duplicate notification;
- an apparently relevant event that adds no decision-changing evidence;
- conflicting evidence that should prevent a confident update;
- one material source change affecting a comparison page; and
- a quiet interval with no signal.

The authoritative fixture remains isolated and marked `_test`; it validates causal behaviour, not
public readership. Each event must close as an accepted article/update, a bounded investigation, an
explicit defer/reject decision or a justified no-op. A universal summary is not the product.

## Workload B - economic opportunity continuity

After Workload A passes the causal and idle gates, run one materially different queue of controlled
opportunity signals: relevant and irrelevant inbound enquiries, public-market changes, duplicate
notices, stale information and one high-value exception. Each useful unit closes locally as a
qualified opportunity state, evidence-backed next action and prepared last mile. Nothing is sent,
purchased or represented as real demand.

This second workload tests economic preparation and repeated independent units, not market validity.
A later dogfood must use real external signals and governed effects before claiming economic impact.

## Validity and harness gates

1. **Exact standing mandate.** Objective, quality floor, source boundary, authority, budget, expiry
   condition and escalation boundary are frozen before the first signal.
2. **Signal truth.** Every event has one source identity, observation time, dedupe key and target
   responsibility. Controlled inputs remain visibly `_test` at every projection.
3. **Causal wake.** A material event wakes the nearest accountable lead or records a durable pending
   fact. Routine unit completion does not wake Exec or produce status theatre.
4. **Bounded cycles.** Every commissioned cycle creates ordinary Work/Attempt responsibility and
   reaches a semantic terminal state. Fixed time is an observation envelope, never completion logic.
5. **Idle correctness.** During the quiet interval Arm M creates no production Work, no cosmetic
   rewrite, no heartbeat artifact and no model call whose only purpose is to appear active.
6. **Review path.** The exact native outcome reaches independent review without the known
   out-of-workspace ReviewTarget failure, callback loss or post-probe dirty-worktree defect. A repeated
   known failure makes the arm product-invalid rather than a topology loss.
7. **Effect safety.** No live publication, send, purchase or external representation occurs in the
   experiment.

## Measures

### Useful output

- accepted outcomes and justified no-ops per signal;
- missed material signals, false-positive work and duplicate outcomes;
- claim/source correctness and worst-cycle quality;
- stale work accepted after a correction;
- time from signal observation to first useful result and accepted closure; and
- whether the prior cycle's lesson improves or harms the next.

### Autonomy and economics

- active owner minutes and interventions by type;
- Exec unavailable time and unnecessary Exec wakes;
- lead/Staff active time, model cost and cost per accepted outcome;
- idle-period cost;
- review and repair loops;
- externally useful opportunity states prepared; and
- work discarded because no marginal value existed.

### Continuity quality

- responsibility and source context retained without replaying whole history;
- contradiction and correction handling;
- duplicate/effect prevention;
- bounded memory growth and stale-context contamination;
- process restart/reconstruction truth; and
- mission expiry or stop behaviour.

## Decision rule

Arm M advances only if it:

1. materially reduces owner relay attention;
2. matches or improves accepted output quality and material-signal recall;
3. creates zero duplicate consequential outcomes;
4. performs no production during the quiet interval;
5. does not increase harmful false-positive work or stale acceptance;
6. keeps Exec available; and
7. applies at least one prior-cycle learning appropriately without mechanical repetition.

If the current primitives pass, the conclusion is to keep them and document the continuous-mission
operating pattern. If one thin file/playbook or wake affordance repairs a repeated causal gap, propose
only that slice. A first-class entity or durable engine is considered only after repeated runs show
the same state cannot be recovered from current responsibility, events and files.

## Failure injections

- duplicate delivery of the same event;
- one event arriving while related Staff work is active;
- a correction that invalidates an in-flight artifact;
- one actor process replacement after a useful checkpoint;
- one source contradiction requiring defer rather than synthesis;
- one irrelevant high-salience event; and
- the quiet interval.

Failure injection stops affected work only where necessary. It does not manufacture a company-wide
incident or broaden the authority envelope.

## Deliverables

1. frozen standing mandate, signal fixtures and repeated-request baseline;
2. per-cycle Work/Attempt/artifact and causal-event evidence;
3. native editorial and opportunity outcomes with independent review;
4. owner-attention, idle-cost, latency, quality and duplicate-work comparison;
5. a friction list separated into business, model/provider, runtime and OrgIntel causes;
6. a disposition: `current primitives suffice`, `thin affordance`, `new concept earned`, `reject` or
   `inconclusive`; and
7. the exact Dogfood 5 activation conditions.

## Risks and dispositions

| Risk | Disposition | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Continuous work becomes continuous churn | **Guarded** | Quiet interval, marginal-value judgement, bounded Work and idle-cost accounting |
| Baseline is unfair because owner relays are deliberately expensive | **Accepted** | Eliminating that relay is the product claim; relay content is frozen and no coaching is allowed |
| Controlled opportunities are mistaken for real demand | **Invariant** | `_test` source identity at input, projection, artifact and report; no live effect |
| Continuity is actually transcript accumulation | **Guarded** | Factual source/work context and bounded actor sessions; measure context growth and stale contamination |
| Schedules become polling or timeout semantics | **Guarded** | Schedules may create an opportunity check; events and artifacts decide meaning and completion |
| A new mission engine is designed before evidence | **Guarded** | Current primitives are the required first treatment; new machinery needs repeated causal failure |

## Stop boundary

This sprint authorises only isolated continuous-mission comparisons and their evidence. It does not
authorise a live watcher, autonomous public publishing, standing external-effects grant, production
mission entity, workflow engine, general event taxonomy or permanent background actor.

## Result

The direct standing-lead editorial arm matched the relay arm's useful behavior while using 48.8% fewer
model attempts, 63.8% fewer summed reported usage tokens, 58.6% less estimated list cost and one Exec
wake rather than nine. The opportunity workload then handled qualified, irrelevant, duplicate,
market-update, stale and high-value-exception signals with five bounded Work nodes, zero external
effects and exact idle during the 45-second quiet interval.

Invalid runs exposed exact Git lineage, revision continuity, workspace-scoped ACP reuse, safe branch
promotion and terminal supervisor delivery defects. Each was repaired around the current Work,
Attempt, Message, artifact and Runtime substrate. A clean targeted replay proved exact producer to
reviewer lineage, clean final promotion and event-driven lead wakes without Staff status mail.

Disposition: **current primitives suffice after thin affordances**. Do not add a `Mission` entity,
poller, heartbeat, workflow engine or autonomous-improvement state machine. See
[`EXP-09/RESULTS.md`](../coordination/experiments/EXP-09/RESULTS.md).
