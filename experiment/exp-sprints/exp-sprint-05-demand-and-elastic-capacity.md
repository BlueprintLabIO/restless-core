# Experiment Sprint 05 — Demand and elastic capacity

**Status:** Draft for founder approval. Do not execute yet.

**Decision owner:** Founder.

**Date:** 25 August 2026

**Primary model envelope:** GPT-5.6 only. `gpt-5.6-sol` is Exec, every accountable lead and the
blinded semantic evaluator; `gpt-5.6-terra` is Staff. The exact first-party runtime selectors and
sustained concurrency must be live-probed before the first counted call. No other model or silent
fallback is authorised.

**Depends on:** EXP-04's local-closure and sparse-fan-in result; Sprint 17's product actor/session,
causal event, supervised Work and native review path. The real Resend callback is not required:
external inputs in this sprint are controlled `_test` events and must never be described as
provider-authenticated.

---

## 1. Frozen organisational decisions

This sprint measures the operating envelope of the current organisation; it does not reopen its
accountability boundaries.

```text
owner
  → continuously available Exec
      → one accountable non-producing lead per coherent outcome
          → one end-to-end Staff worker by default
          → temporary additional Staff only for differentiated seams
             or independently closing queue units
```

- Exec always delegates executable owner work to a lead and returns to portfolio availability.
- A lead frames, commissions, observes, guides, redirects, repairs through Staff and judges. It does
  no planned production or silent artifact rewrite.
- Every executable outcome has at least one Staff producer.
- Independently useful units remain independent. There is no model assembler or mandatory batch
  summary.
- A cognitive fan-in requires one named decision, conflict or integrated artifact. It is Staff work,
  never lead production.
- Work records sparse accountability, Attempts and real dependencies; it does not mirror every unit,
  thought, message or plan step.
- Material events and artifacts wake actors. Elapsed time is a safety/resource envelope, not semantic
  completion.
- Raw chain-of-thought is neither requested nor stored. Effort, phase, tools, messages, artifacts,
  usage, uncertainty and outcome are sufficient coordination evidence.

The diagnostic arms may hold Staff count fixed to measure a capacity curve. Production guidance must
remain lead judgement over observed demand, not a hard-coded task-size router.

## 2. Decision this sprint must produce

EXP-04 proved that local closure works but did not find a parallel crossover at 48 sales accounts or
12 monitored entities. One strong worker cleared those queues too quickly; Q4 was also
infrastructure-invalid. EXP-05 asks the missing real-world questions:

1. At what backlog, arrival rate or response-time value does Q2 or Q4 create more accepted economic
   value than Q1 after worker startup and lead review?
2. When does a preserved non-producing lead materially improve outcomes during policy change,
   blockage or harmful-tail exceptions?
3. How many locally closing workers can one lead supervise before sampling, exceptions or provider
   capacity becomes the bottleneck?
4. Can several independent departments operate concurrently while Exec accepts and dispatches a new
   owner request without becoming a status relay?
5. Does the current minimal substrate—Actor, Work, Attempt, messages, artifacts, schedules and
   process callbacks—already suffice? If not, which repeated observed failure earns one new
   affordance?

The deliverable is a demand/team-size guide, supervisor-span guide, event-response result and
company-level concurrency result. It is not a department catalogue, universal queue engine or fixed
staffing policy.

## 3. Why demand is the missing variable

Nominal task size is a weak predictor. The capacity question is whether valuable work is waiting
while a competent worker is already occupied.

For a locally closing queue, another worker can help only when all of these are true:

1. units are independently ownable and accepted without cognitive recomposition;
2. backlog or arrivals sustain useful overlap after orientation;
3. earlier completion has real response-time, coverage or opportunity value;
4. the provider and tools sustain the added concurrency;
5. lead sampling and exception handling do not consume the saved time; and
6. tail quality, duplication and cross-unit contamination remain controlled.

EXP-05 therefore varies **demand pressure**, then worker count. It does not infer a team from word
count, estimated minutes or a label such as “sales.”

## 4. Counted path and harness boundary

Counted organisational runs use the current Restless product path: OrgIntel responsibility and Work,
the production scheduler, scoped resumable ACP sessions, Runtime capabilities, real model calls,
causal messages, terminal truth and native review. The experiment layer may only:

- create frozen fictional `_test` fixtures and deterministic arrival schedules;
- submit controlled source events with explicit verification metadata;
- calculate exact domain projections and metrics;
- prepare blinded native evaluation packets; and
- preserve bounded run evidence.

It may not implement a second scheduler, actor/session lifecycle, team system, callback protocol,
timeout controller or source of Work truth. A missing product fact is reported as a product gap. A
minimal fix may be proposed, but no new product primitive is built during this sprint without founder
approval after the repeated failure is shown.

## 5. Wave 0 — Validity and capacity gates

No organisational comparison counts until these pass.

### G1 — GPT-5.6 product-path capability

One Sol lead and one Terra worker each resume the same responsibility-scoped session across two fresh
process launches, use the exact coordination and file tools, return a semantic terminal report and
expose per-turn usage without crossing actor or Work boundaries. Missing usage fields remain unknown.

### G2 — Sustained Q4 admission

Four Terra workers concurrently complete four exact, disjoint tool-backed units through the same
gateway and Runtime envelope. All four produce terminal reports and attributable usage. Provider
queueing, missing tools or one absent callback makes Q4 infrastructure-invalid and stops Q4 counted
arms; it does not become a topology loss or trigger route roulette.

### G3 — Event and cancellation truth

A controlled material policy event arrives after observable production begins. It reaches the exact
responsible lead, cancels or redirects only stale Attempts, preserves their workspaces and prevents
post-change artifacts from being accepted. Routine completions remain coalesced.

### G4 — Local closure and evaluation

Randomised completion order yields a byte-identical deterministic unit index with zero missing,
duplicate or cross-unit ownership. Exact population checks inspect every unit. A fresh Sol evaluator
sees native artifacts and authoritative sources but not topology, traces, spend or producer identity.

## 6. Sparse experiment sequence

The search space remains too large for a factorial. Run these gates in order and stop losing branches.

### Wave 1 — Demand-backed sales/account queue

Use 240 frozen fictional account dossiers in an isolated `_test` company. Each account owner returns
one locally complete state: evidence-backed qualification, personalised next action, disposition,
follow-up trigger, uncertainty and exact claims checks. Nothing is sent.

Run the same total population under three predeclared arrival schedules:

- **D0 — available batch:** all units are available at start; establishes the GPT-5.6 Q1/Q2 anchor.
- **D1 — sustained arrivals:** arrivals are paced to keep Q1 meaningfully occupied without creating an
  impossible burst.
- **D2 — service pressure:** the same arrivals carry heterogeneous response-value curves, including a
  small planted high-consequence tail.

For D0, compare Q1 and Q2. For D1/D2, compare Q1, Q2 and one **elastic** arm in which the lead starts
with one worker and may add one only after observing backlog age, service risk and available capacity.
The lead gives a free-form factual rationale; no threshold chooses for it.

Advance to Q4 only if Q2:

1. improves accepted value-adjusted units per request hour by at least 25%;
2. improves p90 response value or backlog age materially;
3. keeps worst-decile quality within 0.5/10 with no high-consequence breach;
4. keeps cost per accepted/value-adjusted unit within 25%, unless the predeclared latency value repays
   the premium;
5. creates no duplicate, missed or cognitively reassembled units; and
6. leaves lead sampling and exception latency inside the frozen span bound.

Replicate the first crossover once with randomised arm order before Q4. Stop at the first clear
marginal loss.

### Wave 2 — Customer operations under change

This is the primary non-coding, event-responsive supervision test. Use 96 frozen fictional support and
success cases arriving over time, with shared policy, eight planted exception classes and asymmetric
harm. Every Staff owner closes a safe response/resolution package and observable next state. Nothing
is sent or applied.

Compare under the same Sol lead and the smallest worker count justified by Wave 1:

- **S-T — terminal supervision:** lead wakes for terminal, genuine question or blocker only;
- **S-E — causal supervision:** one frozen material policy change and one worker-local obstacle reach
  the lead during production; it may guide, cancel, redirect or reassign through Staff.

Both are real supervision; S-T is not a producer or absent-lead arm. The comparison isolates the value
of remaining available and event-addressable during change.

Primary outcomes are stale post-change work, exception recall, harmful-tail defects, accepted cases
per hour, time to safe redirect, lead interventions that changed an outcome, needless interventions,
repair loops and cost per accepted case.

### Wave 3 — Search-breadth monitoring without fan-in

Use a dated frozen corpus of at least 240 documents across 40 fictional companies or market entities.
Workers own disjoint entities and return locally complete alerts with exact sources, severity,
uncertainty and follow-up trigger. The product is an alert feed/index, not a memo.

Compare Q1 and Q2; activate Q4 only through the Wave-1 marginal gate and G2. Measure time to first
useful alert, entity coverage, recall, precision, contradiction handling, cost per accepted alert and
worst-entity quality. This tests whether search breadth creates a crossover that the 12-entity EXP-04
fixture could not expose.

No cognitive fan-in runs in this wave. A decision memo may be proposed for a later experiment only if
several exact alerts demonstrably need one named decision.

### Wave 4 — Concurrent company with available Exec

This wave tests inter-lead organisation, not intra-lead team size, and therefore runs even if Q2 never
wins. Start three independent outcomes concurrently, each with one Sol lead and one Terra worker:

```text
Exec
├── revenue lead → account owner
├── customer lead → case owner
└── intelligence lead → monitoring owner
```

After all three Staff Attempts have begun, submit a fourth bounded owner request from a materially
different function. Exec must appoint a fourth lead and return to availability. Existing leads share
only exact material facts or dependencies; ordinary status and local outputs do not fan into Exec.

Compare each department with its isolated Q1 baseline. Measure dispatch latency for the fourth
request, Exec active and unavailable time, cross-department leakage, provider/runtime contention,
accepted throughput, lead-to-lead material messages and any invented status/summary ritual.

## 7. Team-size and task-size guide this sprint will publish

The guide must be expressed as an observed decision surface, not a universal formula:

```text
one coherent outcome
  → one lead + one end-to-end worker by default

additional Staff
  → only if a differentiated semantic seam changes downstream choices
  → or if locally closing valuable units are waiting while current Staff is occupied

add workers to a queue
  → only while marginal accepted value / response time repays startup, provider and lead review
  → stop at provider failure, tail degradation, lead saturation or first replicated marginal loss

add another lead
  → when a separate outcome has its own closed accountability and native acceptance judgement
  → not because one queue contains many units
```

For every tested shape, publish the observed Q1/Q2/Q4 range, demand level, useful overlap, exception
rate, lead active fraction, provider wait, cost/value crossover and confidence. “Large task” is not an
allowed explanatory variable unless translated into those observed properties.

## 8. Effort, focus and cognitive budget

- The matched core freezes Sol leads and Terra workers at one exact, live-supported effort setting.
- Every actor receives a role envelope, consequence, acceptance target and overall spend ceiling.
- Observable phases remain `orient | produce | verify | handoff | repair`; phases may inform spend and
  lead attention but do not expose private reasoning.
- Preserve dynamic closure headroom for worker verification, lead native review and one repair. EXP-04
  found no universal reserve percentage, so this is an admission judgement, not a fixed 25% bucket.
- Record configured effort separately from observed usage, latency, tool cadence and provider
  metadata. Never infer “more reasoning” from token count or prose.
- No effort-routing comparison runs until a demand/topology crossover is replicated. If activated,
  it stays within GPT-5.6 and varies only one role/effort dimension on the winning fixture.

## 9. Wildcards: preserved, trigger-only

The earlier search space remains useful as a library of candidate remedies, not a to-do list. Activate
at most one wildcard, only after a repeated counted bottleneck names the missing information flow.

| Observed repeated failure | Candidate screen | Why it may help | Why it stays off otherwise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exact artifact access still leaves two Staff repeatedly rebuilding the same causal setup | Shared-history sibling fork | Gives siblings a common bounded conversational prefix before independent ownership | Risks correlated assumptions and hidden authority/context leakage |
| Material changes repeatedly force expensive full reorientation | Causal context delta | Delivers only the changed fact, affected responsibility and acknowledgement boundary | A generic blackboard would duplicate Work and conversation truth |
| Coupled work is tool-parallel but not semantically divisible | One mind, bounded hands | Lets one Staff retain judgement while ordinary tools overlap | Any hand exercising project judgement is Staff and must be attributable |
| Genuine cross-unit dependency repeatedly arrives late | Direct addressed peer message | Lets the owning Staff exchange one decision-changing fact without lead relay | Routine peer chat creates mutual-state maintenance and narration |
| Independent evidence coverage, not throughput, is the measured deficit | Blind sibling diversification | Buys genuinely independent search or alternatives | Same-evidence duplicate production previously lost |

Activation requires: two failures of the same causal kind, a matched no-wildcard control, no new
durable source of truth, and one predeclared deletion criterion. A wildcard that does not win is
purged or returned to `deferred`; it is never kept as optional machinery.

## 10. Measurements

### Economic outcome

- accepted and value-adjusted units per request hour and active worker hour;
- time to first useful unit, p50/p90/p99 unit latency, backlog age and missed service value;
- cost per accepted/value-adjusted unit and total closure cost;
- exact population quality, worst decile/entity, exception recall and high-consequence breaches;
- duplicates, omissions, contamination, stale work and unnecessary cognitive fan-in.

### Organisation

- lead active fraction, wake causes, sample size, exception/question response latency and interventions
  that changed an outcome;
- worker orientation, productive overlap, verification, handoff and repair time;
- Exec dispatch latency, active/unavailable time and portfolio interruptions;
- direct lead-to-lead material exchanges versus status traffic;
- team expansion/contraction decisions and the lead's contemporaneous factual rationale.

### Runtime and truth

- exact actor/session/process identities, hot resumptions and explicit reconstructions;
- provider admission/queueing, tool readiness, callback completeness and cancellation latency;
- configured effort and observed input/cache/output/tool/cost fields, with missing values unknown;
- owner-visible native ReviewTarget, linked source verification state and exact decision requested;
- operator pauses and safety envelopes separated from actor and provider time.

## 11. Sparse gates and invalid results

- Randomise arm order and fixture assignment. Freeze prompts, inputs, tools, model selectors, effort,
  evaluation, sampling and value curves before outputs exist.
- One provisional crossover is replicated once. One clear marginal loss stops the next doubling.
- A provider/tool/callback failure before useful cognition is infrastructure-invalid, not a team loss.
  Retry once only after the exact fault is repaired; a second identical failure stops that branch.
- A quality invariant or harmful-tail breach stops the larger team even if average throughput wins.
- Lead saturation is observed when the frozen sample is missed, exception latency grows beyond the
  fixture's consequence window, a systemic error survives review or lead active time erases worker
  overlap.
- No branch advances by tuning the fixture, threshold or acceptance test after seeing outputs.
- No run uses live-company state, fabricates provider authentication or performs an external effect.

## 13. Stop and authority boundaries

Stop for founder direction before:

- changing the non-producing lead or always-delegating Exec invariants;
- building a universal queue/event/signal primitive, deterministic team router, semantic blackboard,
  workflow engine or second actor/session lifecycle;
- installing a new provider bridge, changing model family or silently falling back from GPT-5.6;
- using a real inbox, customer, prospect, account or live company;
- sending, publishing, paying, applying an account mutation or making a professional opinion; or
- exceeding the approved spend ceiling.

Completion is callback/process/artifact based. A hard wall envelope may terminate runaway spend, but a
timeout never decides that Work succeeded, failed or finished.
