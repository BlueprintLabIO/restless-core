# Experiment Sprint 02 — Natural-team affordance frontier

**Status:** Completed 23 August 2026. Experiment execution is closed; production promotion remains
separately governed.

**Decision owner:** Founder.

**Date:** 23 August 2026

**Depends on:** EXP-01 evidence checkpointed and any active mutable runs drained or explicitly
superseded. EXP-01 remains immutable evidence; this sprint narrows the next search rather than
rewriting it.

---

## 1. Outcome

Select the smallest set of tools and implementation shapes that lets a strong accountable lead hire,
communicate with and integrate Staff naturally, efficiently and truthfully.

The architecture is held fixed:

```text
Owner → continuously available Exec → one accountable lead → 0..N Staff
```

Exec always dispatches. The lead always owns the complete outcome and chooses the smallest effective
team. Work/Attempt records prove cross-actor responsibility but never become the lead's plan or a
scripted conversation. The sprint therefore asks **how to make that architecture ergonomic**, not
whether to replace it with a router, workflow engine or mechanical team ritual.

The deliverables are:

1. a production recommendation for zero or more lead affordances;
2. a scoped advisory guide for when a lead should remain solo, add a producer or add an independent
   critic;
3. evidence about the task/team-size crossover on coding and non-coding work;
4. final dispositions for the still-useful EXP-01 wildcards; and
5. deletion of losing mechanisms and harness paths.

## 2. Evidence carried forward

These are premises to challenge with counterevidence, not results to rerun indiscriminately:

- a strong solo lead won or tied on small/high-coupling and broad-but-below-saturation game work;
- ordinary team structure did not repay its briefing and integration cost in those cells;
- natural one-Staff leadership produced a blind-quality win on one G-WORLD screen while remaining
  slower;
- shared-history session mitosis showed a promising reduction in briefing/reconstruction cost but
  needs clean replication before product promotion;
- callback absence can leave useful Staff artifacts stranded even when the cognitive work succeeded;
- messages and Work records can expose false claims of delegation, but more structure does not by
  itself create better collaboration;
- team value should come from useful difference—specialisation, independent evidence, parallel
  breadth or criticism—not copies of the lead with less context.

EXP-01 reports, cards and registry rows remain the source evidence. EXP-02 adds linked replications or
new cards; it does not edit old outcomes.

## 3. Fixed decisions

- `gpt-5.6-sol` is the accountable lead and `gpt-5.6-terra` is Staff/critic unless a provider-invalid
  control specifically says otherwise.
- Model and tool envelopes are identical inside a matched comparison except for the one mechanism
  under test.
- Exec dispatch and availability are not experimental variables.
- The lead's natural accountable behaviour is the N1 baseline, not a rigid brief template.
- Zero Staff is a successful team decision when the work does not justify delegation.
- The lead owns canonical integration and native outcome review in every arm.
- Work proves responsibility; real Attempt/artifact/terminal evidence proves contribution.
- Addressed event delivery and process/artifact preservation are fixed product requirements; the
  experiment changes only the context carrier or lead-facing presentation of those facts.
- Elapsed time is an operating envelope, never semantic success or failure.
- No live company, consequential effect or production promotion is authorised.
- Sparse sequential experiments replace a full combinatorial matrix.

## 4. Questions this sprint must answer

1. Which minimal affordance reduces coordination cost without reducing outcome quality or making
   collaboration mechanical?
2. Does shared history help because it preserves causal understanding, or merely because it injects
   more tokens?
3. Does a bounded material-event capsule outperform an ordinary addressed message plus normal context
   assembly when a question, artifact or interface changes?
4. Can a compact recovery capsule reduce reconstruction after a missing callback without treating
   artifact existence as success?
5. At what work shapes does one Staff producer or one independent critic beat the lead alone?
6. Does useful team size depend on lead saturation, separability and differentiated value rather than
   crude task size?
7. Which wildcard ideas still address an observed bottleneck, and which should be purged or deferred?

## 5. Candidate affordances

Each candidate must solve a measured bottleneck. Only one candidate changes in a matched pair.

| ID | Candidate | Minimal implementation shape | Hypothesis | Primary risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Commission context capsule | Free-form commission plus the smallest automatically bound actor, workspace, Work/Attempt, upstream-artifact and capability facts | Removes factual retyping and omission while preserving lead judgement | A hidden mandatory template or oversized payload grows around it |
| A2 | Shared-history fork | Staff begins from a bounded fork of the lead's task history at the commission point | Preserves causal understanding and reduces rediscovery on tightly related work | Excess context, authority leakage or correlated thinking |
| A3 | Material event capsule | An addressed question, blocker, interface change or artifact event adds one bounded factual delta to the recipient's normal wake | Reduces replay and noisy coordination beyond ordinary linked messages | Meaning classification becomes brittle or the capsule becomes a second protocol |
| A4 | Recovery capsule | Existing process/artifact observations are synthesised into one bounded `unknown` review brief for the lead | Reduces reconstruction and needless reruns after missing semantic completion | Artifact existence is mistaken for outcome success |
| A5 | Bounded micro-audition | For an unfamiliar actor or uncertain seam, lead commissions a small diagnostic artifact before full ownership | Buys evidence about fit when role uncertainty is material | Selection overhead exceeds the task's value |

A1–A4 are primary candidates. A5 activates only when an experiment contains genuine actor/seam
uncertainty. No arm combines candidates until each component has passed alone and the combination is
needed to resolve an observed residual bottleneck.

## 6. Work portfolio

The portfolio is deliberately small. Each task freezes an immutable brief, seed, native target,
evaluator and negative control before execution.

| Cell | Work shape | Candidate real-world problem | Routing signal it tests |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Tightly coupled, below lead saturation | Repair a gameplay interaction spanning battle state and immediate feedback | Correct zero-Staff choice; team ceremony should lose |
| P1 | Broad, stable independent seam | Build two independently useful player tools over one frozen data interface | Producer parallelism and integration cost |
| P2 | Interface volatility during execution | Add a world feature whose shared interface changes once after commission | Material question/change delivery and shared causal context |
| P3 | Evidence breadth, non-coding | Produce a decision memo from a frozen corpus split into independent evidence regions | Parallel research breadth and synthesis quality |
| P4 | Independent judgement | Blindly review a native game/document candidate containing planted and emergent defects | Critic information gain without production coupling |
| P5 | Recovery | Produce a declared artifact, then terminate the Staff process before semantic completion | Artifact-native recovery without guessed success or duplicate work |

P0 is a routing sentinel, not a place to force Staff. P1/P3 are candidate crossover cells. P2 tests
communication. P4 tests critics. P5 tests implementation resilience rather than creative quality.

## 7. Organisations and comparisons

| Arm | Organisation | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B0 | Sol lead alone | Strong-singleton baseline |
| N1 | Sol lead with natural authority to choose 0..N Terra Staff | Settled architecture baseline |
| N1+A* | N1 plus exactly one candidate affordance | Component screen |
| C1 | Sol lead plus one fresh artifact-only Terra critic | Independent-judgement baseline |

N1 is judged on the team choice it actually makes. If it chooses zero Staff on P0, that is the
expected valid behaviour, not a failed team run. For a producer crossover comparison on P1/P3, the
task contract explicitly offers stable responsibilities but still leaves the lead free to decline.

Larger teams activate only after lead + 1 produces a quality-adjusted win on at least two distinct
work shapes:

```text
lead alone → lead + 1 → lead + 2 → lead + 4 only after positive marginal evidence
```

Scope and evaluator remain fixed. Additional Staff receive genuinely independent responsibilities,
not newly invented requirements.

## 8. Sparse execution plan

### Wave 0 — Freeze and audit

- checkpoint all relevant EXP-01 evidence;
- freeze P0–P5 cards, seeds, evaluators and negative controls;
- verify model identity, cache accounting, event capture and clean isolated workspaces;
- record two independent pre-run classifications of saturation, separability, coupling, interface
  volatility and differentiated value.

### Wave 1 — Establish the natural baseline

- run B0 versus N1 on P0 and P1;
- run B0 versus N1 on P3 only if P1 does not settle the producer crossover;
- run B0 versus C1 on P4;
- stop if N1 behaviour cannot be truthfully attributed to real Work/Attempt/artifact evidence.

### Wave 2 — Screen the primary bottleneck

Select the highest measured churn source from Wave 1:

- briefing/context reconstruction → A1, then A2 only if residual causal-context loss remains;
- repeated context assembly around consequential messages → A3;
- manual reconstruction or needless rerun after missing semantic completion → A4.

Run one conformance probe, then one matched live pair. A first clear loss stops and purges that branch.
At most two candidates may be screened before an intermediate decision.

### Wave 3 — Replicate one provisional winner

Replicate a provisional candidate across:

- one coding/product task;
- one non-coding task; and
- one different coupling or interface-volatility level.

A candidate may be production-recommended only if the mechanism works, outcome quality is
non-inferior, its targeted churn improves and the new machinery remains smaller than the avoided
ceremony.

### Wave 4 — Team-size frontier

Run only if N1 with one producer has already beaten solo. Test +2 and then +4 under the marginal gate
above. Stop at the first non-win. Separately retain the P4 critic result; a critic is not counted as a
producer merely to make team size look larger.

### Wave 5 — Synthesis and purge

- publish the affordance decision and scoped routing guide;
- update every carried wildcard disposition;
- preserve counterevidence and invalid runs;
- delete losing candidate implementations and obsolete harness branches;
- name the smallest product change, if any, for a later implementation sprint.

No wave runs simply because it exists. Each transition cites the unresolved question it will answer.

## 9. Measurements

### Outcome quality

- native artifact acceptance and hidden evaluator result;
- blind preference and severity-ranked defects;
- factual coverage, citation correctness or decision usefulness for non-coding work;
- regressions outside the changed seam;
- contribution accepted unchanged, revised or rejected.

### Efficiency

- request to accountable lead, first useful artifact and accepted outcome;
- wall time, summed actor time and useful overlap;
- lead, Staff, critic, cached and newly processed tokens;
- tool calls, environment rediscovery and repeated file reads;
- briefing, question, integration, review and recovery effort;
- owner interventions and Exec occupied time.

### Communication information gain

A communication event counts as useful only if later evidence shows that it changed at least one of:

- the responsibility or acceptance understanding;
- an interface or artifact consumed by another actor;
- a decision, repair or rejection;
- a material unknown that would otherwise remain unresolved.

Narration, acknowledgements and repeated status do not count as information gain. The report retains
raw event types so this remains auditable rather than a model-scored vanity metric.

### Team-choice evidence

For every N1 run, record before result visibility:

- lead saturation: below, near or beyond one effective session;
- shared-state coupling: low, bounded interface or high;
- artifact separability: coupled, stable seams or evidence breadth;
- differentiated value: specialisation, independent evidence, parallel capacity, criticism or none;
- expected integration/review cost;
- chosen team size and rationale.

After the run, record whether the marginal contribution repaid its measured coordination cost. Do not
derive a universal scalar score.

## 10. Advisory routing guide — intended output, not runtime policy

The sprint should produce a short evidence card for leads:

1. Can I complete this coherently below saturation?
2. Is there a stable responsibility with an independently useful artifact or decision?
3. Does another actor add differentiated skill, evidence, criticism or real parallel capacity?
4. Can I state the interface and observable proof without pretending uncertainty is resolved?
5. Is expected integration and review cost lower than the likely gain?
6. If the work changes, can we exchange one consequential update instead of maintaining a meeting
   ritual?

Provisional interpretation to test:

- **0 Staff:** coupled work, volatile shared state, below lead saturation or no useful difference;
- **1 producer:** one stable seam or evidence region with clear proof;
- **1 critic:** uncertainty is primarily independent judgement, not production capacity;
- **2–3 Staff:** several stable, independently useful fronts and a lead with integration capacity;
- **4+ Staff:** exceptional breadth only after positive marginal evidence.

These remain prompts for lead judgement. They must not become hard-coded thresholds, a task router or
an owner-facing team-size form.

## 12. Validity and stop rules

- Freeze task bytes, seed, model/tool envelope and evaluator before each matched set.
- Randomise arm order and isolate workspaces.
- A model/provider/launcher failure before useful cognitive work is infrastructure-invalid.
- A missing callback is not automatic invalidity when A4 is the variable; preserve process/artifact
  evidence and apply the frozen recovery judgement.
- Evaluator mutation restarts every affected arm.
- First clear live loss of an affordance stops that branch and deletes its implementation.
- A provisional winner requires three-shape replication including one non-coding task.
- No lead + 1 win means no larger-team experiment.
- A candidate that improves speed but materially worsens quality, provenance, authority separation or
  epistemic honesty loses.
- A candidate whose mechanism cannot be distinguished from extra context/token allocation is not a
  mechanism win.
- Pause for founder judgement before introducing a new durable product entity, live-company effect,
  model change or combined candidate arm not named here.

## 13. Evidence and knowledge-base layout

Every run uses the existing coordination knowledge base; the sprint adds no parallel learning folder
or registry:

- `REGISTRY.md` remains the only experiment-status canon;
- `EVIDENCE.md` indexes claims to raw runs and native artifacts;
- `FAILURES.md` keeps the stable failure vocabulary and counterevidence;
- `CANON.md` receives only repeated or strongly discriminating findings;
- `PROGRAM.md` retains unexecuted hypotheses and final wildcard dispositions; and
- `experiments/<id>/` contains only activated frozen cards, small evidence indexes, blind reviews and
  immutable results.

New learning claims use `templates/learning.md` and are folded into `CANON.md` when warranted. Reports
link to native artifacts; they do not copy productive work into a custody store.

## 15. Start resolutions

The 23 August start signal applies the proposed conservative defaults:

1. Run A4 first because stranded useful artifacts are already an observed product failure. Return to
   A1 and select A2/A3 only from measured residual churn.
2. Keep P0–P5 as the complete initial portfolio. A new task requires a live unresolved dimension, not
   a desire for matrix coverage.
3. Require replication across two coding shapes and one non-coding shape before production
   recommendation. A smaller evidence set may remain provisional but cannot change product canon.
