**Status:** Draft for founder approval

## Why this programme exists

Sprint 26 proved that Restless can preserve exact work, gates, process cleanup and evidence without an
operator repairing the harness. EXP-16 proved that deterministic NPC policies are useful mechanics
testers but do not establish a rendered player experience. EXP-17 found that one durable producer beat
routine model-mediated supervision on four bounded work shapes, while leaving true parallel capacity
untested.

The next programme converts those results into product behaviour and a harder dogfood target:

1. make accountable supervision event-driven and cheap;
2. turn Swift Arrival from a mechanics prototype into one convincing rendered workday; and
3. test a team only where several workers can close disjoint units concurrently.

## Frozen operating principles

- Exec delegates each company outcome to one accountable lead and returns to availability.
- The lead remains non-producing. It owns mission, authority, partitioning, exceptions and the final
  owner-facing account.
- One durable worker owns one coherent artifact end to end by default.
- A lead model turn is caused by material judgement, not by every progress event or passing gate.
- Parallel workers receive disjoint custody and close their own units. Collection is mechanical; a
  semantic fan-in exists only for a named decision that cannot be made locally.
- The Work graph records ownership, causal events, attempts, evidence, effects and terminal truth. It
  does not prescribe the worker's reasoning process.
- Deterministic gates establish mechanics. A source-blind rendered operator establishes usability.
  Only a human sample can support a fun or convincing-play claim.
- Timeouts are safety envelopes, never success evidence. Completion comes from terminal events and
  receipts.

## Programme-level success

The programme succeeds only if all of the following are true:

1. A nominal bounded task retains an accountable lead but incurs no routine paid lead turn between
   delegation and a passing worker outcome.
2. Material failure, ambiguity, authority and owner-feedback events each wake the accountable lead
   once and preserve one causal decision trail.
3. Swift Arrival exposes a complete 10-15 minute rendered workday through ordinary player controls,
   passes deterministic mechanics, and earns both source-blind visual completion and explicit founder
   acceptance.
4. EXP-18 produces an honest `single`, `parallel` or `no crossover` decision under a frozen comparison.
5. Every company, model request, candidate, review and cleanup action has an exact identity and spend
   record. No raw screenshots, videos, model homes, credentials or evaluator processes remain after
   synthesis.

## Global stop rules

- Stop a track when its declared terminal gate passes or its negative boundary is reached.
- Do not add content to hide a missing foundation, or refactor a stable foundation to tune a parameter
  the current architecture already expresses.
- Three consecutive valid candidates failing for the same structural reason force a written boundary
  decision before another paid repair.
- Infrastructure-invalid work is excluded and may be replayed once after a bounded repair. Product
  failure remains counted.
- Do not expand into an MMO, broad content production, public launch or live external effects during
  this programme.
