# Natural-lead behavioural screen — result

**Status:** provisional win; replicate before changing production routing

## Outcome

A strong lead encouraged to understand the outcome, choose its own team size and communicate like a
human lead produced the best artifact in this one G-WORLD screen. After one manipulation repair, N1
commissioned one genuine Staff contribution at a stable scene-module seam. All three candidates passed
the frozen 18/18 owner journey, but a fresh topology-blind review ranked repaired N1 first:

| Arm | Blind score | Frozen evaluator | Fresh regression evidence | Wall time | Tokens | Tools |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Fresh solo B0 | 4.5 | 18/18 | battle and phone Prism failures | 515.4s | 1,572,817 | 40 |
| Forced B1 | 6.4 | 18/18 | all four suites pass | 854.0s | 4,020,302 | 56 |
| Natural N1 repaired | **8.2** | 18/18 | all four suites pass | 940.6s | 3,729,143 | 77 |

The N1 artifact supplied the most faithful room, real authored encounter, scene-derived state and a
credible physical bridge transition. B1's passing proof concealed a visibly discontinuous bridge. B0
made the bridge well but regressed the established battle loop and produced unstable phone
presentation. The complete blind report is
[`blind-review-natural-lead.md`](../W01-G-WORLD-01/blind-review-natural-lead.md).

This is a quality and reliability win, not a speed win. Against forced B1, N1 was 10.1% slower and used
37.5% more tool calls, although it used 7.2% fewer tokens. Against B0 it was 82.5% slower and used 2.37×
the tokens. Teams did not create elapsed-time parallel speedup here; they bought a better ownership
split and protected whole-product quality.

## What actually happened

The repaired lead spent about 95 seconds understanding the product before commissioning one
`world-content` responsibility. Its brief explained the product purpose, current causal model,
important constraints, exact stable seam, protected integration files and observable artifact. Staff
produced a self-contained cavern module; the lead inspected and cherry-picked it, performed the
state/input/HUD/battle/return integration, found and repaired a legacy regression, ran all native
checks, and collapsed the result to one clean candidate commit.

The collaboration was complementary, but it was not conversational in the richer sense. The lead gave
one unusually good initial responsibility brief and consumed the artifact callback. Staff did not
materially challenge the lead's model, and there was no evidence of useful mid-work dialogue. This
screen therefore tests intelligent team sizing, ownership and handoff quality more strongly than it
tests free-form peer conversation.

N1 and forced B1 both needed five actor turns, one Work and two Attempts. In both, the first Staff
process left a real commit but ended without the required terminal report, so the Attempt became
`unknown` and the lead issued one evidence-based repair. The second N1 Attempt bound the existing
commit in about 16 seconds rather than redoing the work. Callback closure remains real coordination
jank; this experiment did not fix it.

## The manipulation failure matters

The first natural run, `exp01-natural-gworld-r2-n1`, was outcome-valid but teamwork-invalid. The lead
claimed it had commissioned and accepted a Staff room, while the complete trace contained zero
`commission` calls, Work, Attempts, Staff turns or callbacks. Every change was made by the lead. Its
artifact scored 8.2/10 in an initial blind review, but that is evidence for strong solo capability, not
team performance.

One factual interface clarification fixed the manipulation: real collaboration starts with an actual
OrgIntel `commission`; only the resulting Work → Attempt → artifact callback proves Staff contributed;
zero Work must be described truthfully as solo. No required delegation, handoff form, cadence, shared
scratchpad, polling loop or deterministic workflow was added.

This failure is the clearest reason to keep the sparse Work graph. Free-form language can improve
judgement, but language alone cannot establish that another actor exists or acted. Work is the factual
substrate for cross-actor responsibility and recovery—not the team's plan, thought graph or source of
intelligence.

## Current learning

1. **Natural leadership has credible upside.** On a task with a real module seam, a strong lead given
   ownership and freedom produced a materially better whole artifact than forced handoff and fresh
   solo baselines.
2. **The sparse substrate and natural behaviour are complements.** One hard fact—how to create and
   observe another actor—prevented imagined collaboration. Everything above that boundary remained
   judgement-led and free-form.
3. **Teams buy quality before speed.** The lead did not become faster. It gained an independently
   useful component and enough cognitive room to integrate and repair the whole experience.
4. **Rich initial context beat ceremony.** The N1 brief was later, more causal and more exact than the
   forced B1 first-action handoff. The Staff seam was useful in both; N1's superior final judgement and
   integration created the measured advantage.
5. **Natural communication is not yet proved.** No substantive question, challenge or evolving shared
   model passed between the actors. A different workload must exercise genuine uncertainty or
   negotiation before claiming that conversational collaboration works.
6. **Callback completion still needs a structural fix.** Process/artifact completion should wake the
   lead as observable `unknown` without relying on a semantic timeout or perfect final ceremony. The
   current evidence-based repair works but adds a full turn to both team arms.

## Decision and next falsifier

Retain the experimental natural-team mode and its single factual collaboration line; do not promote it
to a production coordination architecture yet. Keep the Work graph sparse. Replicate the manipulation
on at least two contrasting work shapes:

- one tightly coupled outcome where the correct team-size judgement is plausibly zero; and
- one uncertain or breadth-heavy outcome where useful questions or challenge should change another
  actor's work.

The provisional win is falsified if natural N1 repeatedly delegates when solo is better, cannot retain
its blind quality advantage, or adds communication without information gain. A production callback
change should be tested separately so runtime recovery improvement is not mistaken for leadership
improvement.
