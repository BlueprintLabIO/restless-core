## Saturation signals

Add no management layer merely because one fraction is high. First inspect whether the lead:

- misses the frozen sample or exact exception set;
- responds outside the consequence window;
- allows a systemic or high-harm defect through review;
- spends enough review time to erase worker overlap;
- repeatedly guides the wrong Work or cannot recover exact evidence; or
- cannot contract the pool after demand clears.

Ordinary success should remain coalesced. Policy change, harm, blockage, contradiction and exact gate
failure should wake the lead with the affected Work and evidence. EXP-05 establishes Q2 as a useful
span in two scoped queues and shows four-way admission, but it does not establish a general span of
four.
