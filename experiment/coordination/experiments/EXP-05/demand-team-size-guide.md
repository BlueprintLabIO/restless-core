# EXP-05 demand and team-size guide

This is an observed decision surface, not a staffing formula.

## Current operating prior

```text
one coherent outcome
  → one accountable lead + one end-to-end Staff producer

independently closing repeated units
  → add Staff only while valuable work is simultaneously waiting
  → require response-time or coverage value, provider headroom and local acceptance
  → stop at the first replicated marginal loss or tail-quality breach

separate independently judgeable outcome
  → appoint another accountable lead
  → do not turn Exec into a status relay
```

## Observed ranges

| Shape | Demand | Q1 → Q2 result | Larger/elastic result | Current guidance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sales/account ownership | 240 available at once | replicated crossover: 1.98× then 2.83× throughput; p90 0.53× then 0.37×; charged cost lower | Q4 1.76× faster than replicated Q2 but failed tail tolerance | Q2 is the accepted ceiling for this exact backlog; add only against sustained simultaneous waiting work |
| Sales/account ownership | six batches every 45s | D1 1.24× throughput with no p90 gain; D2 1.32× with no p90 gain | both natural elastic leads stayed Q1 | Pace and service curves absorbed the available overlap; do not staff from total population alone |
| Monitoring breadth | 40 entities / 280 documents available | 1.92× throughput, p90 0.55×, charged cost 0.85×; both blind accepted | Q4 not activated by the sales marginal stop | Q2 is justified for this bounded independent search space; keep alerts local |
| Support under change | 96 cases paced | Q2 diagnostic roster created the causal boundary | causal event closed 96/96; terminal delivery failed 16 units | worker count is not the main result; preserve an available, addressable supervisor during material change |
| Company portfolio | four independent outcomes | four Q1 departments produced 90/90 exact units | semantic cell infrastructure-invalid | separate leads match closed accountability; fix dispatch latency before claiming company economics |

## Questions a lead should judge

1. Can each unit be owned and accepted without a model recomposing the batch?
2. Is valuable work waiting **while** current Staff is occupied, rather than merely numerous overall?
3. Does earlier completion change response value, coverage, risk or opportunity?
4. Can the provider and tools sustain the added overlap?
5. Can the lead sample normal work and handle exceptions without erasing the gain?
6. Is marginal tail quality preserved?

“Large task,” word count and department name are not sufficient answers. EXP-05 shows that the same
240-account population crosses under D0 and does not cross under D1/D2.
