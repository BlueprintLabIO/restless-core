## First decision

Compare two identical durable research teams:

```text
Arm R: controlled signal -> owner relay -> Exec -> standing publication lead -> bounded Staff cycle
Arm M: controlled signal -> direct world message -> standing publication lead -> bounded Staff cycle
```

Both companies receive one initial owner mandate, the same repository, GPT-5.6 Sol, the same spend
and authority limits, and the same event sequence. Only later signal delivery differs. The lead
remains a non-producing supervisor; attributed Staff produces or reviews content.

The cell asks whether direct standing responsibility:

- removes owner/Exec relay without losing portfolio accountability;
- maintains one evolving accepted artifact across cycles;
- handles correction, duplicate, contradiction and irrelevance without churn;
- returns to genuine idle when no signal arrives; and
- works through current messages, Work/Attempts, files and Git without a Mission entity or workflow
  engine.

## Event sequence

Signals are injected only after the prior cycle reaches a semantic terminal state.

1. `E01` - publish one pointed article from a new accepted result.
2. `E02` - narrow that article when a later audit changes the boundary.
3. `E02` redelivery - do not create another substantive outcome.
4. `E04` - conflicting but low-trust evidence; investigate or defer without corrupting the article.
5. `E05` - high-salience but irrelevant news; no-op.
6. quiet interval - no input and therefore no new production, model wake or heartbeat artifact.

The controlled fixture validates causal and organisational behaviour only. It is `_test` evidence,
not public readership, adoption or market demand.

## Advancement

Workload B activates only if Arm M preserves exact artifact continuity, handles E02 redelivery and
E05 without substantive production, and creates no work during the quiet interval. A failure first
produces a diagnosis and the smallest alternative architecture to test. It does not automatically
earn new product machinery.
