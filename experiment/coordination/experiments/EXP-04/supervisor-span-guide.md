# EXP-04 supervisor span guide

This is an empirical prior, not a team-size rule.

| Work shape | Observed span | Current operating guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One coherent shared outcome | EXP-03 default remains one end-to-end worker | Add Staff only for differentiated expertise/evidence or a stable contribution that repays integration. |
| 48 locally closing sales accounts | Q2 did not cross the +25% accepted-throughput gate; a corrected/coalesced Q2 diagnostic reached +20.3% worker-window throughput but was 3.8% slower end to end and 17.3% dearer per accepted account | Start Q1. Add capacity only when real backlog/service latency is valuable enough to dominate fixed actor orientation and lead review. |
| 12 evidence-search entities / 84 documents | Q2 worker-window throughput was effectively flat (+0.3%) and cost/alert rose 47.0% | One analyst owns this bounded corpus. Parallelise only a materially larger search space or urgent time-to-first-alert objective. |
| Provider/runtime | Q2 exact terminal work passed; Q4 produced all files but only 3/4 terminal reports | Treat two as the proved concurrency envelope for this route today. Q4 is infrastructure-invalid, not a cognitive team loss. |
| Lead attention | Lead active time was 60% of Q1 sales request time, 76% of Q1 monitoring, and 69% of the corrected E1 uniform arm | Lead sampling and final judgement are already the scarce resource. Full-population deterministic checks should feed anchors/exceptions/sample evidence; the model should not cognitively reread every normal unit. |

Ordinary successful partition callbacks should be coalesced until the batch is ready. Harm, policy
change, blockage, contradiction or a decision-relevant exception still wakes the lead immediately.
Do not add a management layer merely because the lead is saturated: first reduce avoidable review,
improve deterministic evidence, lower exception rate, or give a genuinely independent outcome its own lead.
