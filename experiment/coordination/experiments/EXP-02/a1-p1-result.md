self-contained cavern module in one clean Attempt and commit, and Sol integrated it after inspection.

Attempt after a missing callback, but its final native artifact was materially stronger.

## Decision

A1 does not replicate. It removed factual source loss on P3 and shortened this P1 run, but it did not
preserve whole-artifact quality across work shapes. Under the first-clear-loss rule, the experimental
commission-locator implementation is deleted and A2 is not activated: this run did not expose residual
causal-context loss that a history fork could specifically repair. The general product floor remains
ordinary access to exact authoritative artifacts when the Work actually depends on them; no new
commission capsule is recommended.
