# EXP-02 advisory routing guide

This is evidence for lead judgement, not a score, router or required ceremony.

## Start with one accountable lead

Remain solo when the outcome fits one strong session, shared state is tightly coupled, or the apparent
subtask is merely a fragment that the lead must still understand and integrate. Nominal task size,
estimated minutes and the existence of two editable files are weak signals.

Evidence: solo won small coupled C-SL, repeated small separable C-SH and broad coupled C-LL. On P0 a
natural lead found a genuinely bounded presentation seam, but +1 doubled wall time at outcome parity.

## Add one producer only for valuable difference

One producer is plausible when all of these are true:

1. the lead is near or beyond one effective session;
2. the responsibility has a stable boundary and an independently useful artifact or decision;
3. Staff adds differentiated skill, evidence breadth or real parallel capacity;
4. Staff can access the exact authoritative inputs and native proof target;
5. the lead can inspect and integrate the result without recreating it; and
6. expected quality/evidence gain is large enough to repay briefing, review and integration.

Counterexamples matter. C-SH had a clean accepted seam and still lost. R-LH had evidence breadth but
the producer lacked the frozen corpus, so the lead rebuilt it. A1 repaired that access failure on P3,
yet its coding replication produced a materially weaker native artifact. Exact context access is a
prerequisite, not a team-performance mechanism.

The one positive point is P1's prior natural team: +1 produced the best blind game artifact, though it
was much slower. That is one work shape, not a general crossover.

## Add a critic for independent information, not ritual

A critic is plausible when they bring evidence, expertise, taste, incentives or adversarial distance
the lead does not already have. Do not insert a critic merely to reread the same artifact and checks.

On P4, solo Sol and Terra-plus-Sol found the same planted defects. The critic arm doubled wall time,
lost blind final-review quality and created claims the lead had to unwind. Separately, a fresh Terra
negative control found a real snapshot defect in a previously accepted candidate. That supports
criticism as a discretionary source of independent discovery, not a standing workflow step.

## Larger teams

Do not infer a +2 or +4 frontier. Only one distinct producer shape has a quality-adjusted +1 win; P0,
P3 and prior ordinary-team cells do not supply a second. The sprint therefore did not activate larger
teams. If future +1 wins appear on two distinct shapes, add one genuinely independent responsibility
at a time and stop at the first non-win.

## Communication and recovery floor

Keep these factual and small:

- the lead knows which coordination tools actually exist;
- Staff can retrieve exact authoritative artifacts when its Work depends on them;
- Work/Attempt/artifact events prove that another actor truly owned and returned work;
- process and artifact observations survive a missing callback as `unknown`;
- the lead inspects the native outcome and decides acceptance.

Do not turn those facts into mandatory handoff templates, shared plans, rich recovery briefs, meeting
cadences or a second workflow protocol. A material change should travel as an ordinary addressed event
when one occurs; EXP-02 observed no repeated context-reconstruction bottleneck justifying a special
event capsule.
