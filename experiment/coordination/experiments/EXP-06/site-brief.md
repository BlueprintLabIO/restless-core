# Restless public site benchmark brief

Starting from exact Git commit `06c114fc2ef2244777df78c8a754386f50faeeef`, turn the existing
Astro project in `site/` into an exceptional public website for Restless.

Restless is an autonomous-company control plane for ordinary owner/operators. The central promise is
that the company does the work: the owner supplies mission, judgement, taste and sign-off; an always
available Exec delegates each executable outcome to one accountable lead; the lead remains a
non-producing supervisor; Staff produces and verifies the result; the system brings the prepared
last mile back to the owner. Safety, authority and auditability make this power trustworthy, but they
are not the headline.

Produce the finished site, not a proposal. Read the repository's `AGENTS.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`,
`LLM_CURE.md`, relevant product specs and frontend reference document before making claims or visual
decisions. Inspect the operator app in `web/` and make the public site feel like the same product
family without copying an application dashboard into a marketing page. Research current competitors,
including `opencompany.cloud`, and current criticism of generic AI-made websites. Use primary or
source-first material where feasible. Keep any public comparison fair, specific, supportable and
dated.

The finished information architecture must give distinct, spacious treatment to:

- a home page with an immediately legible value proposition and memorable product demonstration;
- the product and the owner experience;
- how Exec, leads, Staff, authority and prepared owner judgement work;
- research, experimental findings and honest evidence;
- a competitor/category comparison and Restless's precise differentiation; and
- a findings or journal surface that can grow into a high-quality research blog.

Writing quality and product truth matter as much as implementation. Write for a smart owner/operator,
not an agent-framework engineer. Prefer concrete verbs, short sentences, evidence and useful
specificity. Remove generic AI cadence, fake quotations, inflated inevitability, repetitive summary,
decorative jargon and claims the repository cannot support. Do not use em dashes. Do not use eyebrow
labels or subtitles as habitual decoration; use tooltips or hover explanations when secondary context
is genuinely useful.

Give the site a modern technical palette, generous negative space, strong typography and a coherent
design system derived from the existing product. Add one memorable, technically polished visual
signature such as restrained liquid-metal motion or a related material treatment. Study high-quality
patterns from Beautiful UI, Cult UI, Magic UI and the repository's source-first references, then port
only the qualities that fit the Astro implementation. Do not import React or a second visual identity.
Avoid cards everywhere, gradients everywhere, glowing blobs, excessive rounded containers, endless
pill badges, stock dashboard diagrams, ornamental grid noise and motion without meaning. Components
must clarify the product or create a deliberate moment of delight.

The result must be responsive, accessible and fast. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus,
reduced motion and narrow mobile layouts without horizontal overflow. Preserve truthful metadata,
social preview support and sensible progressive enhancement. Keep dependencies proportionate.

You may change only `site/` and may add a short arm-local evidence note under `site/evidence/`. Do not
change Restless product code, experiment contracts or other documentation. Do not publish, deploy,
message anyone, or inspect the other benchmark arm. Work autonomously until the candidate is genuinely
ready for owner review. Run the repository's site checks, build the production site, inspect the
rendered result at desktop and mobile sizes, repair material defects, and leave one final Git commit.
In the evidence note, record the exact checks run, the resulting routes, and any important known
limitation. Your final response should lead with the runnable review target and commit, then state the
checks and the most important design choices concisely.
