# EXP-17 keep, change, purge

## Keep

- Exec always delegates company outcomes to an accountable lead and stays available.
- The lead remains non-producing and owns mission, authority, partitioning, exceptions and owner-facing
  judgement.
- One end-to-end worker owns one coherent artifact.
- Work/Attempt, causal signals, gates, immutable ReviewTargets and effect authority remain the durable
  control plane.
- The first-party Codex runner, exact model/effort admission, durable resume and task-local evidence.
- Locally closing units, direct delivery and sparse outcome-specific fan-in as the basis for real
  parallel capacity.

## Applied during closure

- Product-gateway discovery now derives its default from the active daemon port offset rather than a
  silent hard-coded `7790`.
- The longitudinal R1 controller now removes its productive-process PID marker after verified
  termination.
- Corrected blind-review bundles used read-only `0555` directories and `0444` files and proved access
  from the reviewer identity before the replacement reviews ran.

## Change next

- Make routine single-worker supervision event-driven. A lead should not need a fresh LLM call merely
  to restate an exact owner brief or approve an already passing bounded artifact.
- Add an exact pre-frozen `RP-Q2` controller with disjoint custody, per-worker budgets, local gates,
  mechanical collection and no semantic assembler.
- Promote candidate custody normalization and reviewer-identity readability checks from the corrected
  EXP-17 bundle procedure into a reusable product review primitive.
- Give every concurrent model stream a unique settlement identity and atomic per-request spend record.
- Export per-turn tokens, active time, failed tools, duplicated gates and supervisor decisions directly
  into the run ledger.
- Extend the terminal residue assertion beyond the repaired R1 marker to every evaluator-owned process
  and file namespace.
- Add a fixture-author lint: every hidden exact value must be traceable to the owner brief or starting
  public contract.

## Purge

- The claim that a non-producing lead automatically improves bounded work.
- Mandatory LLM commissioning and acceptance narration for every coherent task.
- Parallel decomposition of coupled artifacts and generic synthesis fan-in.
- Outcome comparisons that pool unlike work shapes.
- Reusing a partial review after access, metering or custody invalidity.
- Designing an `RP` split after inspecting first-pair outcomes.
- Timeout expiry as success evidence; time remains a safety envelope only.
