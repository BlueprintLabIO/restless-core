# EXP-17 results

**Disposition:** concluded after four sparse valid `C/R1` pairs  
**Producing model:** exact GPT-5.6 Sol, high effort, shared first-party Codex runner  
**Valid production spend:** USD 7.6867  
**External effects:** 0

## Outcome

The same Codex worker produced practically equivalent outcomes with and without Restless supervision
across small coherent coding, large coupled coding, independently closing account work and a changing
longitudinal decision ledger. Every final candidate passed its visible and sealed native gates. Two
fresh source-blind reviewers accepted every candidate, and no final score required adjudication.

The supervisor did not earn its measured overhead in any sampled cell. Across the four valid pairs,
`R1` took 1,767.04 seconds and spent USD 5.5093; `C` took 754.84 seconds and spent USD 2.1774. That is
2.34x elapsed time and 2.53x spend for `R1`. Pooling quality across unlike cells is forbidden, but each
individual cell was already within the five-point parity threshold.

`C` means the performance route for this observed bounded shape. It does not mean Exec should bypass
department leadership for authority-bearing live-company work. The product implication is to keep the
lead accountable and hot, but avoid mandatory LLM commissioning and acceptance turns when no material
exception requires judgement.

## What the treatments actually proved

- Exec delegated exactly once in every `R1` run and returned to availability.
- Each lead commissioned one end-to-end worker and changed zero artifact bytes.
- Every `R1` Work produced one immutable ReviewTarget with no external effect or owner rescue.
- Both longitudinal arms applied five unique signals, ignored the causal duplicate, survived one exact
  productive-process replacement and retained the final provenance.
- The solo durable Codex thread recovered as well as Work/Attempt plus lead supervision in this one
  longitudinal instance. The expected unique continuity benefit did not appear.
- Both account arms delivered 24/24 policy-correct, source-isolated units. A single supervised worker
  created no throughput advantage; true `RP-Q2` remains unmeasured.

## Evaluator and harness corrections

The first LC-2 pair was invalidated after its hidden fixture demanded undocumented response strings and
one undocumented repeat-completion convention. Amendment 002 changed only those assertions to the
owner's semantic no-effect/idempotency requirements, then replayed both arms from fresh starting bytes.
The replay passed.

Blind review also found two real harness defects. Concurrent review streams sharing one company/actor
caused fail-closed metering, so all affected no-score attempts were discarded and reviews became
sequential. Separately, root ownership plus inherited `0440` modes made generated `R1` artifacts
unreadable to reviewers. All affected scores and one adjudication were discarded; fresh bundles used
`0444` files and `0555` directories and were verified from the reviewer identity before rerun.

The total blind-review programme spent USD 9.9650 including invalid infrastructure attempts and reruns,
more than the valid production arms. This is not product overhead and is not included in the pair table,
but it is a material experiment-cost finding. The current ledger cannot attribute every review request
separately once they share one actor identity.

## Stopping decision

Second S-C, L-C and E-L instances and transfer tasks stop because the first valid pairs already choose
`C` under the frozen rule. A second `R1` sample cannot become a default without first overcoming the
large observed overhead or showing a new recovery/authority condition.

`RP-Q2` is not rejected. It is the next experiment because P-I quality cleared the continuation gate,
but no exact parallel controller or split commitment was frozen. It must be specified before outcomes
are visible to its controller. Hermes remains not activated because it cannot change the primary Codex
architecture conclusion without a new parity and budget approval.
