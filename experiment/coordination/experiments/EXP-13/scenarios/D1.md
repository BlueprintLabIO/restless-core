# D1: native spreadsheet correction

## Public operator goal

Open `accounts-review.csv` in the prepared native spreadsheet application. Work only through the
visible spreadsheet UI.

For every row whose Review column says `CHECK`:

- replace the Amount with the value stated in that row's Note;
- change Review to `READY`; and
- leave every other field and row unchanged.

Save the result as `accounts-review-completed.csv`, close it, reopen the saved copy and verify the
visible values.

## Player boundary

Do not read or edit CSV files through shell, code, file APIs or model attachments. Do not inspect the
hidden expected output. Use only the assigned visual-control shape and the rendered native application.

## Evidence floor

- Initial spreadsheet capture.
- At least one capture while editing.
- Saved and reopened final capture.
- Ordered action/result trace.
- Concise `pass`, `revise`, `blocked` or `invalid` report.

A separate deterministic verifier compares the resulting CSV after the visual run. Its result is
ground truth for mechanics, not permission to infer what the player saw.
