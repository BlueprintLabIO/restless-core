# Restless product truth pack

Use these facts as the boundary for public claims. They describe the target product and current
research direction. Do not imply that every target capability is already generally available.

## Core promise

Restless is an autonomous-company control plane for ordinary owner/operators. Its target is useful
economic output per unit of owner attention, time, cost and bounded risk. It is not primarily a chat
assistant, agent builder, workflow canvas, task tracker or audit product.

The company does machine-doable work. The owner supplies mission, capital, judgement, taste, approval
and irreducibly personal acts. When identity, sign-in, CAPTCHA, two-factor authentication, legal
attestation, payment confirmation or owner judgement is required, the system should preserve the
prepared state and bring that exact last mile to the owner.

Safety, authority and receipts are the substrate that makes this power accountable. They are not the
main value proposition.

## Three logical layers

1. The Constitutional Kernel controls company identity, authority, secrets, budgets, consequential
   external effects and recovery. Deterministic checks belong at this boundary.
2. Organisational Intelligence coordinates goals, actors, sparse Work, Attempts, messages, memory and
   self-repair. Its state is recoverable and overridable rather than constitutional truth.
3. The persistent Company Linux Runtime is where agents use files, Git, browsers, tools and ordinary
   project software to produce outcomes.

Files are the primary work primitive. Git records meaningful checkpoints. Restless deliberately does
not use a universal command for every mutation, an immutable ledger of every thought, a governed
content-addressed asset lifecycle, a bespoke workflow engine or a disposable sandbox for every turn.

## Accountability shape

- The owner speaks to one singleton Exec.
- Exec owns the portfolio, delegates every executable request to exactly one accountable lead, then
  returns to availability for the next request.
- The lead owns one coherent outcome and remains a non-producing supervisor. It frames, commissions,
  observes, guides, redirects, repairs through workers and judges the exact result.
- At least one Staff worker produces each executable outcome. One end-to-end worker is the default.
- Additional Staff are useful only when a stable specialism, independent evidence, locally closing
  repeated units, unused parallel capacity or context saturation can repay briefing and integration.
- Several leads coordinate through material facts, decisions, artifacts and sparse dependencies.
  Continuous joint state is evidence that one lead should own the combined outcome.

The Work graph is a sparse factual record of real cross-actor responsibility, not a model's thought
graph or a deterministic workflow plan.

## Outcome-native review

Review should bring the outcome in its native form: a live site, playable build, rendered document,
image, audio, video or executable scenario. Commits, diffs, tests, logs and receipts are supporting
evidence unless the outcome cannot reasonably be run or rendered.

Before owner judgement, the accountable team should materialise and live-probe the exact current
candidate, pre-position the useful state, link it to the Work and Attempt, state the exact judgement
sought and make the resume condition observable.

## Maturity and honesty

Restless is a clean-slate rebuild and active research programme. The target architecture is explicit,
and many coordination claims are backed by controlled dogfood, but the full product is not presented
as a mature autonomous company in general availability. Public writing should separate:

- product intent and accepted architectural decisions;
- provisional beliefs supported by one or more experiments;
- hypotheses that still need replication or a real workload; and
- observed implementation or harness defects.

Do not invent customer adoption, integrations, service-level guarantees, financial performance,
security certification or unattended real-world authority.
