## Current routing prior

```text
Owner request
→ available Exec chooses one accountable lead and an envelope
→ lead owns the complete outcome
→ lead chooses player-coach or supervisor-only posture from real oversight and failure load
→ lead works directly when coupled judgement benefits from one causal model
→ lead may use bounded hands for parallel operations that own no project judgement
→ lead adds Staff only for a stable semantic seam with credible differentiated value
→ or scales a Staff pool for independently valuable queue units while marginal throughput repays it
→ independently judgeable outcomes receive separate leads rather than a larger intra-lead team
→ independent critic only when useful evidence or judgement is genuinely independent
→ material exception returns to Exec only at portfolio altitude
```

This is a prior, not a hard-coded router. The wildcard programme tests whether new context and
communication structures make collaboration worthwhile in cases where ordinary handoffs currently
lose.

## Unknowns that matter now

- How much of team overhead is initial briefing versus ongoing mutual-state maintenance?
- What future observed residual context loss, beyond ordinary exact artifact access, would justify reopening shared-history forks?
- Is one cognitive lead with parallel “hands” superior to several autonomous minds for coupled work?
- Which artifact and event boundaries generalise from code to research, documents and live operations?
- Can a cheap observable signal decide when communication is worth opening?
- Does any winning mechanism repay its implementation and operating complexity in production Restless?
- Do role-specific runtime capsules produce comparable gains for research, documents and operational
  work, where the native target is not a browser build?
- What observable pre-run signal predicts that an outcome exceeds one strong lead's effective session?
- What sustained-route probe predicts usable free-provider capacity without turning transient pressure
  into a fixed semantic timeout?
- Can lead guidance improve rejection of tidy but low-value seams without becoming a deterministic
  team-size router?
- Is fast inference itself moving the solo/team crossover, or are current losses mainly caused by
  avoidable briefing and integration weakness?
- Where does a bounded parallel hand stop being a tool and become semantic Staff responsibility?
- When do two separately accountable leads beat one broader lead without turning Exec into their
  coordinator or message relay?
- When does work become a replicated throughput queue, how far does same-role Staff scale, and when
  does lead review become the bottleneck?
- When does preserving a non-producing supervisor reduce drift and repair enough to repay the lost
  production capacity and extra handoff?
