# T7 — Replication and team size

## Observed friction

One attractive run can be order, task or reviewer noise. Conversely, increasing team size without a
one-worker win spends compute on a region already known to lose.

## Outcome

Replicate provisional winners across three distinct work shapes with fresh sessions. Measure lead +2
and lead +4 only after a lead +1 organisation wins at accepted-outcome parity.

## Ownership

Experiment evidence; model/provider allocation stays GPT-5.6 Sol/Terra so organisation remains the
changed variable.

## Acceptance

- Replications randomise order and include one non-coding and one materially different game task.
- Review sessions are independent and artifact-first.
- Team-size scope remains fixed and each added worker receives a genuinely independent seam.
- Marginal value includes all briefing, waiting, integration, churn and compute.

## Deletion

Stop the size curve at the first non-positive marginal step and delete unsupported larger-team paths.
