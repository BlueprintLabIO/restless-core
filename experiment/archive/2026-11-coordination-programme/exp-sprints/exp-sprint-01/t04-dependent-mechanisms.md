# T4 — Dependent mechanism screen

## Observed friction

Reunion, blackboard, delta and ambient-event ideas are meaningful only if a primary mechanism leaves a
measured state, context or delivery bottleneck. Testing them independently would provide weak causal
evidence.

## Outcome

Activate at most two of W02, W05, W06 or W08 only when a primary screen leaves its named residual
bottleneck. Mechanically viable candidates receive one matched live screen.

## Ownership

Experiment communication/state topology; no second source of truth is introduced into OrgIntel.

## Acceptance

- Activation cites the upstream candidate and residual failure code.
- State has provenance and remains reconstructable from ordinary artifacts/events.
- No mechanism becomes a durable plan database or universal coordination protocol.
- First live loss stops and deletes the branch.

## Deletion

Purge candidates that duplicate messages, project state or Work. Retain only evidence and a bounded
conformance probe.
