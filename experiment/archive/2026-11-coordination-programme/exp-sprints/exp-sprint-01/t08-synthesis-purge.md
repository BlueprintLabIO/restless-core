# T8 — Synthesis and purge

## Observed friction

Past experimentation accumulated reports, mechanisms and green checks without turning them into one
current routing decision. Completion requires one canon and fewer surviving modes.

## Outcome

Publish the smallest evidence-backed routing policy for strong lead, Staff, critic and any winning
mechanism; compile counterevidence and falsifiers; close every registry row; and delete losing or
duplicate experimental machinery.

## Ownership

Experiment knowledge. Production architecture changes only through a later founder decision backed by
the completed evidence.

## Acceptance

- `CANON.md`, `REGISTRY.md`, `EVIDENCE.md` and immutable run reports agree.
- Every wildcard is accepted, rejected, blocked, superseded or `not-activated`.
- A fresh-context audit can reproduce every material claim and native review handle.
- The final report states use/avoid conditions, confidence, counterevidence and falsifier.
- Generated state and credentials are absent from Git; `dev` is clean and pushed.

## Deletion

Remove losing mechanism code, obsolete launch paths and duplicate synthesis documents. Preserve small
reproducible probes and immutable evidence only.
