# T6 — Adaptive, authority and runtime candidates

## Observed friction

Adaptive rendezvous, Exec lineage, cached-prefix cloning and common-room/private-office rhythms target
different temporal or runtime bottlenecks. Their feasibility and authority risks differ and must not be
collapsed into one generic communication mode.

## Outcome

Give W14, W16, W03 and W15 final dispositions. Activate only candidates whose named temporal,
authority-transfer or prefix-replay bottleneck is observed and whose cheapest mechanism probe passes.

## Ownership

Experimental temporal/runtime topology. Exec authority, credentials and tool capabilities never leak
through a context fork.

## Acceptance

- W03 requires a redaction and tool-authority audit before any live cognitive comparison.
- W15 distinguishes actual state cloning from ordinary prompt caching or transcript replay.
- Communication gates open on observable events, not fixed semantic timeouts.
- Untriggered or infeasible candidates close without live spend.

## Deletion

Remove polling, cadence and cache adapters that do not beat ordinary event/artifact coordination.
