# T5 — Independence, allocation and redundancy

## Observed friction

Shared history may correlate errors; fixed ownership may allocate poorly; redundant branches may help
or merely multiply cost. W10–W13 should run only when the evidence exposes one of those uncertainties.

## Outcome

Give W10, W11, W12 and W13 final evidence-backed dispositions. Activate no more than two candidates
for any observed independence, transfer, allocation or recovery bottleneck.

## Ownership

Experimental team topology below the lead. Canonical integration and decision authority remain with
one lead.

## Acceptance

- An activated candidate has an explicit observed trigger and relevant baseline.
- Blindness, sealed branches and provenance are verified before inference.
- Added redundancy is charged in full and cannot win through expanded scope.
- Untriggered candidates are `not-activated`, not simulated into results.

## Deletion

Delete speculative branches and allocation machinery that do not produce net accepted value.
