# W1: Restless website comprehension and navigation

## Public visitor goal

Starting on the frozen Restless home page:

1. Explain in one sentence what Restless is for and who it is not for.
2. Reach the research corpus without typing a URL.
3. Find and open the article titled "One worker is a default, not a rule."
4. Return to the product explanation and identify the stated product boundary.
5. Repeat the navigation at a mobile-width viewport.
6. Report only material confusion, broken interaction or visual obstruction encountered naturally.

## Player boundary

Use the visible Chromium page and assigned visual-control shape. Do not inspect HTML, CSS, source,
DOM, accessibility tree, build output, producer rationale or expected findings. External links must not
be opened. Do not submit forms or send email.

## Evidence floor

- Initial desktop page, corpus, opened article and product page.
- Initial mobile page and mobile navigation outcome.
- Ordered action/result trace and exact URL observations from visible browser state only.
- Concise comprehension answer and `pass`, `revise`, `blocked` or `invalid` verdict.
