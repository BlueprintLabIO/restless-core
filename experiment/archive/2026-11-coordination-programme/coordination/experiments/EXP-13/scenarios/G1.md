# G1: Swift Arrival native journey

## Public player goal

Collect the parcel, make and recover from one ordinary mistake, drive the truck to the destination,
leave the seat and deliver the parcel.

Public controls are W/A/S/D movement or driving, mouse look, E interact and Q deliberate drop.

## Player boundary

- Use only the rendered native CLIENT, assigned control shape and public goal.
- Do not inspect source, logs, tests, Git, prior captures or expected defects.
- Start from fresh HOST and CLIENT processes.
- Read every decision-relevant current capture through the active model's native image path.
- Stop on visible completion or a reproducible blocker.
- Report direct observation separately from inference and uncertainty.

## Evidence floor

- Exact CLIENT numeric window identity and geometry.
- Initial native capture.
- Ordered action/result trace.
- Captures for pickup, deliberate mistake, recovery, loaded driving, route end, seat exit and terminal
  delivery or blocker where reached.
- Concise terminal report with `pass`, `revise`, `blocked` or `invalid`.

The controller and adjudicator hold the hidden native checkpoint rubric. A filename or command exit is
not visual evidence.
