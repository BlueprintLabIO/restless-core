# EXP-04 fan-in guide

## Keep local outcomes local

- accounts, cases, alerts, candidates, invoices, listings and similar units retain their own useful state;
- use a deterministic index, feed, roll-up or exact non-conflicting merge for normal aggregation;
- let the lead inspect anchors, exceptions and a frozen sample rather than commission a prose batch rewrite;
- coalesce ordinary unit completion events and surface only decision-changing exceptions early.

## Commission one cognitive fan-in only for

- one named go/no-go, allocation or prioritisation decision;
- one unresolved contradiction or policy exception;
- one integrated contract, release, campaign or incident outcome whose meaning does not close locally;
- one sufficiently large pattern analysis explicitly intended to change the playbook.

The launch must name that exact result, why local outcomes or deterministic aggregation are
insufficient, the bounded authoritative inputs, one synthesis worker and one terminal acceptance
surface. No nested fan-in is justified by EXP-04.

## Anti-patterns

- universal assembler after every batch;
- summary of summaries when exact sources remain available;
- model rewrite of account/case/alert state merely to make one final file;
- status fan-in or immediate lead wake for every ordinary success;
- consensus without a disputed decision;
- synthesis used as memory because actor sessions or artifact handles are weak.

EXP-04 did not activate R4-S: Q2 monitoring produced no material discovery-throughput gain and Q4
provider/tool conformance failed. EXP-03's scoped marketing strategist → producer quality win remains
the positive example of a real bounded fan-in; its research evidence → synthesist chain remains the
negative example.
