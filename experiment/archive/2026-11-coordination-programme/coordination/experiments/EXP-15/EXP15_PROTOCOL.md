# EXP-15 controller protocol

The authoritative sprint is `/company/reference/exp-sprint-15-swift-arrival-vertical-slice-campaign.md`.

## Invariants

1. Exec delegates once to one accountable non-producing Game Product lead and returns.
2. At least one Staff worker owns every production change; the lead never repairs artifacts directly.
3. The campaign runs 30–60 material player-outcome loops, not 30–60 model turns or patches.
4. Every loop freezes candidate, preset, seed, hypothesis, evidence, decision and cost.
5. Stable authority and simulation are changed only after evidence shows tuning cannot express the
   intended interaction; ordinary feel feedback changes data, content or presentation.
6. A playable candidate survives every accepted loop.
7. Mechanical gates run before sparse vision. Source-blind players see only public controls, objective,
   rendered target and their own run evidence.
8. Direct pixels and state receipts outrank agent claims. Founder judgement alone decides `fun enough`.
9. One provider refusal creates one shared cooldown. No polling, timeout cascade or actor-local retries.
10. Raw captures are transient, limited to 12 decisive frames per run and deleted after synthesis.

## Final native outcome

One coherent 15–20 minute workday includes depot loading, one walk-through truck, an obstacle route,
three destinations, six data-defined cargo types with one incompatible combination, a robber encounter,
a police inspection, fail-forward recovery and a cargo-history-aware result. Two rendered players can
complete it; four peers pass focused latency mechanics; a clean second launch also completes.

## Evidence ownership

The product repository is `/company/repos/swift-arrival`. Independent player Work is repo-less. Compact
campaign evidence lives below `/company/outputs/exp15`; raw PNGs and runtime process state never enter
the product repository.
