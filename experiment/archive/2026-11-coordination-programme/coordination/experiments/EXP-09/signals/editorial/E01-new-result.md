## Observation

In two matched all-at-once runs containing 240 fictional sales-account units, two Staff workers
produced materially higher accepted throughput than one worker while preserving the predeclared
quality floor. In a separate 40-entity monitoring workload, two workers produced about 1.9 times the
accepted throughput and about 0.55 times the p90 latency of one worker.

## Current interpretation

Repeated independent units can enter a different scaling regime from several minds coauthoring one
result. When valuable work is simultaneously waiting and units close locally, another worker can
repay orientation and supervision cost.

## Limits known at observation time

These are controlled `_test` workloads using one model/runtime envelope. The result does not establish
a universal worker count, a general law about sales or monitoring, or real market value.
