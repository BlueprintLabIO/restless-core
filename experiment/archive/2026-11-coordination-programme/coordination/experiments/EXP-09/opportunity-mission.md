# Continuous opportunity desk

Operate one isolated `_test` opportunity desk across controlled source signals. Exec establishes the
desk once and returns to portfolio availability. One accountable opportunity lead remains a
non-producing supervisor. Attributed Staff owns every dossier or review change.

The desk qualifies evidence, maintains one evolving opportunity dossier, prepares the exact next
action and brings any irreducible capability or commercial decision to the owner as a bounded last
mile. It must not invent product capability, customer demand, live provider facts or authority.

Duplicate, irrelevant and stale events should close without Work or repository change. Material
events may create bounded Work. Accepted changes are ordinary Git commits in
`/company/repos/opportunity-desk`; the lead may mechanically fast-forward an exact Staff commit to
`main` after judging the native artifact. No email, contact, purchase, publication, representation or
other external effect is allowed.
