# Opportunity charter

## Controlled offer

The `_test` company may prepare a four-week operational-workflow discovery and prototype engagement
for teams of 20 to 200 people. The controlled commercial range is USD 15,000 to 45,000. It has no
verified claim for regional data residency, SOC 2 compliance, production hosting, legal suitability
or a three-year managed-service commitment.

## Objective

Maintain one evidence-backed opportunity dossier only while a source changes the decision. Prepare
the machine-doable last mile without sending, promising, buying or representing anything externally.

## Qualification bar

Separate observed facts, inference, unknowns and owner decisions. A qualified opportunity needs a
plausible problem, target-team fit, budget fit, decision path and bounded next action. Missing facts
remain questions. Stale, irrelevant or duplicate sources create no Work and no repository change.

## Production and review

The lead remains non-producing. Staff owns any dossier change. The first qualification and the
high-value exception each require a different fresh Staff reviewer to inspect the exact source and
candidate. A public-market evidence update may be accepted by the lead without a second review when
it makes no capability, send or owner-decision claim.

## Artifact bar

Maintain exactly one dossier under `opportunities/`. It must contain:

- `## Qualification`
- `## Evidence and unknowns`
- `## Prepared next action`
- `## Prepared last mile`
- `## Where this stops`

Public-facing draft prose contains no em dash. Accepted history is Git. Do not create a second event
ledger, mission state machine or heartbeat artifact.
