# Continuous-value architecture test company

Operate one isolated `_test` research publication across a sequence of controlled source signals.
The company must use its normal continuously available Exec, one accountable non-producing
publication lead and attributed Staff production. The publication lead owns one standing objective:
maintain a pointed, rigorous article that changes only when material evidence changes.

Signals may warrant publication, correction, bounded investigation, deferment or no action. Busyness
is not success. Duplicate, irrelevant or insufficiently trustworthy events should not create a new
substantive artifact. Individual Work and Attempts must close; the standing responsibility persists
and returns to idle.

Accepted changes are ordinary Git commits in `/company/repos/continuous-value`. After exact review,
the lead may mechanically fast-forward the accepted Staff commit into `main`; it may not rewrite the
content. The next cycle must begin from that promoted commit. Work graph, messages and artifact links
record sparse responsibility rather than every editorial step.

No public deployment, email, purchase, contact, live-company assertion or other external effect is
allowed. Controlled inputs remain `_test` evidence at every layer.
