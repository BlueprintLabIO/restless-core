## Outcome

EXP-16 produced a useful deterministic NPC mechanics stack, not a player-equivalent playtester. The
delivery evaluator completed all six released held-out journeys. Recovery completed seven of eight;
the last case stopped boundedly with an honest oscillation packet. Robber and vampire policies each
passed six mechanics-conformance cases, and the final S6 regression kept delivery, held-out recovery,
robber and vampire gates green on the exact candidate.

The experiment found and repaired one genuine product defect: the delivery evaluator approached cargo
to 1.76 m while the authoritative interaction limit was 1.6 m. It did not manufacture the requested
maximum of five repair loops.

S7 then failed for the right reason. The final tree can render an opaque delivery/debug workday, but
arbitrary recovery cases and the new robber/vampire policies exist only in headless or synthetic
fixtures. It cannot render the frozen set of fresh producer-hidden journeys without a new product
candidate. The independent Terra critic therefore has no valid score, and no founder/human sample was
performed.

  required player-visible role journeys. This is explicit false assurance.
