# EXP-16 findings

## Enduring findings

1. **Cheap embodied mechanics are valuable, but only within their evidence boundary.** The shared body
   drove ordinary movement, interaction, vehicle, cargo and authority seams with zero model calls in
   the motor loop. Six of six held-out deliveries completed; seven of eight held-out recovery cases
   completed and the eighth terminated with a bounded oscillation packet.
2. **One real product defect justified the evaluator.** `DEL-C1` exposed a 1.76 m cargo approach against
   the host's ordinary 1.6 m interaction limit. The bounded repair replayed the discovery and survived
   the final delivery, held-out, robber and vampire regression.
3. **Headless role conformance is not rendered role integration.** Robber and vampire policies passed
   their six-case mechanics fixtures, but neither policy was wired into the ordinary rendered world.
   The same limitation applies to arbitrary recovery placements.
4. **Opaque review-target custody is a useful Runtime primitive.** A content-addressed pack plus an
   exact-window handle can keep a critic out of source and history. The first critic's refusal of a
   browseable tree was correct and prevented a false source-blind score.
5. **A final semantic gate is necessary precisely because deterministic gates can all be green.** S7
   rejected the implied player-facing claim after S1–S6 passed. This is measured false assurance, not
   a reason to weaken the final gate.

## Harness and protocol findings

- Gate-produced evidence must be generated inside the registered Runtime gate, not before it.
- External provider recovery needs an attributable reprobe/cooldown-clear primitive; this run required
  one audited operator correction after the configured key's local quota was raised.
- Role seeds were already visible in the activation manifest. S4/S5 are production-role conformance,
  not held-out generalisation.
- A target-preparation Work must receive a controller-owned journey contract, not be asked to invent
  supposedly hidden seeds. The exact candidate could not satisfy that contract anyway.
- Progress and infrastructure failures must stay separate from product repair counts. EXP-16 records
  one product repair loop, not five manufactured findings.

## Product routing

| Component | Decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared NPC body and receipts | Keep | Useful cheap mechanics/regression substrate with ordinary action and authority checks |
| Delivery evaluator | Keep, then expose through normal presentation | Found and repaired a real defect; rendered path exists but debug/probe presentation is not a human verdict |
| Recovery evaluator | Revise | Bounded and useful headlessly; arbitrary adverse placements are not rendered ordinary-world journeys |
| Robber policy | Revise before product use | Conformance passes, but the policy is not integrated into the rendered main world |
| Vampire policy | Revise before product use | Same integration gap; synthetic network actions do not establish a passenger experience |
| Claim that NPCs replace human playtesting | Purge | S7 and the missing founder sample directly contradict it |
| Opaque candidate-bound review surface | Keep as a Runtime primitive | It preserves evaluator independence when the product actually exposes the required journey |

No measured result crosses a defensible 10x product-quality threshold. The motor loop eliminated model
calls, but the frozen baseline did not provide a comparable counted model-call denominator, elapsed
delivery improved by only about 2.5x against the one 231 s public-surface journey, and S7 failed.
