# EXP-05 fan-in and wildcard dispositions

## Fan-in non-use

No cognitive fan-in ran. Account states, support packages, alerts and reconciliations each closed
locally and entered deterministic indexes. This avoided a central model rewrite and preserved exact
ownership. The company cell also did not funnel routine department status into Exec.

A later fan-in is justified only for one named result that local units cannot supply:

- a go/no-go, allocation or prioritisation decision;
- an unresolved cross-unit contradiction or policy exception;
- one integrated contract, campaign, release or incident outcome; or
- a bounded pattern analysis explicitly intended to change a playbook.

Summary-of-summaries, universal batch assemblers, status fan-in and model rewrites of accepted unit
state remain anti-patterns.

## Wildcard dispositions

The frozen activation rule required two counted failures of the same causal kind plus a matched
no-wildcard control. No candidate met it; `wildcard_authorized` is false in
[`program-status.json`](results/program-status.json).

| Candidate | EXP-05 trigger | Disposition | Reopening evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shared-history sibling fork | Staff repeatedly rebuild the same causal setup despite exact artifact access | not activated | two matched context-loss failures not explained by artifact, role or judgement defects |
| Causal context delta | material changes repeatedly force expensive full reorientation | not activated | ordinary addressed Work feedback delivered the policy change and preserved continuity; show repeated replay cost first |
| One mind, bounded hands | coupled work is tool-parallel but not semantically divisible | not activated | a coupled-work bottleneck with a genuinely non-judgement executor; no hidden cognitive Staff |
| Direct addressed peer message | genuine cross-unit dependency repeatedly arrives late | not activated | two decision-changing late dependencies; routine peer chat remains insufficient |
| Blind sibling diversification | independent evidence coverage is the measured deficit | not activated | monitoring Q2 already won through disjoint ownership; reopen only when duplicate independent evidence, not throughput, is the desired product |

The support terminal failure earns better exact failure delivery through existing events and gates, not
a semantic blackboard, shared transcript or new workflow primitive. The company evaluator failure
earns schema-constrained test output, not a production coordination mechanism.
