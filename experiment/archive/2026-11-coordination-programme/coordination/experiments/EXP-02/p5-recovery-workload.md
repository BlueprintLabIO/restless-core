# EXP-02 P5 — stranded research artifact recovery

**Status:** first non-coding screen complete; no A4 replication activated.

## Frozen success contract and native target

The productive target is one Markdown decision-support dossier derived only from eight frozen player
records Q01–Q08. A lead receives it after the producer process ends without terminal semantic
completion. The recovery target is a separate compact decision record that links the preserved
candidate and chooses accept, revise, resume, reassign or abandon with evidence.

The dossier acceptance contract requires:

- every Q01–Q08 identifier appears against a supported finding;
- at least two contradictions or segment differences remain visible;
- limitations distinguish this frozen qualitative sample from market truth;
- implications cover onboarding, navigation, battle legibility and social/retention direction;
- unanswered questions are explicit; and
- no external or repository-derived claim is presented as part of the frozen corpus.

The native reviewer opens both Markdown files and checks cited statements against the frozen records.

## Domain and why the work is real

This is non-coding evidence synthesis. The orphaned EXP-01 R-BREADTH Attempt demonstrated the exact
failure with a real Terra producer: a useful 7,895-byte dossier survived, but its callback/artifact row
did not. P5 freezes that work shape without counting the accidental run.

## Candidate fixture protocol

1. Run one Terra producer from a clean seed using the frozen corpus.
2. After its candidate passes the byte-level citation gate, preserve its exact workspace but suppress
   semantic completion for the P5 fault.
3. Hash the candidate and clone the same bytes/Git state into R0 and A4 recovery workspaces.
4. Freeze Work, Attempt, input fingerprint, start/end Git observations and evaluator before either
   Sol lead sees the candidate.
5. Randomise and record R0/A4 arm order.

Producer generation is charged in the report but is not compared between arms.

## Negative control

An otherwise identical workspace omits the dossier. It must produce “no recoverable artifact” rather
than an accept/revise content judgement. A verifier that passes the negative control is invalid.

## Blind review

Review recovery decision artifacts before reading lead narration. Prefer correct uncertainty,
candidate inspection, defect detection and economical next action. No aggregate speed score may hide
a false acceptance or duplicate producer launch.
