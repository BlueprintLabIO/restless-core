# EXP-02 production recommendation

## Decision

Recommend **no new durable coordination mechanism** from EXP-02.

Retain the existing architecture and product floor:

```text
available Exec → one accountable natural lead → 0..N Staff chosen by judgement
```

Work remains sparse factual evidence of real responsibility. Exact inputs, observable artifact/process
facts and native review remain ordinary runtime/context capabilities. They do not need a commission
capsule, shared-history entity, material-event protocol, rich recovery object, mandatory critic or
team-size router.

## Why

- A4's rich recovery brief improved blind provenance but did not reduce reconstruction and was slower.
- A1 fixed source loss on non-coding work but lost material native quality on coding replication.
- P0 showed that a natural lead can overvalue a neat but low-value seam; +1 doubled wall time.
- P4 showed that a critic with the same evidence duplicates a strong lead and can add claims that must
  be unwound.
- No producer won on two distinct work shapes, so larger teams were not evidence-authorised.

## Expected benefit

Avoid new schemas, prompt payloads, context compilers and standing review turns while preserving the
few facts that prevented false collaboration and destructive retry. This keeps judgement at the lead
and makes future failures attributable rather than hidden inside more machinery.

## Risks and disposition

| Risk | Disposition |
|---|---|
| Staff lacks an essential owner artifact | Guard with ordinary exact artifact access and native lead review; do not auto-attach every source. |
| Lead invents delegation or assumes tools are absent | Guard with factual capability context plus Work/Attempt/artifact evidence. |
| Missing callback strands useful work | Preserve process/workspace/artifact observations as `unknown`; wake the lead from evidence. |
| Lead chooses an unnecessary colleague | Accept as judgement variance; use the advisory guide and measure outcomes rather than hard-code routing. |
| Same-context review misses a defect | Use a critic when it brings independent evidence or perspective; do not mandate one. |

## Verification for later product work

No EXP-02 harness code is promoted. A later implementation sprint should prove the existing core
contracts in real OrgIntel/runtime dogfood: factual tool discovery, exact artifact access, event-driven
Attempt completion, preserved `unknown` recovery and native outcome review. Compare the complete
company outcome; do not create a unit-test proxy for a rejected capsule.

## Deletion target

Delete EXP-02-only commission-locator, recovery-capsule and critic harness branches after recording
their immutable result hashes. Retain the general natural-lead factual tool clarification because it
repairs a manipulation bug common to all arms, not an experimental affordance.
