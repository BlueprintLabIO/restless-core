# EXP-17 crossover guide

| Observed work shape | Performance route | Governance route | Evidence | Reopen when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small coherent bounded work | `C` | accountable lead may remain dormant/hot | quality parity; `R1` 2.91x slower and 3.10x spend | repeated solo regressions or authority-sensitive effects appear |
| Large coherent coupled work | `C` | lead wakes on failed gates, contract ambiguity or escalation | quality parity; `R1` 1.77x slower and 1.73x spend | a genuinely lead-saturating artifact or material mid-run change is frozen |
| Independent locally closing units at base load | `C` for one worker; `RP` unknown | lead owns policy, partition and exceptions | 24/24 in both arms; `R1` 2.94x slower and 3.30x spend | run pre-frozen disjoint `RP-Q2` against the best single-worker baseline |
| Longitudinal changing work with durable worker state | `C` | lead remains authority/exception owner | both recovered perfectly; `R1` 2.21x slower and 2.53x spend | horizon exceeds one durable thread, several workers rotate, or external authority changes |
| Live effects, cross-department conflict or regulated authority | unknown | Exec delegates to an accountable lead | not tested | run an effect-bounded governance experiment; do not infer from isolated fixtures |

These are observed routing decisions, not universal model truths. `C` denotes the minimal producing
topology. Restless may implement that topology behind a persistent accountable lead so long as routine
model calls do not recreate the measured overhead.
