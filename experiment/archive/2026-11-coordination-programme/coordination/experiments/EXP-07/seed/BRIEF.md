# Build the Restless public site from scratch

Create an exceptional, production-ready public website for Restless using only this brief,
`PRODUCT-TRUTH.md`, and `EVIDENCE.md` as private source material. You are starting in an otherwise
empty Git repository. Produce the finished site, not a proposal or mock-up.

Restless is for ordinary owner/operators who want a company that handles work, not another agent
framework to configure. The owner supplies mission, judgement, taste and sign-off. Restless keeps the
organisation moving, delegates production, supervises outcomes, repairs failures and returns with the
prepared decisions only the owner can make. Safety and authority make that power trustworthy, but
they are not the headline.

## Creative mandate

Choose a strong original thesis for the site. You have full freedom over framework, information
architecture, typography, palette, composition, illustration, motion and interaction. Before coding,
privately explore at least three materially different directions, reject the most generic one, and
commit to the direction that best expresses a self-running company for a smart owner/operator.

The result should feel designed, not assembled. Spend boldness in one memorable signature and keep
the rest disciplined. Use generous space, exceptional typography, confident editing and real product
content. A restrained direction must show exacting craft; a richer direction must remain legible and
fast.

Do not use the current Restless website, the BlueprintLabIO/restless repository, any EXP-06 output or
another benchmark arm as reference or source. Do not recreate a remembered incumbent. Research
current competitors and source-first design references independently when useful.

Reject common AI-site defaults unless the subject genuinely requires them: decorative eyebrows,
cards for every paragraph, gratuitous gradients, glowing blobs, interchangeable dark SaaS visuals,
endless pills, ornamental grids, fake dashboards, stock agent diagrams, oversized claims with no
proof, and motion that communicates nothing. Do not use em dashes anywhere in public copy. Secondary
context should be available through meaningful links, captions or accessible explanations rather
than repetitive subtitles above headings.

## Audience and writing

Write for a capable business owner, not an agent-framework engineer. Lead with what changes in their
working life. Use concrete verbs, short sentences, evidence and useful specificity. Explain technical
architecture only where it clarifies the product boundary or earns trust. Remove generic AI cadence,
inflated inevitability, fake quotations, repetitive conclusions and claims the supplied truth pack
cannot support.

The site is an honest research-stage product publication, not a fabricated customer-success page.
Calls to action may invite people to inspect the research, understand the product, follow the work or
discuss the direction. Do not invent users, revenue, waitlist counts, testimonials, integrations or
availability.

## Required public journey

Use distinct routes with enough space for each question. You may name and order the navigation in
your own voice, but the public site must include:

- `/` for the clearest expression of the value proposition and a memorable product demonstration;
- `/product/` for the owner experience, product boundary and prepared last mile;
- `/how-it-works/` for Exec, accountable leads, Staff, authority and outcome-native review;
- `/research/` for the theory, what is enduring, what is provisional and how experiments change it;
- `/compare/` for a dated, fair, sourced category comparison and precise differentiation;
- `/journal/` for a durable findings index; and
- three substantive finding pages under `/journal/` using supplied evidence.

The home page must demonstrate the product rather than merely naming it. This may be an interactive
owner moment, a narrative transformation, an animated operational state, a concrete before-and-after
or another native idea. It must remain understandable without animation.

## Research and sources

Use primary or source-first material for current competitor descriptions. At minimum inspect
`https://opencompany.cloud/` and choose two other genuinely adjacent products or frameworks. State the
comparison date. Distinguish observed facts from interpretation. Do not claim that an unrun benchmark
proves Restless is better.

For visual implementation, you may study public licensed sources such as Beautiful UI, Cult UI,
Magic UI and high-quality editorial or product sites. Extract a relevant quality rather than importing
a second identity. Record material external sources in your evidence note.

## Production contract

- Choose the stack and dependencies yourself, but keep them proportionate.
- Provide `npm run verify`, `npm run build`, a committed lockfile and a production `Dockerfile` that
  serves the site on port 80.
- Make every required route statically reachable or production-runnable without secrets.
- Support 390x844 and 1440x1000 without horizontal overflow.
- Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Supply truthful metadata, social preview basics and useful progressive enhancement.
- Add `evidence/FINAL.md` with the exact checks run, routes, sources, important choices and known limits.
- Inspect the rendered result at both target viewports, repair material defects and leave one final
  clean Git commit.

Do not deploy, publish, contact anyone or inspect the peer arm. Work autonomously until the candidate
is genuinely ready for blind owner review. Your final report should lead with the runnable local
review target and commit, then state observed checks and the most important design decisions.
