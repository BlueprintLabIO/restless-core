# EXP-07 blind outcome rubric

Reviewers receive only two hosted production sites labelled Candidate A and Candidate B. They may
inspect the complete rendered desktop and mobile experiences and public page source. They do not
receive arm identity, topology, prompts, traces, cost, timing, commit authorship or framework choice
until the decision is locked.

## Gates

A candidate is not rankable until it clean-installs, builds, serves every required route, has no
horizontal overflow at 390 px or 1440 px, exposes visible keyboard focus, respects reduced motion and
contains no em dash. A material product falsehood, fabricated evidence, copied incumbent design or
broken primary navigation is a rejection rather than a small deduction.

## Weighted score

| Dimension | Weight | Question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Immediate clarity | 15 | Can an owner/operator understand what changes in their working life within one screen? |
| Product truth and differentiation | 20 | Is the promise faithful, specific and meaningfully distinct from assistants, builders and agent frameworks? |
| Writing | 20 | Is the prose economical, natural, confident and free of generic AI cadence or technical self-absorption? |
| Creative direction | 20 | Does the site have a coherent identity and memorable subject-specific thesis that could not be mistaken for a template? |
| Information architecture | 10 | Do the pages form a spacious, persuasive journey and make research easy to inspect? |
| Interaction and responsive craft | 10 | Are the demonstration, navigation, motion, mobile behaviour, focus and details deliberate and robust? |
| Evidence and trust | 5 | Are experiments, limits, comparisons and calls to action honest, dated and useful? |

Score each dimension from 0 to 10, multiply by its weight, and report the weighted total out of 100.
For each candidate, name the three strongest choices, the three weakest choices and every defect that
would block publication. Then choose `A wins`, `B wins`, `tie` or `neither publishable` with a concise
evidence-based reason. The owner remains the final judge.

## Process evidence revealed after owner judgement

- elapsed time from identical brief delivery to review-ready commit;
- actor turns and observable usage;
- productive actor count and real Work or Attempt attribution;
- supervision interventions, repair loops and rejected attempts;
- files, dependencies, bundle size and audit findings;
- source and visual inspection evidence; and
- truthful owner handoff or missing last-mile delivery.

Process efficiency explains a result and breaks only a near tie. It does not rescue a weaker public
outcome.
