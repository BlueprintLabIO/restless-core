Non-negotiable grammar

  1. 01One dominant action per surface or owner decision.
  2. 02Status is a sentence. Shape and colour only reinforce it.
  3. 03Outcome → recommendation → evidence → related work → diagnostics.
  4. 04Fog and texture stop before truth, forms, receipts and authority.
Strength

Distinctive without making the work itself decorative. It gives background autonomy and foreground attention different visual weight.

Risk to test

Fog can read as vagueness or glassmorphism if contrast rises or content becomes translucent. Keep all consequential material fully opaque.
