Supervision must remain available. Presence is not correctness.

Finding 03AccountabilitySupport experiment

A lead is not a ceremonial layer. It carries the mission while Staff works: policy, context, correction and the judgement that the exact outcome is ready. One support experiment made the value visible.

96 / 96cases closed with causal policy delivery
80 / 96accepted by the terminal-only comparison

Causal policy delivery prevented 16 stale outcomes. The supervisor could change the work while it still mattered, rather than evaluating only at the end.

The uncomfortable counterevidence

The same supervised run introduced one duplicate-case contamination. Independent evaluation caught it. A mission keeper improved policy compliance and also created a fresh failure.

Supervision can make intervention possible. It cannot certify its own correctness.

The product decision

Restless keeps the lead distinct, non-producing and accountable for the complete outcome. The lead supervises through material events and artifacts, not status theatre or constant polling. Staff remains attributable for production.

At consequential acceptance boundaries, an independent outcome check is still needed. The exact form depends on the native outcome: a live probe, a rendered review, a policy check or another evaluator with a genuinely separate line of evidence.

What remains unknown

The experiment does not establish the best review mechanism for every domain or the latency worth paying for it. It does show why removing lead supervision to save time, or trusting supervision as its own proof, are both weak defaults.
