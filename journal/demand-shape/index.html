Demand shape changes the scaling regime.

Finding 02Elastic capacityTwo workload families

A large job is not automatically parallel work. A queue of independently closable units can be. Sales accounts, monitoring entities and support cases each create a different coordination economy from several minds coauthoring one result.

Simultaneous demand crossed a threshold

With 240 sales accounts available at once, two workers crossed the required throughput threshold twice. When the same account population arrived gradually, extra workers did not improve p90 response time.

Four workers were faster in one arm, but marginal tail quality weakened. Capacity was useful, and it was not free.

1.9×throughput with two workers at 40 independent entities
0.55×p90 latency in the same monitoring workload

Charged cost per alert was also lower. Worst-unit consistency fell by 1.5 points. Aggregate success therefore hid a meaningful edge-quality trade.

Count alone did not predict the answer. Arrival shape and local closure did.

The product decision

A lead may hold a charter, calibration standard and exception policy while a larger Staff pool closes repeated units. Each unit must be independently useful without a central model rewriting it. The Work graph should record genuine batches or territories, not mirror every item in the queue.

What remains unknown

The threshold will vary by workload, model speed, evidence burden and acceptable tail quality. The current measurements support elastic capacity for repeated independent units. They do not justify staffing every department with more agents.

Capacity still needs a mission keeper.
