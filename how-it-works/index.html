Intelligence leads. Facts keep it accountable.

Restless uses a wide, shallow organisation. Each outcome has one accountable lead. Staff produces. The owner receives a native result, not a management problem.

One chain of accountability

Owner

Mission, judgement, taste and authority.

Exec

Owns the portfolio and names one lead.

Lead

Supervises one coherent outcome without producing it.

Staff

Produces a useful result in real tools.

Exec returns to availability after dispatch. A lead frames, commissions, observes, redirects, repairs through workers and judges the exact result. One end-to-end Staff worker is the default inside a tightly coupled outcome. More workers enter only when a real seam repays the coordination cost.

Three logical layers

Constitutional Kernel

Controls identity, authority, secrets, budgets, consequential external effects and recovery. Deterministic checks live where irreversible harm can occur.

Organisational Intelligence

Coordinates goals, actors, sparse Work, Attempts, messages, memory and repair. This operational state remains recoverable and overridable.

Company Linux Runtime

A persistent company computer where agents use files, Git, browsers, tools and project software. It survives across turns and restarts.

Review the outcome

A website should arrive as a live site. A deck as rendered slides. A decision as the exact case and prepared choice.

Before review, the accountable team materialises and probes the current candidate, pre-positions the useful state, links it to the work, and states the exact judgement sought. Commits, tests, receipts and logs remain supporting evidence unless the outcome cannot reasonably be opened.

Deliberately absent

No universal command for every mutation. No immutable ledger of every thought. No governed asset custody machine. No bespoke workflow engine. No disposable sandbox for every turn. Files and mature operating tools remain the ordinary substrate until real workloads prove a stronger abstraction is needed.
