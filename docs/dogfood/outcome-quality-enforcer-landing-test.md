**Status:** complete; exact local owner review remains live and unpublished

## Capability under test

Can an accountable Restless lead turn one sparse owner mandate into a genuinely strong, complete
Restless Cloud public-site candidate by forming the right team, grounding itself in product truth,
operating native artifacts, commissioning independent criticism, and iterating until quality
converges?

This run tests the shared `outcome-quality-enforcement`, `landing-page-design`,
`reference-led-reset`, and `web-native-review` skills plus the accountable-lead system doctrine.

## What the lead did autonomously

Mara Venn formed a small accountable team with one end-to-end creative engineer, an independent
browser critic, and—only after the original critic mechanism proved too context-heavy—a separate
fresh comparative auditor. Without owner-provided layout, copy, component, team, reference-site, or
repair instructions, the lead:

1. established product truth and the rendered incumbent as the floor;
2. commissioned exactly three genuinely different browser-native directions;
3. selected Decision Room from rendered desktop/mobile evidence and rejected blending;
4. commissioned the complete site across the homepage, Product, How it works, Blog index, three
   standalone articles, legal pages, footer, mobile navigation, reduced-motion state, and 404;
5. refused to count an interactive diagnostics prompt as a passing gate;
6. commissioned independent native capture and comparative judgement;
7. changed the evaluation mechanism after two identical provider-history failures instead of
   repeating it or lowering the acceptance bar; and
8. prepared one exact live ReviewTarget only after independent acceptance.

The lead explicitly chose not to revise three non-consequential observations: the long mobile
homepage, a denser middle passage on mobile Product, and a visually ambitious Blog index for three
articles. The auditor found none impaired comprehension, navigation, trust, or task completion.

## Failure that paid rent

Observed harness/runtime failures:

- The development reconcile path reported degraded release-health/source-digest diagnostics even
  though the isolated company runtime could proceed.
- Vite's cache under `node_modules` was unwritable in the first Work worktree. A clean continuation
  isolated the cache and proved the exact retained direction commit.
- Retrying that original Work could not materialise its old declared base over the retained candidate;
  the lead correctly abandoned it and continued from the exact commit.
- The first diagnostics command displayed an install prompt instead of proving diagnostics. The lead
  caught it rather than accepting the producer's claim.
- Combining exhaustive capture and qualitative judgement exceeded the provider history limit twice.
  The lead then split native fact production from fresh-context synthesis, which completed reliably.
- Chromium initially rejected the inherited long `TMPDIR` socket path. The critic distinguished the
  environment failure from a site defect and reran successfully with `/tmp`.

The shared `outcome-quality-enforcement` skill now explicitly requires large evidence corpora to stay
as durable linked outputs, recommends separate capture and synthesis when one session would saturate,
and requires verification commands to be non-interactive and non-mutating.

## Comparison with the previous landing-page run

The earlier run reached a strong design only after extensive owner-supplied references, component
preferences, copy corrections, direction selection, and repeated visual feedback. This run reached a
complete independently accepted site from one outcome sentence. The lead supplied the missing
quality contract, team shape, product/reference hierarchy, rendered divergence, selection rationale,
native checks, critic topology, repair decisions, convergence judgement, and prepared last mile.

The remaining efficiency gap is real: the first two oversized critic Attempts were expensive, and
fresh reviewers still created avoidable package-manager files before cleaning their exact candidate.
The run therefore validates the quality-enforcer behavior while identifying evidence-context
budgeting and non-mutating verification as the next leverage points. Owner attention improved
radically; runtime/model efficiency did not improve by the same margin.
