# Landing-page playbook dogfood

## Outcome

Produce through Restless one complete local Restless Cloud public-site candidate that is demonstrably
better than the accepted incumbent at `29f4f6a68239a69fed8ccb429553a1da601b0f51`, while preserving
product truth, working routes, standalone Blog content and human publication authority.

## Capability under test

- shared `landing-page-design` skill available to every company actor;
- repository-local `restless-public-site` identity and component-registry skill;
- lead formation of a quality-complete specialist team;
- three compact live directions before full-site production;
- incumbent asset reuse without compositional lock-in;
- Astro/React/Cult UI motion and shader mechanics used with restraint;
- independent side-by-side comparative criticism; and
- exact owner-facing ReviewTarget admission through the isolated review origin.

## Control

- Repository: `restless-cloud`
- Exact base/incumbent: `29f4f6a68239a69fed8ccb429553a1da601b0f51`
- Candidate branch: `dogfood/landing-playbook-candidate`
- Product identity: current rendered Restless cockpit and its Bridge Light design sources
- Maturity reference: Neon, observed during the run; identity copying prohibited
- Component source: Cult UI repository pinned initially at
  `3b855612fb524cb042cc91b65f0cd575057471cc`

## Required evidence

1. Model, credential, shared/project skills, browser and component-source preflight.
2. Named team coverage for product, art, copy, motion engineering and independent criticism.
3. Three materially different live direction captures at desktop and mobile.
4. Lead selection record explaining why one direction beats the others and the incumbent.
5. Complete candidate routes: `/`, `/product/`, `/how-it-works/`, `/blog/`, every linked Blog post,
   legal routes and a real 404 state.
6. Candidate/incumbent captures at identical desktop/mobile viewports and reduced motion.
7. Comparative matrix for comprehension, distinctiveness, product fidelity, demonstration, copy/IA,
   craft, depth and trust.
8. Repair evidence for every consequential critic defect.
9. Exact final commit/tree, clean build, live probe and owner-openable isolated ReviewTarget.

The run fails if the lead accepts `clean`, `coherent` or `different` as a substitute for `better`, if
the site collapses to one page, or if infrastructure churn consumes the run without a substantive
rendered revision.
