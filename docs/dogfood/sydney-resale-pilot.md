## What the company should do

Restless Exec owns the sourcing objective and delegates bounded search/research work to one accountable lead and producer as appropriate. The company should maintain a private candidate queue and inventory ledger, run search and comparison work, and deliver a **prepared decision** only when an item clears the purchase screen. Routine rejected listings stay quiet. An Attention item should come from a real unresolved Work/handoff or Authority request; Restless does not offer a generic “create Attention card” shortcut.

The owner-facing purchase decision must show: exact listing and timestamp, model/configuration, seller's full-item ask, comparable *completed* sales, condition/ownership uncertainties, expected seller proceeds, all fees and pickup/return costs, 0/10/20% discount sensitivity, a fixed maximum offer, inspection checklist, cash tied up, expiry/staleness risk and what happens if the owner declines. A buying recommendation is not an offer. The owner can approve an exact seller contact or purchase step after reviewing this packet; the agent cannot infer standing purchase authority from the A$3,000 budget. Seller messages, payments, listings and sales need their own explicit authority and evidence.

Once trading is active, the company records **every** offer, rejection, accepted purchase, failed inspection, sale, refund and unsold item. Inventory is counted at cash cost with age and an explicit liquidation assumption. The operating result includes search and inspection hours, AI/tool costs, fixed costs and cash-cycle days. Separate `estimated`, `committed`, `paid`, `listed`, `sold`, `refunded` and `unsold` so an agent cannot turn a promising spread into recorded profit.

## Local Clapping Hands integration

1. **Choose the company-owned runtime boundary.** Package the existing Clapping Hands build and Python dependencies into the company environment, pin the exact source/build revision and give it a private data/profile directory under the company home. Do not bind-mount the developer checkout or reuse a personal browser profile. Probe Node, Python, Chrome, MCP handshake and tool list inside the company container.
2. **Support local stdio MCP in Restless's explicit actor launch contract.** Add a company-scoped local server descriptor (command, arguments, environment references and owning actor) that Restless validates and materialises only for that actor's next session. Keep it distinct from the current OAuth/HTTPS `connected-tool` path. Never rely on ambient `mcp.json` discovery, which Restless deliberately excludes. Do not pass session cookies or model keys through goals, logs or checked-in config. The actor's tool list must be observed after launch.
3. **Choose the browser boundary for each tool.** A fixed-origin public read may use a fresh, isolated, unauthenticated headless profile as an ordinary read-only MCP tool; it must never touch the company browser/profile, accept an arbitrary URL, or expose login, CDP or write tools. Authenticated Marketplace work must use Restless's persistent owner-visible company browser and its owner-takeover rules. For that path, verify login, session reuse, owner takeover and teardown without copied cookies; do not launch a second browser against the company profile or bypass its broker.
4. **Attach only read tools initially.** Start with listing search and detail read, with a fixed output schema and browser fallback. Tool results must include fresh timestamp, completeness, engine, latency and error/fallback information. Do not expose message, offer, purchase or account-change actions through this MCP phase.
5. **Prove the attachment live.** From a new company Staff session, call MCP `status`, perform one harmless search, compare at least two current cards against the browser, restart the actor and repeat. Mark the capability `working` only after this; an installed binary or MCP handshake alone is insufficient.

If local stdio attachment cannot be implemented promptly, use Restless's ordinary company browser for the first sourcing pilot and label the Clapping Hands arm `not connected`. Do not mislabel a CLI invoked through a shell as a per-project MCP installation.

## First six weeks, with three separate scorecards

| Outcome | Target | Evidence that counts | Failure/off-ramp |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Restless harness** | One company runs a sourcing brief to a prepared purchase decision with quiet rejects and an actual owner Attention handoff; owner can approve/decline and the same Work resumes correctly | Work/Attempt IDs, candidate packet, Attention source and action, approval/decline receipt, resumed state, owner minutes and AI spend | If a card fires for every listing, a handoff loses its prepared state, or a declined purchase reappears, repair the harness before scheduling more work |
| **Clapping Hands** | One repeated Facebook Marketplace read workflow is reliably attached as MCP; Gumtree and used eBay get feasibility probes | In-company tool-list probe, fresh listing checks, at least 30 same-task browser/tool pairs over five days, median full-path time, human review minutes, critical-field errors and fallback count | If attachment/authentication is brittle or fewer human minutes are saved than added by checking, retain browser sourcing and narrow the compiler work |
| **Resale business** | Build a documented funnel: roughly 20 fully researched candidates, several exact offer packets, then 3–5 carefully priced purchases **only after the operating prerequisites and exact owner authority are ready** | Every candidate/offer/inspection in ledger; actual sale proceeds and fees; 30/60/90-day sell-through; inventory-inclusive contribution and capital tied up | Stop a category when deals repeatedly fail inspection, cannot be sold promptly, or losses/returns erase expected margin |

The proposed business target is **avoid a capital loss while learning the actual funnel**, not A$850 per month. That A$850 was a hypothetical upper bound assuming four sales and zero charged human labour; physical pickup, inspection and fulfilment remain. Report separate business profit, agent/tool spend and owner attention. A good tool speedup does not prove a good resale business, and a profitable flip does not prove the compiler or harness works.

**Stage 3 — repeat and measure.** Run the short [second-hand marketplace pilot](/home/black/Documents/Codex/2026-09-24/clapping-hands/docs/SECONDHAND_MARKETPLACE_BENCHMARK_2026-09-25.md). Only after a successful manual run, enable a modest recurring Restless loop that looks for genuinely new candidates and stays quiet on unchanged inventory. Use the existing scheduler, not a second timer. Preserve failed runs and zero-lead sessions.

**Stage 4 — trading pilot.** Confirm the applicable dealer setup and consumer obligations for the chosen categories, then present exact buying decisions through Attention. After separately authorised offers/purchases, run the 30/60/90-day ledger review. The owner handles the physical last mile. Scale only if inventory-inclusive economics, cash velocity and owner attention remain acceptable.

This plan becomes an execution record by adding actual company IDs, model/tool probes, Work IDs, benchmark results and trading receipts as each stage completes.
