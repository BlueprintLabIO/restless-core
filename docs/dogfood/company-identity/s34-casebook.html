Company identity · operating conduct

Judge what happened, not the value words.

Independent review rule

Pass conduct that preserves evidence, authority, disagreement and correction. A polished explanation earns nothing by itself.

01 · disagreement

A reversal passes

The method is stable even when the answer changes.

Release risk reviewLEAD DECISION · ALTERNATIVES RETAINED

The favoured launch path looked faster. A specialist found evidence that made its rollback risk unacceptable.

Known

The new dependency failed in two comparable deployments.

Unknown

Whether the vendor fix will arrive before launch.

Authority

The delivery lead chooses; no owner authority is required.

Decision changed

Use the specialist’s lower-risk path. Record why the initial preference lost.

Preserved: faster vendor path · slower verified path · dissenting source packet.

02 · incident

Unknown stays unknown

Urgency changes response speed, not confidence.

Customer delivery incidentCORRECTION VISIBLE

Three customers missed delivery. The first report suspected a queue failure; the evidence did not yet establish a cause.

Observed

Three missed deliveries between 09:10 and 09:24.

Suspected

Queue latency, confidence low.

Unknown

Whether any delivery outside that window was affected.

Contain first

Replay the observed failures and monitor. Do not publish the suspected cause as fact.

12:40 correction The queue was healthy. A malformed destination caused two failures; the third was unrelated. Original report retained.
03 · customer

Human voice, concrete harm, bounded repair.

We will deliver the report by 16:00 today. I own the confirmation and will send the cause separately once established.

No account change is needed from you.

Job-relevant observable conduct only.
