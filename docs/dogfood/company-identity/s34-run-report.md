## Walking slice

One owner release carried a founding decision, observed disagreement, uncertainty and customer recovery,
a quality counterexample and a scenario-based hiring norm. Every item included the situation,
consequence, actors, authority, observed conduct and outcome, confidence, counterexample, boundary and
operational implication. The API rejected a bare “Ownership” slogan before it could become evidence.

Five Work contracts compiled role- and consequence-relevant postures for research, engineering, support,
Exec and hiring. The disagreement case changed the initially favoured decision without changing who was
accountable. The incident kept cause and scope unknown until evidence arrived, then added a correction
without deleting the original. Customer recovery coupled a human communication with a bounded action.
The quality case selected a smaller verified outcome. Hiring used job scenarios and explicitly excluded
personality, “culture fit”, sentiment and demographic proxies.

## Native casebook review

The native casebook exposes the exact situation, knowns, unknowns, authority, decision, alternatives and
correction. Browser review found five complete cases, no horizontal overflow at the narrow viewport, a
visible retained correction, a concrete 16:00 recovery action and explicit hiring-proxy exclusions. The
owner Company Identity surface passed Svelte diagnostics, type policy and production build.

## Harness behavior proved

- Culture cannot be admitted as abstract value words; evidence must name observed conduct under
  consequence plus a counterexample and boundary.
- Founding decisions require explicit owner-founded confidence. Bounded exceptions cannot excuse safety,
  discrimination, deception or authority violations.
- Work posture is immutable, role/case filtered, byte bounded and automatically injected with accounting.
- Posture explicitly cannot grant capability, override Work/Authority, suppress dissent or manufacture
  certainty; no employee score or personality profile exists.
- Exact case records preserve alternatives, unknowns and corrections. Customer recovery requires an
  operational action, not empathetic prose alone.
- Native mechanical checks differ for disagreement, incidents, customer recovery, quality and hiring.
- A producer cannot approve their own explanatory artifact. Slogan recitation blocks acceptance.
- CLI paths expose evidence, binding, posture inspection, case recording and independent review.
- The owner sees observed conduct, consequence, counterexample, boundaries, native case decisions and
  corrections—never a leaderboard, sentiment panel or person score.

## Quality judgement

S34 turns culture from a prompt monologue into governed evidence and a bounded decision posture. The
strongest proof is not stylistic consistency: it is that a reversal, an unknown, a correction and a
smaller outcome all pass when the method is credible. The remaining proof gap is the same admitted-model
gate, reported honestly rather than replaced with a synthetic success.
