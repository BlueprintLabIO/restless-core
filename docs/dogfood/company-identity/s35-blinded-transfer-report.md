# Sprint 35 blinded transfer report

**Run date:** 1 September 2026
**Frozen corpus:** `s35-integrated-corpus.json`

## Method

The evaluator received the nine Restless artifacts and three Harbour Ledger held-back examples without
candidate labels, implementation rationale or a scalar identity score. Truth, channel fitness, named
authorship, visual language and conduct were judged separately. The evaluator was also asked to name
the evidence for each judgement and to flag vocabulary or visual grammar that appeared to transfer.

The frozen review was followed by a live held-back transfer in a separate `_test` company. A fresh
producer and independent critic received Harbour Ledger's own released evidence, not the Restless
candidate or its implementation rationale. The owner then reviewed and accepted the exact prepared
outcome.

## Result

The companies are materially distinguishable for evidence-bearing reasons:

| Dimension | Restless | Harbour Ledger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product truth | Accountable company Work, exact evidence, owner decision | Offline vessel maintenance record |
| Voice | Direct action, proof and consequence | Terse, calm vessel-log language |
| Visual language | Compact operational surface; real state transitions | Spacious paper record; marine blue; no ornamental motion |
| Conduct | Narrow scope before accepting unfinished work | Stop and record uncertainty before safety-critical decisions |
| Decision posture | Retain, revise or retire an exact affected artifact | Keep equipment out of service until the missing check is named |

No Harbour Ledger example contains “accountable company work”, “owner attention”, “evidence returns
judgement”, Restless mint-glow language or motion-led status theatre. The transfer therefore passes the
frozen contamination check.

The live transfer reached the same judgement. Its first producer/critic pair failed closed after the
lead supplied a nonexistent placeholder repository. The lead replaced both nodes without repository
coordinates. The fresh critic independently accepted the corrected native gallery at SHA-256
`38c6b6ee2008dead0f544cf93ee6b7bdfa678868cf88b081b0b32a3480b9d9ae`; owner review then accepted that
exact outcome. Total transfer spend was $4.40 and no publication or external effect occurred.

## Channel judgement

The nine Restless assets do not read as one template cut to different lengths. The founder email uses
first-person observation; support answers one customer concern; the transactional message reports one
state change; the Blog supplies its own context and reasoning; product UI makes the decision primary;
and the internal memo states an operating boundary. Named authors remain credible and different.

## Correction judgement

The correction from “every company artifact” to “every accepted artifact” affects one proof line in
the frozen package. Eight unrelated assets remain outside the migration set. The original remains
historically visible and no content changes automatically. This passes concrete drift and fails any
bulk “make everything match” interpretation.

## Quality adjustment

The integrated system is stronger than the earlier manual pillar prompts because it removes four
quality hazards: duplicated context, silent generic defaults, mutable artifact inference and vague
brand-drift scores. The initial matched A/B is retained as failure evidence rather than a measured
uplift claim because its stale treatment Runtime bound Truth but omitted Voice, Visual and Culture.
After reconciliation, the all-pillar Restless package and the held-back Harbour Ledger transfer both
converged through real producer, critic and bounded replacement paths. This proves operational transfer
and contamination resistance; it does not manufacture a clean causal estimate from the invalid first
pair.
