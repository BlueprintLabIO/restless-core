Subject

Equipment

Decision owner
Accountable maintenance lead

Current record

The inspection record is incomplete. It does not contain the discharge-side pressure reading under test load. No return-to-service conclusion is recorded.

Consequence

Without that reading and its test conditions, the record does not support a safety-critical decision. The equipment remains out of service and the decision remains paused.

Stop-and-document boundary

Stop before deciding the pump is ready. Document the missing pressure reading, the conditions of the test and any uncertainty. Do not treat silence, schedule pressure or the partial record as evidence of readiness.

Authority to resume

After the missing evidence is entered, only the accountable maintenance lead may review the record and decide whether work resumes. This memo does not make that decision and does not state that maintenance is complete.
