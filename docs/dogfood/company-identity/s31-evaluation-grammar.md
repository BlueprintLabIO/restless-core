# Sprint 31 identity evaluation grammar

This corpus is frozen before identity behaviour is judged. Evaluation names the source meaning; it
does not award a synthetic identity score.

- **Fact:** may guide company output only when its provenance and owner-recognised authority are
  intact. Two active statements for one claim key are a conflict, not an invitation to choose the
  better sentence.
- **Belief:** stays attributed and uncertain even when it is useful. A compiler cannot upgrade it to
  fact by summarising it.
- **Expression guidance:** describes how the company expresses truth. It cannot change the truth.
- **Culture observation:** describes repeated behaviour with evidence. It does not prescribe an
  employee personality.
- **Example:** positive and negative artifacts are comparative evidence. Rejection is not a global
  prohibition.
- **Exception:** applies only to its named scope and lifetime. Expiry removes it from future briefs;
  historical bindings retain the release that contained it.

Native fitness is judged by whether an artifact tells the truth, fits its channel and audience, is
recognisably from this company, and survives comparison with its evidence. Conflict, missing source,
expired exception and corrected truth are discrete judgements; none may be averaged away.
