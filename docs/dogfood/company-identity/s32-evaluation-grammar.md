# Sprint 32 evaluation grammar

Reviewers see the rendered artifact, fact packet, named author, audience and channel. They do not see
candidate mode, prompt, model or producer rationale.

For every candidate, record concrete findings under factuality, channel fitness, plausible authorship,
meaning retained after compression, unsupported claims, abstraction and repetition. A fluent candidate
may lose to the plain control. An anti-pattern is a prompt for judgement, never a banned phrase.

The six channel decisions are independent. Company recognition must come from stable truth,
terminology and judgement—not identical cadence. Blog artifacts must be standalone. Support and UI
artifacts must expose state and recovery. Email review includes subject/preheader/link and narrow-client
evidence when those elements exist.
