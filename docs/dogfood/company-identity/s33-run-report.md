## Harness behavior proved

- Visual evidence is typed and atomic; motion, product rules, semantic tokens and primitives cannot omit
  required provenance.
- Work direction binds one immutable release and cannot silently change after continuation.
- Channel briefs share one grammar but differ in hierarchy, density, imagery, motion and native checks.
- Unsupported or stale primitives fail before production; the compiler never dumps the registry into
  context.
- Native review binds exact artifact digest, channel, viewport and full/reduced/static motion state.
- Failed mechanical accessibility checks block qualitative review.
- Artifact producers cannot approve their own work; revision/rejection needs a concrete finding.
- A restrained control can be compared only under the same Work, channel and motion state.
- CLI paths expose evidence, binding, brief inspection, primitive use, native capture and art-direction
  review; these are not database-only methods.
- Company Identity shows typed visual evidence, exact primitive provenance, native targets, motion state,
  control comparisons and concrete findings without becoming a design tool.

## With/without-system comparison

The live model plane still has no admitted provider: matched generation and genuinely independent blind
art direction are **provider-blocked, not passed**. No output from this Codex session was mislabelled as

non-native or mechanically failing evidence. The without-system baseline has none of those enforceable
constraints; the frozen anti-example corpus records the historical failure modes.
