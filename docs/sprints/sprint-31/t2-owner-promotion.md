# S31-T2 — Make promotion and exceptions owner-governed

**Serves:** [Sprint 31](../sprint-31.md) · Authority / Owner action

## Outcome

Reuse the authenticated owner decision path to approve, reject, narrow, supersede and explicitly except
identity proposals. Model-authored work can propose evidence but cannot make itself canonical.

## Acceptance

- Only the owner/Authority path changes the effective release.
- Promotion is atomic with its decision, release evidence and audit record.
- Rejection remains attributed evidence without becoming a permanent prohibition.
- Exceptions require scope, rationale and expiry or a deliberate indefinite owner decision.
- Revocation or supersession never mutates historical asset bindings.
- Identity decisions cannot change capability, provider, spend or effect authority.

## Makes deletable

Prompt instructions that treat repeated model wording or lead preference as company policy.
