# S31-T0 — Freeze identity evidence and evaluation grammar

**Serves:** [Sprint 31](../sprint-31.md) · Product contract / Evaluation

## Outcome

Freeze the shared corpus entries used by Sprint 31, including a verified fact packet, one belief, two
conflicting claims, approved and rejected examples, an intentional exception and a later correction.
Record current behavior before adding identity state.

## Acceptance

- Every fixture names author, source, authority, scope, time and evidence locator.
- Evaluators distinguish fact, belief, expression guidance, culture observation and exception.
- Conflict and staleness have explicit expected outcomes; fluency cannot resolve either.
- Evaluation uses concrete contradictions and native fitness, never an identity score.
- Inputs and expected judgements are immutable before T1 begins.

## Makes deletable

Remembered examples, quality-by-vibe review and retrospective fixture editing.
