# S31-T4 — Expose truth, evidence, lineage and conflicts sanely

**Serves:** [Sprint 31](../sprint-31.md) · Owner cockpit

## Outcome

Add one Company → Identity surface showing the effective release, pillar evidence, truth conflicts,
pending proposals, exceptions and release history in reader language.

## Acceptance

- Refresh and a second client show source state, not browser-local edits.
- Fact, belief, proposal, released observation and exception are visibly distinct.
- Each material statement opens its author, source and evidence locator.
- Promotion and rejection use the existing deliberate owner-action pattern.
- Empty and legacy companies explain the absence of authored identity without fake defaults.
- The surface has no numeric consistency score, giant free-form prompt or asset editor.
- Narrow and desktop layouts remain one coherent owner surface.

## Makes deletable

Hidden identity defaults and scattered settings that cannot show why guidance is authoritative.
