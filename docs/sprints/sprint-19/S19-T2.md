# S19-T2 — Reconcile every authority decision into the company

**Outcome:** Granting, declining or revoking an approval reaches the company exactly once, and a lost
announcement is repaired rather than lost.

**Layer:** Authority Plane stays the only writer of the decision; OrgIntel receives one idempotent
projection of it.

**Observed friction served:** `approval::grant` emits the durable Authority record and then sends the Exec
message on a best-effort path that only logs a warning if it fails — leaving the owner's answer recorded
and the blocked Work still blocked with nobody told. `approval::decline` and `approval::revoke` send the
company nothing at all, so a declined first contact leaves its Work waiting on an answer that already
exists. Because the announcement is an ordinary Exec message, it also inherited every delivery defect
S19-T1 repairs.

**Change:** One reconciler compares Authority approval decisions against OrgIntel announcement events and
emits any that is missing, keyed on the exact Authority record id so a repeat is a no-op. `grant`,
`decline` and `revoke` call it directly so the common case stays immediate; `scan_company` calls it so a
crash between the two writes is repaired on the next scan. Decline and revoke gain the announcement they
never had.

**Acceptance:** Each decision produces exactly one company-facing fact; running the reconciler repeatedly
adds nothing; a decision whose announcement was dropped is announced by the next scan; Authority remains
the sole writer of the decision itself.

**Deletion:** The best-effort announcement branch inside `grant`, and the silent `decline`/`revoke` paths.

**Evidence:** Focused tests for exactly-once announcement, repair after a dropped projection, and
idempotency under repeated reconciliation.
