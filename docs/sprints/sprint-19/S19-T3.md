# S19-T3 — Present the Work outcome to a reader

**Outcome:** The Work detail page reads as a document a person can scan, at desktop and mobile width,
with the exact outcome contract still available in full.

**Layer:** Owner cockpit only. It carries projections; it does not rewrite or summarise source truth.

**Observed friction served:** `work.outcome` on the live company is 1,500–4,250 characters of imperative
instructions to a model, frequently one unbroken paragraph, rendered verbatim as the first thing on the
page. The layout makes it worse: the reading column caps at `76ch` inside a
`grid-template-columns: minmax(0, 1fr) 210px` grid, so at desktop width a tall wall of text sits on the
left with several hundred pixels of dead space between it and a thin rail holding three short facts.

**Change:** The detail layout stops leaving a dead zone — the reading measure comes from the column, and
the facts rail is wide enough to be worth the space it takes. The outcome contract leads with what the
page is about and bounds its own height, with the complete exact text one disclosure away. No summarising,
no model call in the BFF, no new component library: existing tokens and the existing Svelte design system.

**Acceptance:** No dead horizontal zone between the reading column and the rail at desktop width; the
first screen is scannable rather than a wall; the full contract is reachable and unaltered; mobile is
unregressed.

**Deletion:** The `76ch`-inside-`1fr` dead zone and the unbounded raw contract block.

**Evidence:** `pnpm check`, plus a real desktop and mobile render against live company data compared with
at least one source-first reference from `docs/FRONTEND_DESIGN_REFERENCES.md`.
