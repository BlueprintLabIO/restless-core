# S19-T1 — Make owed work a durable fact, not a wake watermark

**Outcome:** Every accountable actor is woken by the exact thing it is owed, on every scan, until that
thing has been delivered into a turn that actually ran — and never again after that.

**Layer:** OrgIntel owns the delivery record; the Runtime scheduler owns re-derivation and backoff.

**Observed friction served:** A prepared outcome, an escalated judgement or a lead's message can be owed
to Exec forever without ever waking it. Exec is the only actor that can put a judgement in front of the
owner, so this is the single point of failure for owner attention. Three mechanisms produce it:

1. `handoffs_assigned_to("exec")` is compared against `latest_event_at("wake")` — the last Exec wake for
   *any* reason. One unrelated wake moves the watermark past the handoff permanently.
2. `recover_exec_conversation` filters to `from_actor == "owner"`, so a lead-to-Exec message has no
   durable recovery path; it survives only in a `NOTIFY` and an in-memory `WakeClaims::pending` entry.
3. `exec_conversation_is_owed` treats a message that arrived *during* an earlier wake as observed, and a
   `blocked_wake` — which assembles no context and runs no model — as an observation.

**Change:**

- `owner_handoffs` gains `delivered_at TIMESTAMPTZ` (migration `0016`). It is set only when a turn that
  carried the handoff in its context completes; it is cleared by `escalate_handoff`,
  `fallthrough_handoffs_to_exec`, `refresh_owner_handoff` and the brief-retraction assignment, so a
  changed or reassigned judgement is owed again.
- OrgIntel exposes the owed facts as queries: undelivered pending handoffs for an actor, and unread
  conversation mail for an actor that excludes Work-attempt inputs and self-addressed notes in SQL.
- `scan_company` re-derives owed Exec work from those two queries on every scan. Delivery is decided by
  `messages.read_at` and `owner_handoffs.delivered_at`, never by a wake timestamp.
- `dispatch_actor_conversation` uses the same rule, so a lead is woken once per undelivered judgement
  instead of on every five-second scan while one sits unresolved.
- A bounded per-company backoff suppresses the next wake after a `Blocked` or failed turn, and is cleared
  by the next successful one. This replaces the accidental silence the timestamp comparison provided.

**Acceptance:** A judgement assigned to Exec wakes it once per undelivered generation with unrelated wakes
in between; a lead-to-Exec message survives a restart with no `NOTIFY` and no in-memory queue; a blocked
wake marks nothing delivered and does not spin the scan; escalation, refresh and reassignment make a
handoff owed again; a delivered-but-unresolved judgement still appears in the assignee's context.

**Deletion:** `exec_conversation_is_owed`, `recover_exec_conversation`'s owner-only filter, the
`> latest_event_at("wake")` handoff comparison, and the two scheduler tests that assert the lossy
behaviour as intended.

**Evidence:** Focused `restlessd` and `restless-orgintel` tests covering each adversarial case above, plus
one live `_test` company probe showing an escalated judgement reaching owner Attention after an unrelated
Exec wake.
