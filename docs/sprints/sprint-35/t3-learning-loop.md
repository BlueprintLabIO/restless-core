# S35-T3 — Turn evidence into owner-governed next-release proposals

**Serves:** [Sprint 35](../sprint-35.md) · OrgIntel / Authority

## Outcome

Generalise the proven Voice edit loop across pillars: exercised outcomes and owner/customer evidence may
create attributed, scoped proposals that only owner promotion turns into a next release.

## Acceptance

- Proposal identifies triggering event, before/after evidence, pillar, scope and contradiction check.
- Generated repetition, evaluator preference and mere frequency cannot promote policy.
- Fact correction, taste change and observed culture are not collapsed into one proposal type.
- Owner may approve, reject, narrow or defer without blocking unrelated production.
- Rejection remains evidence and does not become a permanent automatic ban.
- Proposal production is bounded and event-driven; no continuous self-training worker exists.
- A promoted proposal creates one immutable successor release and exact change account.

## Makes deletable

Hidden learned preferences, model-majority canon and background constitution rewriting.
