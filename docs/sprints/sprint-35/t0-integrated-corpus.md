# S35-T0 — Freeze the multi-channel release and held-back transfer

**Serves:** [Sprint 35](../sprint-35.md) · Product contract / Evaluation

## Outcome

Freeze one verified product change and its nine-asset release package, one later truth correction and a
held-back second-company transfer case. Record pre-integration behavior and owner effort.

## Acceptance

- Every asset names audience, channel, author, native environment, truth dependency and effect boundary.
- The correction has a deterministic stale-dependency expectation.
- The second company differs materially in truth, voice, visual language and culture.
- First-pass evaluators do not see company labels, candidate mode or production rationale.
- Cross-channel truth, identity, channel fitness, authorship and culture are evaluated separately.
- Inputs are immutable before T1 and no real publication or customer contact is authorised.

## Makes deletable

Integration cases selected after implementation and transfer tests that differ only by logo or colour.
