# S35-T4 — Identify concrete drift and make migration an explicit decision

**Serves:** [Sprint 35](../sprint-35.md) · Runtime / Owner judgement

## Outcome

When truth or identity changes, compute evidence-backed affected artifacts and present bounded retain,
revise or retire decisions without mutating content automatically.

## Acceptance

- Drift names artifact/version, old and new evidence, dependency and consequence.
- Truth staleness, voice difference, visual difference and culture difference remain distinct.
- Historically correct archival work may remain without being labelled defective.
- No asset is edited, published, removed or externally changed without existing authority and Work.
- One owner decision can govern a genuinely coherent migration set without hiding exceptions.
- Unknown dependencies stay unknown and do not become a clean bill of health.
- No scalar drift percentage or green consistency badge is emitted.

## Makes deletable

Bulk “make everything on-brand” jobs and asset-age heuristics masquerading as drift analysis.
