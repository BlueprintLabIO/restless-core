# S35-T1 — Compile accepted pillars without flattening their semantics

**Serves:** [Sprint 35](../sprint-35.md) · Runtime / Company Identity

## Outcome

Compile Company Truth plus whichever Voice, Visual and Culture pillars passed their native sprints into
one bounded, inspectable Constitution Brief for the exact outcome and channel.

## Acceptance

- Pillars retain distinct provenance, authority, conflicts and omission accounts.
- Failed or unavailable pillars are named rather than replaced with generated defaults.
- Retrieval is deterministic and bounded by relevance and bytes.
- Truth conflicts block dependent expression without disabling unrelated work.
- Channel, author and culture context cannot widen capability or effects.
- Brief digest and release identity survive restart and handoff.
- No generic preference map, universal consistency score or whole-identity prompt blob appears.

## Makes deletable

Manually concatenated brand/culture prompts and pillar-specific context assembly duplicated by teams.
