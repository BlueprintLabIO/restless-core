# S35-T5 — Give the owner one quiet, source-backed Identity surface

**Serves:** [Sprint 35](../sprint-35.md) · Owner cockpit

## Outcome

Complete Company → Identity with current release, four pillars, evidence, proposals, exceptions, affected
artifacts and exact decisions while keeping routine production out of Attention.

## Acceptance

- First view answers what is current, what materially changed and whether owner judgement is needed.
- Every claim and drift item links to source and exact artifact evidence.
- Proposed learning is visually distinct from effective identity.
- One owner action may approve, reject, narrow or select a migration disposition.
- Ordinary asset compliance and low-confidence observations do not create attention spam.
- The surface remains coherent at narrow and desktop widths and is keyboard/screen-reader operable.
- It contains no free-form constitution prompt, consistency score, CMS or design editor.

## Makes deletable

Pillar-specific settings pages, decorative identity dashboards and tactical migration menus.
