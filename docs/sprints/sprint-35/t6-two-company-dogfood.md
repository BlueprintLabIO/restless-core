Use Restless to produce, operate, independently review and repair the nine-asset release package, then
run the held-back transfer case in a second `_test` company from the same implementation.

- The first company has no surviving cross-channel factual contradiction.
- Reviewers recognise company and channel for concrete truth, voice, visual and conduct evidence.
- Named authors remain credible and different.
- Visual artifacts pass product fidelity, responsive, accessibility and motion requirements.
- The correction produces an exact stale inventory and bounded revision decision.
- The second company remains materially distinct; no Restless vocabulary, graphics or culture leaks.
- Owner interactions, context bytes, reversals, attributable/unattributed spend and native evidence are
  retained honestly.
- No publication, deployment, outreach or customer effect occurs without separate authority.

## Closing artifact
