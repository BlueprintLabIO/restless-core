# S29-T0 — Freeze the standard, corpus and evaluation grammar

**Layer:** Product contract + Evaluation.

**Serves:** Sprint 29 criteria 3–8 and 11–14.

**Observed friction:** “Exceptional” is compelling but unfalsifiable unless different outcomes,
quality floors, evidence and stopping judgements are frozen before implementation.

**Makes deletable:** Remembered examples, screenshot preference and quality-by-vibe acceptance.

## Outcome

A versioned corpus and evaluation grammar define what the four standards promise, what never varies
by mode and how independent evidence can distinguish adaptive ambition from additional ceremony.

## Scope

- Capture every case in the sprint's frozen acceptance corpus and the current behaviour for each.
- Freeze the four enum values and their owner-facing promises; reject synonyms in persisted state.
- Define mode-independent floors for safety, truth, authority, privacy, accessibility, reversibility
  and exact source acceptance.
- For each outcome shape, state what fitness, ground truth, native proof, consequential challenge and
  likely stopping evidence mean without prescribing a team topology.
- Record the prior quality-enforcer dogfood as evidence: outcome quality, elapsed time, tracked token
  use, context failures, owner interactions and the lead adaptations that changed the result.
- Hold back one transfer case from implementers until the final behavioural evaluation.

## Acceptance

- A reviewer can distinguish a stronger outcome from a more elaborate process using the grammar.
- At least one Exceptional fixture should rationally use one actor and one pass.
- At least one consequential fixture requires independent challenge and recovery/native evidence.
- The corpus contains an explicit limit conflict and defines the honest expected escalation.
- Evaluator prompts do not expose the selected mode before their first judgement.
- No aggregate quality score, word count, actor count, loop count or token threshold is pass/fail.
- Founders approve the corpus and standard language before T1 or T2 freezes storage.

## Non-goals

- selecting final UI styling;
- claiming the four standards cover every future preference;
- inventing a universal quality rubric; or
- spending against real providers or causing real external effects during corpus capture.
