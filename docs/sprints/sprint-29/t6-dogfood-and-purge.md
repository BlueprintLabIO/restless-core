# S29-T6 — Prove adaptive exceptional work and purge

**Layer:** Full slice + Evaluation.

**Serves:** Every Sprint 29 criterion.

**Depends on:** S29-T0–T5.

**Observed friction:** One excellent landing-page run proves that intensive guidance can work; it does
not prove a general inherited standard, efficient adaptation or honest owner control.

**Makes deletable:** Losing policy/storage branches, prompt-only claims, unexercised compatibility
paths and mode-specific orchestration tables.

## Outcome

One `_test` company runs the frozen heterogeneous corpus with `Exceptional` as its inherited default,
plus explicit Fast and Frontier exercises. Independent reviewers judge native outcomes without being
told the selected mode first. The run proves persistence, adaptive intensity, honest limits and rare
owner interruption, then deletes losing machinery.

## Run

1. Record the pre-sprint baseline and company policy, then set the company default once.
2. Commission the trivial correction, migration, research and creative outcomes without restating
   quality process in owner prose.
3. Continue one outcome through feedback and verify it retains its commissioned standard after a
   company-default change and daemon restart.
4. Run a comparable bounded case under explicit Fast and Frontier overrides.
5. Exercise one infeasible or ask-before-crossing limit without crossing the hard company ceiling.
6. Capture charter, team topology, native evidence, elapsed time, attributable/unattributed spend,
   context failures, revision decisions and owner interruptions.
7. Give native outcomes and acceptance contracts to blinded independent evaluators before revealing
   the mode or implementation history.
8. Reuse one genuinely earned quality asset in a later case and record whether it reduced work or
   improved the outcome.
9. Delete rejected stores, duplicate mode maps, quality scores, fixed recipes and compatibility paths.

## Acceptance

- All fourteen sprint criteria pass on recorded evidence.
- Exceptional remains direct for the trivial case and materially more searching where consequence
  warrants it.
- Fast passes every applicable invariant and exact native acceptance criterion.
- Frontier demonstrates broader uncertainty-seeking behaviour without claiming endless work or
  guaranteed novelty.
- Blinded evaluators can point to outcome evidence—not team size or polish theatre—to explain their
  judgements.
- Every owner interruption is necessary under the sprint's four allowed reasons; unnecessary
  implementation-choice escalation fails the run.
- Spend reporting reconciles attributable, unattributed, estimated/advisory and hard-ceiling values.
- No external publication, deployment, outreach, payment or provider connection occurs unless
  separately authorised through existing source-owned controls.

## Closing artifacts

- frozen corpus and baseline;
- generated standard/config/input contracts;
- inheritance and persistence trace;
- mode-specific charters and lead decisions;
- native evidence and blinded evaluator reports;
- time/spend/context-failure account;
- escalation trace;
- quality-capital reuse trace; and
- deletion audit with residual uncertainty.
