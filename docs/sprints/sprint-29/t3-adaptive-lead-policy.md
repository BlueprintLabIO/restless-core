# S29-T3 — Make the Exec and lead operationalise ambition

**Layer:** Exec + accountable lead + Runtime context.

**Serves:** Sprint 29 criteria 3–7, 10–13.

**Depends on:** S29-T0 and S29-T2.

**Observed friction:** The quality-enforcer lead found a strong process only after costly retries and
context failures; the current doctrine has no durable ambition contract to operationalise.

**Makes deletable:** Fixed team recipes, actor/critic quotas and scattered prompt fragments that map
modes to mechanics.

## Outcome

The Exec commissions against the effective standard. The lead turns it into a concise,
outcome-specific quality contract and adapts team shape, exploration, independent challenge, native
evidence and revision intensity until it can justify returning, continuing or escalating.

## Scope

- Add the effective standard, source and honest limits to the Exec and lead's authoritative context.
- Require the commissioned charter to define outcome fitness, strongest available references,
  material alternatives, native proof, evaluation independence where consequential and stopping or
  reset rationale.
- Preserve the lead's authority to choose one actor or several, reuse or clean-room reset, direct
  evaluation or an independent critic according to outcome consequence.
- Separate bulky native evidence capture from comparative judgement when combining them would exceed
  useful context, incorporating the observed dogfood failure.
- Require root-cause revision when an evaluator finds a systemic gap; do not reward cosmetic patch
  counts.
- Return earned references, rubrics, primitives or observations to existing company memory/skill
  seams only when they are reusable and source-backed.

## Acceptance

- The heterogeneous corpus produces materially different quality contracts under one Exceptional
  default.
- The trivial case adds no critic/team ceremony beyond exact native verification.
- The consequential cases make creation/evaluation independence explicit where it changes confidence.
- No code maps a standard to a fixed model, reasoning effort, agent count, loop count, reference
  count, token budget or tool list.
- A lead can justify a clean-room reset or stopping decision with observed evidence.
- Context-overflow fixtures produce bounded decomposition, not repeated identical critic failures.
- The standard cannot grant capabilities, spend or external authority.

## Non-goals

- one universal “S-tier” prompt;
- a deterministic orchestration graph for each mode;
- requiring a registry contribution from trivial work; or
- accepting lead self-report as native outcome proof.
