# S29-T5 — Project the live frontier and escalate only real boundaries

**Layer:** OrgIntel + owner projection + Attention.

**Serves:** Sprint 29 criteria 8–10 and 13–14.

**Depends on:** S29-T2–T4.

**Observed friction:** The owner cannot tell whether a long run is productively converging, repeating
itself or blocked by a real constraint without asking the lead for process narration.

**Makes deletable:** Process spam, fake completion/quality scores and owner menus of tactical choices.

## Outcome

An active outcome exposes a compact, attributed frontier account: effective standard, current phase,
observed strengths, consequential gaps, next-loop rationale, honest time/spend state and any real
boundary. Attention is raised only when owner judgement is necessary.

## Scope

- Reuse existing Work/team/attempt/spend/Attention projections; add only distinctions the frozen
  corpus cannot express.
- Freeze a small phase vocabulary such as framing, exploring, building, evaluating, converging and
  awaiting-boundary only if each phase is source-derived and useful to the corpus.
- Keep strengths, gaps and expected improvement as attributed lead judgement linked to evidence.
- Show elapsed time and spend only from observable sources, including unavailable/unattributed state.
- Emit an owner handoff for infeasibility, ask-before-crossing, authority or irreducible taste; require
  a recommendation and consequence of waiting.
- Treat diminishing returns as a lead recommendation to return or stop, not an automatic numeric gate.
- Keep routine progress in Work; do not flood Attention or conversation.

## Acceptance

- The owner can explain why each corpus outcome continues, returns or escalates from the first view.
- No numeric quality score, percent complete or unsupported ETA is generated.
- Every factual usage/limit claim maps to Runtime or Authority evidence; every quality judgement is
  visibly attributed.
- An ask-before-crossing handoff does not itself raise the hard company ceiling.
- The infeasible fixture produces one decision-ready owner interruption, not repeated blocker spam.
- No tactical implementation choice is sent to the owner when the accountable lead can decide it.
- Attention, Work and conversation agree on the current boundary and next owner.

## Branch and purge

Compare a projection over current Work/evidence with a new quality-run state machine. The expected
prior is projection. If the corpus does not earn a durable phase vocabulary, omit it rather than
persisting model-authored lifecycle state.
