# Sprint 44 T0 — Freeze the comparative corpus and claim thresholds

**Layer:** Product proof

**Serves:** A fair comparison between direct native harness use, Restless-managed work, and hybrid takeover.

## Work

- Freeze the three comparison arms before counted runs begin:
  - direct native harness;
  - Restless managed autonomy;
  - Restless managed autonomy with native takeover and return.
- Record the exact harness builds, models, authentication mode, billing assumptions, runtime image, and available native features for every arm.
- Choose a representative company-outcome corpus that includes hard implementation, investigation, recovery, and owner-decision work.
- Define the scoring rubric, blind-review method, invalid-run rules, and minimum thresholds for each proposed product claim.
- Separate outcome quality from owner attention, elapsed time, cost, reliability, and capability coverage.

## Acceptance

- [ ] The corpus and comparison arms are versioned and immutable for counted runs.
- [ ] Every outcome has observable success criteria and an evidence packet schema.
- [ ] Reviewers can judge outcome quality without knowing which arm produced it.
- [ ] Claim thresholds are written before results are available.
- [ ] Invalid, interrupted, and retried runs have explicit treatment.

## Makes deletable

- Anecdotal superiority claims based on one successful demo.
- Comparisons that silently change models, tools, inputs, or judging criteria between arms.
