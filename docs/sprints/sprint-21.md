## Why this sprint exists

Sprint 20 can close a finite publication backlog. EXP-09 can show in isolation whether a standing
mission reacts to signals without owner relays or idle churn. Neither proves the product promise that
a real company continues producing useful public and economic artifacts over time.

Dogfood 5 therefore operates the accepted Restless research publication through several real cycles.
The company must notice material change, decide whether work is warranted, produce and peer-review the
exact outcome, exercise only pre-granted authority, preserve rollback and return to idle. The owner
sets the mandate and authority once rather than prompting each cycle.

This is a whole-company acceptance run, not a matched causal experiment. A failure diagnoses the next
product or operating gap; it does not prove a universal law about continuous agents.

## Activation rule

Sprint 21 does not start merely because this specification exists.

- If EXP-09 concludes `current primitives suffice`, use the existing Goal/responsibility,
  event/schedule, Work/Attempt and file paths unchanged.
- If EXP-09 earns one thin affordance, Sprint 21 depends on a separately founder-approved minimal
  implementation sprint and its live proof.
- If EXP-09 is rejected, product-invalid or inconclusive, revise or cancel Sprint 21 rather than
  building the imagined continuous system.

No `Mission` table, workflow engine, polling service or permanent sampling loop is pre-authorised.

## Outcome

Restless operates its public research site across three materially different real change cycles with:

- one standing editorial mandate;
- no per-cycle owner directive or agent chasing;
- rigorous writer, independent peer-review and lead-acceptance boundaries;
- bounded, reversible publication authority;
- at least one correct no-op when no material improvement exists;
- a healthy, coherent public site after every accepted change; and
- truthful evidence of public output and any observed external usage, without claiming revenue or
  adoption that did not occur.

The final ReviewTarget is the live public research site and its exact change history. Internal Work,
commits, reviews, receipts and rollback notes are supporting evidence.

## Standing organisation

```text
owner
  -> continuously available Exec
      -> Research Publication lead - durable mission keeper and non-producing supervisor
          -> article/site Staff producer commissioned for each useful cycle
          -> fresh independent peer reviewer for each consequential publication
```

The lead does not remain busy to remain present. It wakes for material source/artifact events,
scheduled opportunity checks and Staff outcomes, then returns to idle. Each publication or update is
ordinary bounded Work. The lead may retain a proven writer across cycles for context/style continuity
or commission a fresh worker when independence, saturation or capability requires it.

If EXP-08 promotes a web-production playbook, it is available as a revisable prior. The lead's
appropriate use, adaptation or rejection becomes reuse evidence; adherence is not a goal.

## Real change cycles

Freeze the exact source identities and observation boundary at entry. The run must include:

1. **New evidence.** A real completed Restless experiment or dogfood result warrants a new article or
   a substantive extension to an existing one.
2. **Correction or changed belief.** New evidence narrows, reverses or materially qualifies an
   existing public claim. Stale copy cannot remain accepted merely because it is already deployed.
3. **External/source change or no-op.** A dated source-first change affects a comparison or research
   page, or the lead correctly concludes that the observed change does not warrant publication.

An observation window may bound evaluation resources, but elapsed time never decides semantic
completion. If the required real changes do not occur naturally, use already-real repo/provider
events not yet processed by this company. Do not manufacture market facts and call them real.

## Standing authority

Before the run, the owner may grant one narrow, revocable and expiring authority envelope for the
exact research site and deployment path. The company operating mandate permits the accountable lead
to exercise that grant only when:

- the source and claim map exists;
- the fresh peer reviewer and publication lead have accepted the exact commit;
- deterministic build, route, accessibility and live-probe gates pass;
- the change touches only the named site and contains no secret, personal data, pricing promise,
  legal commitment or unapproved external representation;
- the rollback point is recorded; and
- the effect broker has an idempotency key and authoritative receipt.

Anything outside that envelope comes to the owner as a prepared bounded decision. Unknown deployment
outcomes are reconciled and never blindly repeated. The owner can revoke authority or stop the
company at any time.

The Authority Plane does not interpret article quality, peer-review prose or OrgIntel state. It
enforces only its deterministic boundary: exact capability and target scope, expiry, budget,
idempotency, execution and receipts. The publication lead owns the editorial preconditions;
evaluation audits whether the company obeyed them.

## Success contract

### Continuous usefulness

1. **Three closed cycles.** Each real change reaches accepted publication, explicit defer/reject or a
   justified no-op with exact source and responsibility evidence.
2. **No per-cycle prompting.** After mandate and authority setup, no owner message is required to tell
   the company to notice, research, draft, review, repair, deploy or stop ordinary in-envelope work.
3. **Correct idle behaviour.** A quiet/no-material-value period produces no article, cosmetic churn,
   heartbeat artifact, duplicate Work or model activity whose purpose is merely to appear autonomous.
4. **Learning across cycles.** At least one prior article, review finding or playbook lesson changes a
   later decision appropriately. Mechanical repetition counts against the run.

### Quality and truth

5. **Editorial gates persist.** Every consequential public change has one accountable writer, a
   fresh peer review against exact sources and rendered output, and publication-lead acceptance.
6. **Correction works.** A changed belief updates or retracts the exact affected public claim and its
   related pages without silently rewriting historical experimental evidence.
7. **Native quality holds.** The public site remains coherent, accessible and live across desktop and
   mobile after every effect. Design quality does not decay into accumulated components or inconsistent
   page patterns.

### Authority and operation

8. **Governed autonomy.** Every public effect is inside the standing grant or separately approved,
   attributable, idempotent and receipted. No unknown outcome is repeated.
9. **Exec remains available.** Editorial cycles do not occupy Exec as a watcher, status relay or
   integrator. An unrelated bounded owner request can be durably dispatched while a cycle is active.
10. **Local recovery.** One controlled actor/process interruption after useful work exists is repaired
    from durable responsibility and files without duplicated publication or owner debugging.
11. **Prepared owner surface.** Only out-of-envelope strategy, taste, authority and final sprint
    judgement reach the owner. Ordinary coordination stays below the lead.

### Economic evidence

12. **Public asset output.** Accepted pages are externally reachable and useful as durable research,
    comparison or product-education assets. Reachability is evidence of output, not evidence of demand.
13. **Honest external signals.** Real page use, referral, subscriber, enquiry or search evidence is
    recorded when available. Missing analytics remains unknown, never zero. No revenue, conversion or
    adoption claim is inferred from publication alone.
14. **Activation-only opportunity extension.** After the publication mission passes all prior gates,
    one separate opportunity-intelligence lead may process a real public or inbound signal into a
    locally complete, evidence-backed opportunity brief and prepared next action without owner
    prompting. Nothing is contacted or represented externally without its own authority. Failure here
    does not erase the publication result.

## Observation covenant

Founders observe rather than coach ordinary cycles.

- Do not ask for progress because the company is quiet.
- Do not suggest an article, fix prose, move commits or repair Work state during the run.
- Record every owner intervention and whether it was mandate, taste, authority, external-human action,
  agent chasing, unsupported-claim correction or harness repair.
- Let a missed signal, false-positive article, review rejection or failed deployment become evidence
  unless authority, spend or data integrity requires a stop.

## Evidence package

1. standing mandate, expiry, source boundary, model/spend envelope and authority grant;
2. exact real signals and their causal delivery to responsibility;
3. per-cycle Work/Attempt, artifact, review, acceptance and effect receipts;
4. public URLs, commits, live probes and rollback points after each accepted cycle;
5. quiet-period process/model/Work evidence;
6. Exec availability and unrelated-request dispatch evidence;
7. owner attention, elapsed/active time, spend, repair, duplicate and rejected-work accounting;
8. public usage/economic observations with unknowns preserved; and
9. concise after-action separating content, model/provider, runtime, OrgIntel and authority friction.

## Risks and dispositions

| Risk | Disposition | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standing authority publishes harmful or false material | **Invariant at effect boundary** | Narrow expiring grant, exact claim/review/build predicates, idempotent effect and owner stop |
| Agents optimise for visible activity | **Guarded** | Explicit no-op success, quiet interval and idle-cost evidence |
| Repeated updates degrade the site's identity | **Guarded** | Native whole-site review, accepted design system and deletion/simplification pass each cycle |
| The company overfits to its own research | **Guarded** | External/source-change cycle, peer review and honest counterevidence |
| Three cycles are called proof of an autonomous business | **Guarded** | Scope the claim to one continuous research operation; economic extension remains activation-only |
| Real demand is absent | **Accepted** | Publication output can succeed without demand; report external response honestly and choose the next market test |
| A known callback/review bug causes owner rescue | **Pending fix at entry** | Must pass the EXP-09 activation gate or make the run product-invalid; founders do not patch it mid-run |

## Non-goals

- a general autonomous-improvement engine or permanent background sampling service;
- automatic strategy changes, legal claims, pricing promises or unbounded public representation;
- a universal mission/event schema or per-domain workflow;
- claiming that content publication equals revenue or product-market fit;
- using synthetic readers, prospects or analytics as real economic evidence;
- keeping actors hot when no valuable work exists; and
- expanding into sales, marketing, support and operations simultaneously.

## Entry, stop and exit gates

**Entry:** EXP-09 supplies an advancing disposition; founders approve the exact site, standing
mandate, real source identities, GPT-5.6 Sol envelope, spend ceiling, narrow expiring authority grant,
observation covenant and rollback boundary.

**Stop:** stop for authority escape, source/privacy uncertainty, uncontrolled external
representation, duplicate or ambiguous public effect that cannot be reconciled, spend breach, branch
or data corruption risk, repeated harmful false publication or founder stop. Ordinary review
rejection and correct no-op are evidence, not stops.

**Exit:** three cycles and the quiet-period test have terminal evidence; public outputs and receipts
are inspectable; owner attention and external response are truthfully reported; and the run ends
`accepted`, `rejected`, `inconclusive`, `product-invalid` or `effect-ambiguous`. Only a fully accepted
native operation justifies calling continuous research publication ready.
