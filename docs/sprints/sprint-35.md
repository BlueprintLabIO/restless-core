# Sprint 35 — Run the executable Company Constitution

**Status:** Complete — implementation, two-company dogfood, architecture promotion and purge verified

**Date:** 31 August 2026

**Programme:** [Company Identity](./company-identity-programme.md)

**Depends on:** Accepted Sprint 31 plus whichever of Sprints 32–34 pass their native transfer tests.
Failed pillars remain excluded rather than being integrated by schedule.

## Why this sprint exists

Separate truth, voice, visual and culture capabilities can still drift if every team assembles them
manually. The company needs one bounded constitution compiler, a safe learning loop and release-aware
drift account. Integration must preserve the distinct authority and evidence semantics of each pillar
rather than flattening them into a universal score or prompt.

## Outcome

Restless takes one verified product change through a coherent multi-channel release package:

- product UI copy and state;
- landing-page section with product proof;
- founder email;
- newsletter edition;
- support and transactional messages;
- standalone Blog update;
- social copy and visual;
- internal decision memo; and
- one incident correction that changes a previously stated fact.

Every artifact binds to one Company Identity release, fits its channel and named author, passes native
truth/voice/visual/culture review where applicable and remains inspectably connected after restart.
A second `_test` company runs a held-back transfer case and must remain recognisably different.

## Integrated contract

1. One release identity binds the effective pillars, without merging their evidence into an opaque blob.
2. Runtime retrieves only relevant evidence and records what was included, omitted and unavailable.
3. The accountable lead owns channel selection, authorship, independent review and convergence; no fixed
   team or review topology is encoded.
4. Every artifact records exact identity release, channel contract, author, truth dependencies and
   native evidence identity.
5. Owner edits produce attributed proposals. Generated artifacts, evaluator taste and repeated model
   phrasing cannot promote themselves.
6. A new release can identify stale truth, voice, visual or culture dependencies without rewriting or
   bulk-mutating old artifacts.
7. Migration is a decision: retain historically correct work, revise a bounded asset or explicitly
   retire it. “Make everything match” is not an automatic effect.
8. Drift is concrete and pillar-specific. No company-consistency score or traffic-light badge substitutes
   for the contradiction and its evidence.
9. The owner sees current release, material proposals, consequential drift and required judgements from
   one Identity surface; ordinary asset production does not create Attention spam.
10. Company Identity cannot approve publication, outreach, customer contact, deployment, payment or any
    other effect outside existing source-owned controls.

## Safe learning loop

```text
owner/customer evidence or exercised outcome
→ attributed observation
→ scoped proposal with positive and negative evidence
→ independent contradiction/drift check
→ owner approval, rejection or narrowing
→ immutable next identity release
```

No background model continuously rewrites the constitution. Proposal generation is event-driven,
bounded and inspectable. Rejection is retained as evidence without becoming a permanent prohibition.

## Slice per layer

**Authority.** Own successor release promotion, explicit exceptions and migration decisions. Constitution
state cannot authorise publication, customer contact or another external effect.

**OrgIntel.** Preserve pillar semantics, release lineage, proposals, artifact bindings, truth dependencies
and concrete drift using the existing authored-record and artifact boundaries.

**Runtime.** Compile bounded briefs, operate native renderers, resolve evidence and compute affected
dependencies. It does not mutate assets or continuously retrain identity.

**Exec / accountable lead.** Assemble outcome-specific authors, producers and critics, then converge on
the accepted multi-channel result without a fixed constitution workflow.

**Cockpit.** Present one quiet Identity surface for current state and consequential judgement, not a CMS,
design suite, employee system or generic policy console.

## Salvage

Only the S31–S34 pillars that pass their frozen native and transfer acceptance may be integrated. A
failed pillar is evidence, not schedule pressure to salvage its implementation.

## Programme acceptance

- The release package contains no cross-channel factual contradictions.
- Blinded reviewers identify the correct company and channel for evidence-bearing reasons while named
  authors remain distinguishable.
- Visual targets remain product-grounded, responsive, accessible and motion-safe.
- Exercised decisions and corrections match promoted cultural methods without quoting culture slogans.
- A changed fact produces a bounded, correct drift inventory and no automatic external mutation.
- The owner resolves only consequential identity promotion or migration judgement, not routine tactics.
- Context remains bounded and no model context-limit failure is caused by identity retrieval.
- The second company does not inherit Restless vocabulary, visual grammar or culture accidentally.
- The closing audit finds one identity source, existing Work/Attempt/effect lifecycles and no generic
  policy engine, CMS, employee score, quality score or self-training prompt loop.

## Terminal decision

- **Pass:** both companies satisfy the integrated acceptance corpus and the proven contracts are promoted
  into target architecture.
- **Revise once:** repair one bounded compiler, drift, projection or native-rendering defect and rerun the
  affected slice plus transfer case.
- **Stop negative:** preserve each independently successful pillar but reject the integrated constitution
  if it increases context failure, homogenises companies or creates an unauditable source of policy.
