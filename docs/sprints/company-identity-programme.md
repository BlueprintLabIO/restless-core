## Thesis

Restless should not merely produce individually competent assets. It should help a company remain a
recognisable institution across time: factually coherent, verbally human, visually distinctive and
behaviourally consistent, while preserving the real voices of the people inside it.

The programme introduces one source-owned **Company Identity** with four related pillars:

1. **Company truth** — verified product, audience, positioning, vocabulary, beliefs, limits and claims.
2. **Voice** — approved language evidence, expression principles, channel dialects and named authorship.
3. **Visual language** — tokens, composition, motion, imagery, product representation and native proof.
4. **Culture** — observed decision, disagreement, uncertainty, correction and customer-treatment norms.

The identity has an invariant core and context-specific expression. A founder newsletter, support
reply, product error and research report must not sound identical; they must remain recognisably from
the same company. Identity constrains truth and character, not every sentence or individual.

## The 10× outcome

The final capability is an executable Company Constitution. For each outcome, Runtime compiles only
the relevant, bounded evidence into an Identity Brief:

```text
current company truth
+ relevant voice and visual evidence
+ observable culture expectations
+ channel, audience, named author and outcome contract
= bounded production and independent review context
```

It applies to public sites, newsletters, founder and sales email, support and transactional messages,
product UI, Blogs, research, decision memos, incidents, hiring material and Staff conduct. It does not
become a generic preference engine, a personality prompt, a mandatory approval ceremony or a second
Work lifecycle.

## Programme invariants

1. **Truth precedes expression.** Consistent unsupported claims are still wrong.
2. **One source, versioned releases.** Browser state, prompt fragments and asset-local style guides do
   not become competing identity authorities.
3. **Generated work cannot canonise itself.** Only owner-approved or otherwise source-authorised
   evidence enters a released identity.
4. **Evidence over adjectives.** Positive examples, rejected examples, decisions and observed conduct
   outrank labels such as “bold”, “innovative” or “customer obsessed”.
5. **Invariant core, variable surface.** Channel and named-author expression may vary without silently
   changing company truth or culture.
6. **Humans remain visible.** Company voice never requires every person to impersonate one synthetic
   narrator. A named human should be able to sign what they publish.
7. **Bounded retrieval.** Staff receive the smallest relevant slice and a few strong examples, not a
   growing brand-book prompt or whole-company transcript.
8. **Independent challenge where consequential.** Creation and identity review are separate when a
   false claim, public asset, high-consequence communication or durable product pattern warrants it.
9. **Native proof.** Emails are rendered as emails, UI in the product, sites in browsers, documents in
   their reader and behavioural norms in exercised decisions.
10. **No identity score.** Drift is reported as specific contradiction, unsupported claim, channel
    mismatch or evidence-backed difference—not a laundering scalar.
11. **Stable, not frozen.** Change occurs through attributed proposals and immutable releases; a
    rebrand or corrected truth never silently rewrites the historical contract of old assets.
12. **Exceptions are explicit.** A campaign or legal constraint may narrow expression for a bounded
    scope and time, but cannot fork an undocumented shadow identity.

## Source and layer boundary

- **Owner / Authority:** approves releases, consequential truth changes, canonical examples and
  deliberate exceptions. Identity cannot widen capability, spend or external-effect authority.
- **OrgIntel:** retains attributed observations, proposals, decisions, releases, evidence locators and
  the identity version used by an outcome. It does not decide taste.
- **Runtime:** resolves live resources, compiles a bounded Identity Brief, supplies native renderers and
  captures exact evidence. It does not infer a new canonical identity from model output.
- **Exec and accountable lead:** select the relevant channel/author contract and commission creation,
  independent review and repair in proportion to consequence.
- **Cockpit:** makes current truth, releases, examples, proposed learning, exceptions and concrete
  drift inspectable without becoming a design tool or content-management system.

## Shared frozen acceptance corpus

The programme retains one cross-sprint corpus with a verified fact packet and deliberately conflicting
inputs. Every item names its audience, author, channel, native environment and consequence:

- a product-navigation label and destructive-action confirmation;
- a transactional failure email;
- a support reply after a customer-visible defect;
- a short personal sales email;
- a founder-signed product announcement;
- one complete newsletter edition containing raw observations and a pointed conclusion;
- a standalone Blog article;
- a landing-page section with adjacent product proof;
- a social announcement and matching visual;
- an internal decision memo with dissent;
- an incident update containing uncertainty and correction;
- a hiring brief that must express culture through expected conduct;
- one campaign intentionally requesting a voice or visual departure; and
- one changed product truth that makes a previously approved claim stale.

At least one held-back transfer case comes from a second `_test` company with a materially different
identity. Evaluators see the artifact, truth packet, channel and audience, but not the selected identity
label before first judgement.

## Programme measures

- factual contradictions and unsupported claims;
- owner edits required before an artifact is usable;
- whether a blinded reader recognises the correct company for evidence-bearing reasons;
- channel fitness without identity loss;
- named-author believability and signability;
- words and concepts removed without meaning loss;
- visual/product-language deviations found in native review;
- culture claims supported by observed decisions rather than slogans;
- stale assets correctly identified after a truth or identity release;
- relevant context bytes retrieved per outcome and context-limit failures;
- proposed learnings approved, rejected or narrowed; and
- whether a later outcome actually reuses a previously earned observation.

Word count, aesthetic novelty, number of rules, number of examples and similarity to one canonical
artifact are diagnostic only. None is a quality gate.

## Sequence and stop conditions

- **Sprint 31** proves the canonical identity release and bounded compiler with company truth first.
- **Sprint 32** proves human, channel-specific written expression and an independent copy desk.
- **Sprint 33** proves a reusable visual language through native assets and motion/accessibility review.
- **Sprint 34** proves culture as observable operating conduct rather than brand prose.
- **Sprint 35** integrates all pillars, learns only through approved proposals, manages drift across
  releases and exercises the constitution across two companies.

Each sprint may stop negative without invalidating the earlier ones. If a pillar cannot improve its
native corpus without prompt growth, homogenised expression or owner coordination tax, preserve the
evidence and do not promote that pillar into the integrated constitution.
