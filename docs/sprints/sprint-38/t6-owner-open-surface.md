# S38-T6 — Give the owner one honest Open surface

**Layer:** Owner UI

**Serves:** Stable infrastructure is not actual usability until a person can find and use the artifact
from the product.

## Work

- Add one Open action to existing artifact/review surfaces rather than an application-store dashboard.
- Embed only explicitly embeddable owned HTTPS artifacts; launch native clients through the broker;
  route streamed fallbacks to the existing Company Computer.
- Show Ready, Preparing, Mac must remain awake, Requires always-on runner, Unavailable, Expired and
  Stopped states with the exact next action.
- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion and the established Cockpit visual language.
- Keep normal singleton and schedule health quiet; surface only actionable exceptions.

## Acceptance

A source-blind owner opens all three counted shapes without terminal or provider knowledge and can
distinguish deployed, native and streamed execution. Failed and empty states explain the actual missing
condition and offer no inert action.

## Makes deletable

Provider-oriented resource detail as the only opening path, fake iframe universality and decorative
service cards with no working action.
