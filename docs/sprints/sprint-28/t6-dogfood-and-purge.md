# S28-T6 — Prove one readable causal narrative and purge

**Layer:** Full owner-facing slice + Evaluation.

**Serves:** Every Sprint 28 criterion.

**Depends on:** S28-T0–T5.

**Observed friction:** Attention, Work, artifacts and chat can each look improved while still telling
different stories. Automated structure tests cannot prove that a person understands the result.

**Makes deletable:** Losing schema/renderer branches, duplicate presentation copy, legacy prose-only
paths for current records and the claim that prompt presence proves readable output.

## Outcome

One `_test` company produces a real prepared outcome, presents a consequential owner choice, records
the explicit action, resumes the correct Work, exposes the native artifact and reports the observed
result. A blinded reader reconstructs that causal narrative from the product without source coaching.

## Run

1. Replay every frozen corpus item through the selected contracts and compositions.
2. Run one live source-backed decision from creation through owner action and downstream observation.
3. Include one long but necessary explanation, one concise ordinary conversation and one materially
   uncertain recommendation so the test does not reward only short copy.
4. Capture desktop, narrow mobile and keyboard-only operation.
5. Give a reader who has not seen the implementation the first view, then ask the questions in the
   sprint measures. Record their account before showing source evidence.
6. Challenge every factual clause and control consequence against its owning source.
7. Compare Attention, decision history, Work and conversation for causal agreement.
8. Delete rejected contract shapes, duplicate copy and renderer branches not exercised by the run.

## Acceptance

- All twelve sprint criteria pass on recorded evidence.
- Structural tests cover source/action/provenance/freshness invariants and are not described as prose
  quality evidence.
- The reader identifies purpose, owner need, recommendation, choice consequences and next ownership
  without opening raw evidence; any mistake is a failed criterion, not coached away.
- Evidence expansion reveals the exact source without contradicting the primary explanation.
- No external effect, spend, publication or outreach occurs unless separately authorised by the
  existing Authority path.
- The report records where prose remained the superior form and which tempting structure was
  deliberately not built.

## Closing artifacts

- frozen before/after corpus;
- generated contract snapshots;
- source-fidelity audit;
- browser and accessibility evidence;
- blinded reader account and timings;
- end-to-end causal trace;
- rejected-branch and deletion report; and
- residual risks with explicit dispositions.
