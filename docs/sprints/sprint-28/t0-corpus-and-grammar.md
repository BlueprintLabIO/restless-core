# S28-T0 — Freeze the reader corpus and semantic grammar

**Layer:** Product contract + Evaluation.

**Serves:** Sprint 28 criteria 1, 2, 4, 6, 9 and 11.

**Observed friction:** The prior owner-brief work was necessarily tested against a small number of
long Attention items. One fixture cannot establish a communication grammar for decisions, reviews,
human steps, Work, artifacts and conversation.

**Makes deletable:** Ad hoc screenshots, remembered examples and one-off copy discussions as the
acceptance basis.

## Outcome

A versioned corpus records the existing source truth and owner-visible presentation for the varied
cases named in the sprint. Alongside it, one concise grammar defines which distinctions are shared,
which are surface-specific and which remain free-form judgement.

## Scope

- Capture every case named under the sprint's frozen acceptance corpus.
- Preserve the exact source record, owner projection and rendered output separately.
- For each case, record the reader's real question, required facts, material uncertainty, available
  source operations and what must remain inspectable.
- Classify each content relationship before choosing a visual form: sequence, alternatives,
  independent selections, parallel facts, change/diff, criteria or nuanced explanation.
- Identify repeated fields and contradictions in the current Attention, Work, artifact and message
  views without prescribing the replacement schema first.
- Record whether any real independently composable choice earns multi-select.

## Acceptance

- The corpus can regenerate its owner projections from frozen source inputs.
- Every required fact is traceable to a source observation or explicitly marked authored judgement.
- At least one case would be harmed by a rigid short template, proving that free-form explanation must
  remain available.
- At least one current wall is shown to result from semantic repetition rather than merely long
  sentences.
- The grammar states the conditions for list, comparison, timeline, single-choice, multi-select,
  bounded input and prose presentation.
- Founders approve the corpus and grammar before T1 freezes wire changes.

## Non-goals

- choosing final visual styling;
- scoring prose quality automatically;
- manufacturing a multi-select fixture to justify the component; or
- treating synthetic external outcomes as observed reality.
