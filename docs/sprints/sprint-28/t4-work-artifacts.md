# S28-T4 — Give Work and artifacts a human opening without weakening the contract

**Layer:** OrgIntel + Company Runtime + owner cockpit.

**Serves:** Sprint 28 criteria 6–8, 10 and 12.

**Depends on:** S28-T1 and S28-T2.

**Observed friction:** Work outcomes still ask one text field to serve a person and an executing actor,
and artifact/gate names are understandable only when the author remembers how the cockpit will render
them.

**Makes deletable:** Prompt-parsing a human opening out of a machine contract and command-produced
artifact labels as primary owner copy.

## Outcome

A Work view opens with what will exist or what was achieved and why it matters, while the executing
actor retains the exact contract unchanged. Important artifacts are recognisable, availability is
observed and native opening remains the primary review path.

## Scope

- Use T0 evidence to decide whether Work needs one narrow attributed owner summary distinct from its
  exact outcome contract. Do not add it merely because the sprint predicts it.
- If added, define who writes it, when it becomes stale and how the exact contract remains primary
  actor input.
- Give important artifact references an owner-readable label and contextual purpose through the
  smallest existing Work/artifact seam.
- Project observed availability separately from authored description.
- Present native artifacts first; keep commits, paths, gates and raw contract detail available behind
  deliberate disclosure.
- Keep ordinary files and source tools authoritative; add no artifact custody or universal editor.

## Acceptance

- A reader can state the Work outcome without reading its execution contract.
- The actor receives byte-for-byte equivalent exact constraints and evidence rules after the change.
- Changing the contract invalidates or visibly stales any summary tied to the prior revision.
- Artifact availability comes from a probe/current source state, never from confident prose.
- Unsupported artifact types receive an honest label and evidence path rather than “not ready.”
- No second artifact record, file-copy lifecycle or renderer catalogue is introduced.

## Decision gate

If the frozen corpus shows that the existing two-paragraph authoring convention is consistently
separable without parsing and remains readable in real runs, T4 keeps the current schema and improves
projection only. If it fails, the observed repetition earns one narrow Work summary field. Record the
branch and purge the losing representation.
