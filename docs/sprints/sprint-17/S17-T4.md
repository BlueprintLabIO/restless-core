# S17-T4 — Correlate and route untrusted inbound mail

**Outcome:** Inbound mail reaches the nearest already-accountable actor with exact source/thread/Work
context and an explicit untrusted-evidence boundary.

**Layer:** OrgIntel projection and Runtime context.

**Observed friction served:** Every inbound reply currently wakes Exec, lacks thread/Work correlation
and presents sender content without a sufficiently strong trust boundary.

**Acceptance:** Exact Work/thread/account metadata wins; known departmental mail reaches its lead;
only unresolved/conflicting portfolio mail reaches Exec; delivery-only events do not wake models;
material replies/bounces do; bodies are bounded and fenced; links/HTML grant no authority;
attachments are references/quarantined and never executed.

**Deletion:** Exec-as-inbox routing and context-free `REPLY` projection.

**Evidence:** Deterministic correlation cases plus injection, attachment, burst and mid-Work controls.
