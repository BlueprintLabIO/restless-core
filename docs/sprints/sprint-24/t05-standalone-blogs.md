# S24-T5 — Make every Blog route independently readable

Open every Blog directly and supply its own title, deck, definitions, claim, rendered evidence,
limits and human-readable public source destinations. Remove visitor-facing `.md` filenames, local
paths and repository-only evidence obligations; explain non-public evidence plainly.

**Observed friction:** route independence currently masquerades as editorial independence.

**Deletion target:** internal authoring and evidence locators exposed as reader navigation.
