# S12-T4 — Present one accountable outcome

**Layer:** Owner gateway + cockpit.

**Observed friction served:** owner truth can fragment across Work status, Staff narration, process
state and artifact links, tempting a team-administration dashboard instead of a prepared outcome.

## Outcome

The owner sees one accountable lead, outcome state, consequential risk and native ReviewTarget. Staff
contributions and recovery facts are progressively disclosed and remain attributable to their real
Work/Attempts.

## Acceptance

- Primary projection shows the accountable lead and native outcome, not agent topology.
- Solo, accepted Staff, revised Staff and rejected Staff contributions are distinguishable on
  inspection from Work/Attempt/artifact evidence.
- A blocked unknown-outcome recovery names the exact preserved target and judgement needed without
  reporting success or failure.
- The lead prepares and probes a live/playable/rendered/native target where feasible.
- The cockpit never displays hidden reasoning, prompts, secrets or a second transcript.
- Desktop and mobile visual review follows the owner-cockpit and frontend reference rules before the
  ticket closes.

## Non-goals

- a people/task dashboard;
- live presence theatre or raw event feeds;
- owner controls for routine team staffing.

## Deletion target

Duplicate status surfaces, team-admin-first views and generic review links that require owner setup.
