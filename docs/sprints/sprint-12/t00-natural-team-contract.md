# S12-T0 — Freeze the natural-team recovery contract

**Layer:** Cross-layer.

**Observed friction served:** the natural accountable-lead rule is implemented in prompts and specs,
but process exit, artifact observation, message delivery and owner projection can still imply
different completion stories.

## Outcome

One executable contract maps each Sprint 12 fact to exactly one writer and names the negative controls
that would expose duplicate ownership or guessed completion. The contract reuses Sprint 11 evidence
and identifies the exact missing code paths before other tickets change them.

## Acceptance

- Map Exec dispatch, team lead, Work, Attempt, process observation, artifact reference, message, native
  review and owner projection to their authoritative owners.
- Demonstrate in current code where structured completion, crash and supervisor restart diverge.
- Freeze scenarios A–F and the expected observed evidence for each.
- Name which Sprint 11 contracts are already evidenced, reused but unproved, or missing.
- Prove that no proposed Sprint 12 field represents the lead's plan, reasoning or meeting rhythm.

## Non-goals

- implementing the repair;
- adding a lifecycle or universal result type;
- declaring Sprint 11 complete by inspection.

## Deletion target

Any duplicate completion rule, callback-as-truth wording or ticket that assigns the same fact to two
layers.
