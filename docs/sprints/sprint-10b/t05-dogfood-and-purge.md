# S10b-T5 · Dogfood the clear Attention floor and purge

**Layer:** Full owner-facing slice; source truth remains with OrgIntel and Runtime.

**Serves:** Sprint 10b criteria 15–18 and the exit evidence.

**Depends on:** S10b-T0–T4.

**Observed friction:** A runnable charming demo does not prove that the office helps the owner,
represents live state honestly, survives real organisation shapes or deserves the empty Attention
surface.

**Makes deletable:** the prior clear-state placeholder, losing production concepts, unused amenity
assets, status-only animation, experimental renderer/path code and obsolete office CSS.

## Outcome

One `_test` run and one passing-doctor real-company run show that the retained company floor is calm,
cared for, truthful, navigable and performant. Founder comprehension decides whether it replaces the
prior clear state. The sprint closes with one canon and a run report.

## Work

- Exercise representative topology and truth fixtures without writing simulation into a live company.
- Start the selected real company through the supported local stack and pass its doctor.
- Confirm a real observed person opens matching canonical Work detail and return restores the floor.
- Review clear, active, waiting and degraded Attention states at 390/768/1440 CSS pixels.
- Record source, geometry, frame/asset, accessibility and founder-comprehension evidence separately.
- Name and remove the least useful accessory plus every losing runtime path.

## Acceptance

- The founder can distinguish observed work from ambient life without explanation.
- The owner can identify who owns the current outcome and open the canonical detail.
- Stale/unavailable state never reads as a clear queue or a busy company.
- The office is judged calmer and more useful than the prior placeholder; otherwise record the failed
  premise and do not force it into production.
- `sprint-10b/run-report.md` lists exact inputs, observations, deletions and remaining accepted risks.

## Deletion

Purge losing production layouts, flags, dependencies, custom paths, assets, CSS and fixed-fountain
assumptions. Keep the pinned vendor engine and only the Restless adapter/art needed by the accepted
floor.
