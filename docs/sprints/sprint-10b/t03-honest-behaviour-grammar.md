# S10b-T3 · Give people a small honest behaviour grammar

**Layer:** Owner projection consuming OrgIntel and Runtime observations.

**Serves:** Sprint 10b criteria 7–9 and 12.

**Depends on:** S10b-T2.

**Observed friction:** Characters can walk and idle, but motion alone cannot distinguish fresh work,
active-but-unobserved Work, source-owned waiting, availability, stale state or source failure. More
animation without one truth grammar would make the office less trustworthy as it became prettier.

**Makes deletable:** timer-inferred busy/waiting states, browser-invented current steps, stale working
loops, duplicate path controllers and any persisted office-behaviour history.

## Outcome

One interruptible in-memory behaviour controller maps source truth into work, waiting, ambient
restoration and degraded presence without becoming an organisational or life simulation.

## Work

- Implement the Sprint 10b behaviour grammar and freshness rules.
- Associate fresh observations with the correct person and canonical Work/Attempt.
- Route available people only to reachable locally reserved restorative points.
- Pre-empt ambient behaviour immediately when source state changes.
- Keep interaction reservations ephemeral and release them on interruption, rebuild or removal.

## Verification

- Fixtures cover fresh observed, active-unobserved, waiting, available, stale, unknown and unavailable.
- Only fresh observed state claims a current step or semantic work animation.
- Waiting always has a source reference and canonical destination.
- Two people never occupy one exclusive interaction point; reload requires no recovery protocol.
- Ambient social proximity is never exposed as collaboration or progress.

## Deletion

Remove status-only animation, elapsed-time inference, persisted movement/event state and behavioural
branches unsupported by the committed first wave.
