# S10b-T1 · Author the first-wave amenity kit

**Layer:** Owner presentation and attributed artwork.

**Serves:** Sprint 10b criteria 5, 6, 12 and 16.

**Depends on:** S10b-T0.

**Observed friction:** Desks, generic plants and a landmark prove the renderer, but they do not make
the company feel comprehensively cared for. The scratch studies identify useful categories while
also showing how quickly furniture scatter becomes clutter.

**Makes deletable:** one-off canvas drawings for rejected amenities, concept-specific duplicates and
custom assets that do not survive the canonical composition.

## Outcome

Ship the smallest cohesive asset and metadata kit that communicates nourishment, focus, recovery,
reading, social comfort, practical care, greenery, pet friendliness and one whimsical signature.

## Work

- Define presentation-only footprint, draw order, interaction point, facing and optional animation
  metadata outside the vendor subtree.
- Prefer recombination/recolouring of the licensed base where it remains visually coherent.
- Author only the missing winning assets, at the existing sprite scale and sunlit palette.
- Record source, licence and authorship beside the retained pack.
- Give every interactive amenity at least one safe approach point; static amenities need no fake verb.

## Acceptance

- The full care baseline is legible without labels or a permanent legend.
- Furniture scale, outline, shadow and palette read as one art direction.
- No important route, person or status cue is hidden by decorative foreground mass.
- Pet furniture is omitted when pets are disabled.
- New artwork lives outside `static/vendor/pixel-agents` and retains provenance.

## Deletion

Remove duplicated prototypes, rejected centrepiece assets and any amenity whose only justification is
that it was already drawn.
