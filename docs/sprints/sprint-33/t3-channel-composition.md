# S33-T3 — Compile distinct web, email, product and social directions

**Serves:** [Sprint 33](../sprint-33.md) · Runtime / Design direction

## Outcome

Compile channel-aware art-direction briefs from one Visual release for a landing section, email or
newsletter header, product panel and social visual.

## Acceptance

- All four briefs share visual principles without prescribing one template.
- Each names information hierarchy, proof, density, imagery/motion role and native constraints.
- Email safely excludes unsupported browser effects and retains a coherent static fallback.
- Product composition starts from actual product interaction and vocabulary.
- Social composition remains legible without the accompanying post.
- An ambiguous visual departure becomes an explicit proposal or exception, not silent drift.
- Briefs are deterministic, size-bounded and free of irrelevant registry listings.

## Makes deletable

Universal hero recipes and channel guessed from output dimensions alone.
