# S33-T4 — Operate responsive, accessible and motion states natively

**Serves:** [Sprint 33](../sprint-33.md) · Runtime / Independent evaluation

## Outcome

Give an independent art director exact native artifacts across representative viewport, interaction and
motion states, with product evidence legible at the size where the owner encounters it.

## Acceptance

- Browser targets cover desktop, narrow, keyboard and complete reduced-motion states.
- Email/document targets use their real rendering constraints and fallbacks.
- Product panels verify focus, state transitions, text fit and actual product fidelity.
- Evidence records exact artifact digest, renderer, viewport and motion preference.
- The critic judges hierarchy, density, distinction, proof and visual defects—not screenshot beauty alone.
- Revision invalidates stale captures and verdicts.
- Mechanical accessibility failures block qualitative acceptance.

## Makes deletable

Single-viewport approval, stale screenshot verdicts and motion validated only by source inspection.
