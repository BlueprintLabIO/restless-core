# S33-T1 — Admit typed visual-language evidence and provenance

**Serves:** [Sprint 33](../sprint-33.md) · Company Identity / OrgIntel

## Outcome

Represent semantic tokens, typography roles, composition principles, imagery direction, motion
character, product-representation rules, approved primitives and rejected anti-examples as typed
Visual evidence in an identity release.

## Acceptance

- Each item has source, scope, channel relevance, rationale and evidence locator.
- Tokens remain semantic and cannot become arbitrary per-asset values.
- Motion evidence includes purpose and reduced-motion replacement.
- External primitives retain origin, licence and local adaptation status.
- Product-representation rules resolve to exact Company Truth/product evidence.
- Generated compositions cannot promote themselves.
- Retired evidence remains historical and is absent from new briefs.

## Makes deletable

Asset-local visual rules, unexplained design tokens and copied effects with lost provenance.
