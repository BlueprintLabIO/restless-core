# S33-T6 — Prove identity without decorative component theatre

**Serves:** [Sprint 33](../sprint-33.md) · Integrated evaluation

## Outcome

Produce and independently review the four frozen visual targets from one release, including restrained
controls and one requested visual departure.

## Acceptance

- Reviewers identify one company across four distinct compositions for product-grounded reasons.
- Product depictions are exact or clearly abstract; no uncanny middle survives.
- Every target passes native responsive/accessibility requirements and has appropriate motion fallback.
- At least one effect-rich candidate loses to a restrained control.
- The requested departure is rejected, scoped as an exception or promoted through owner judgement.
- Registry/context use and visual reversals are measured.
- Closing purge removes component quotas, screenshot-only gates and duplicate token sources.

## Closing artifact

A native owner gallery plus exact evidence manifest and independent decision report.
