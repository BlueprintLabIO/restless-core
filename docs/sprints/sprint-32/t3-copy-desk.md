# S32-T3 — Separate drafting from blinded human-copy evaluation

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · Accountable lead / Evaluation

## Outcome

Give consequential writing a fresh-context copy desk that receives truth, channel, author, relevant
voice evidence and the exact rendered candidate—but not the producer's rationale.

## Acceptance

- The copy desk can accept, reject or return concrete revision findings.
- It checks abstraction, unsupported claims, repetition, channel mismatch and signability.
- The plainspoken control competes with ambitious candidates under the same evidence.
- Anti-patterns are judgement prompts, not deterministic bans.
- Copy desk verdict binds to exact artifact identity and becomes stale after revision.
- Trivial UI copy may remain one direct pass when exact native verification is sufficient.
- No tier requires a fixed critic, loop or candidate count.

## Makes deletable

Self-review, rationale-contaminated approval and regex-based “humaniser” passes.
