# S32-T2 — Compile one identity into distinct channel contracts

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · Runtime / Exec

## Outcome

Translate the released Voice plus audience, channel, author and consequence into a bounded contract
that preserves company identity while changing information order and expression appropriately.

## Acceptance

- Six frozen channels compile materially different contracts from the same facts.
- Every contract names reader situation, desired understanding/action, proof and author relationship.
- Founder, support, product and institutional authorship remain distinct.
- Channel contracts cannot change truth, culture, external authority or identity release.
- Ambiguous channel or absent author remains visible rather than defaulting to marketing voice.
- Continuations retain their contract unless an explicit owner change warrants a new binding.

## Makes deletable

One universal company-voice prompt and channel behavior inferred from file extension alone.
