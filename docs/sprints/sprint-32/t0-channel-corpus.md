# S32-T0 — Freeze human, AI-pattern and channel-transfer examples

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · Product contract / Evaluation

## Outcome

Freeze human-authored positives, plausible AI-heavy negatives and same-fact channel fixtures for
founder email, newsletter, support, transactional email, product UI and Blog.

## Acceptance

- Positive examples have real author, audience, context and reason for inclusion.
- Negative examples name the concrete failure rather than merely “sounds AI”.
- One plainspoken control exists for every consequential fixture.
- First-pass evaluators do not see candidate mode, prompt or production rationale.
- Human review assesses factuality, channel fitness, authorship and meaning retained after compression.
- No phrase, word-count, sentence-length or readability threshold is pass/fail.

## Makes deletable

Taste-only feedback and retrospective selection of examples that flatter the implementation.
