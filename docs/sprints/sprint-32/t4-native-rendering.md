# S32-T4 — Review email, UI, Blog and newsletter in native form

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · Runtime / Native evidence

## Outcome

Render and operate each frozen channel where readers encounter it, including email constraints,
product layout, Blog measure and newsletter hierarchy.

## Acceptance

- Email targets render in representative narrow and desktop clients with text fallback.
- Product UI copy is tested in its real state, action and recovery path.
- Blog copy is standalone and has no internal-file dependency.
- Newsletter hierarchy supports reading rather than reproducing a landing page.
- Links, subjects, preheaders, focus, truncation and responsive wrapping are verified where applicable.
- Review captures exact artifact and renderer identities.
- Screenshots may support but never replace semantic and interaction checks.

## Makes deletable

Markdown-only copy approval and browser-only assumptions about email or product presentation.
