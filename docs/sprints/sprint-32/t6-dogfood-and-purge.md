# S32-T6 — Prove human consistency without homogenisation

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · Integrated evaluation

## Outcome

Run the six-channel corpus from one identity release, including plain controls, independent copy desk,
native rendering and one owner edit that becomes a scoped proposal.

## Acceptance

- No cross-channel factual contradiction survives.
- Blinded reviewers identify one company and six credible contexts for concrete reasons.
- Named authors remain distinguishable and can plausibly sign their work.
- At least one ambitious candidate loses to its plain control.
- Owner edit burden falls relative to the frozen baseline without hiding unresolved taste.
- Context limits, copy-desk reversals and concepts removed are recorded.
- Closing purge removes phrase-blacklist, universal-cadence and duplicate voice paths.

## Closing artifact

One reviewable channel gallery and decision report containing full standalone artifacts, not snippets
or references to inaccessible internal files.
