# S32-T1 — Admit approved/rejected and named-author voice evidence

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · Company Identity / OrgIntel

## Outcome

Give Voice evidence typed semantics for approved passage, rejected passage, expression principle,
vocabulary, named-author evidence and channel observation, all bound to source and release.

## Acceptance

- Evidence records author, audience, channel, scope, source and judgement.
- A rejection records why and where it applies; it is not a global banned phrase.
- Named-author evidence cannot silently become the anonymous company voice.
- Product terminology resolves to Company Truth rather than duplicating it.
- Generated candidate text remains ordinary artifact evidence until owner promotion.
- Retired evidence remains historical and cannot appear in newly compiled briefs.

## Makes deletable

Flat tone-adjective lists, global phrase bans and copy-pasted author personas.
