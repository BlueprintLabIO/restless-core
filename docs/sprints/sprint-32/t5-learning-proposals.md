# S32-T5 — Turn edits into reviewable observations, never auto-policy

**Serves:** [Sprint 32](../sprint-32.md) · OrgIntel / Owner judgement

## Outcome

Convert meaningful owner edits and repeated copy-desk findings into attributed Voice proposals with
before/after evidence, scope and an explanation the owner can approve, reject or narrow.

## Acceptance

- Pure typo and fact corrections do not become style doctrine.
- A proposal cites exact before/after artifact identities and the decision that motivated it.
- Repeated evidence may increase confidence but cannot auto-promote.
- Rejected proposals remain historical and do not poison future retrieval.
- Narrowing can bind an observation to author or channel.
- Proposal generation is event-driven and bounded, not a continuous background rewrite.

## Makes deletable

Unattributed “learned style”, hidden preference accumulation and self-training prompt loops.
