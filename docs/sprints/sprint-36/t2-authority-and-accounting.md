# S36-T2 — Authorise exact publication and account for it

**Layer:** Authority Plane

**Observed outcome or friction:** Building software is not authority to expose it, spend on hosting,
widen its audience or keep it alive indefinitely.

**Work:** Admit one effect plus resource envelope bound to publication, candidate, profile, audience,
expiry, connection ceiling and compute. Keep invite-only and public decisions distinct. Send one
idempotent provider operation and retain exact pending, ready, failed, usage and terminal receipts.

**Evidence:** Replay never creates a second charge or endpoint; widening, renewal and resource growth
require new authority; ambiguous provider settlement remains local to the exact publication.

**Deletion target:** Implicit deploy permission, company-wide uncertainty and a generic command or
resource state machine.
