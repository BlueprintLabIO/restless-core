# S07-T2 · Authored attention meaning and truthful action semantics

**Layer:** OrgIntel + owner projection.  
**Serves:** Sprint 07 criteria 4, 5, 9 and 10.  
**Depends on:** S07-T1.  
**Observed friction:** any Exec `owner_judgement` currently reaches the owner, whether or not it is
owner-only or prepared, and the projection invents generic meaning and review actions.  
**Makes deletable:** generic judgement copy, Work-title reuse and the assumption that every ordinary
handoff is an outcome review.

## Outcome

Owner attention is admitted only after the actor below the owner has made and recorded the judgement
that it is irreducible. The final ordinary judgement carries a current authored brief. Its kind
selects truthful affordances; the projection maps source-backed fields and never summarizes raw logs.

## Scope

- Route Exec-authored ordinary judgement to Exec first; final Exec→owner escalation is explicit.
- Refuse that final hop when the brief is absent, malformed or stale.
- Keep the five irreducible human categories direct and render a minimal factual fallback when no
  optional brief exists.
- Project authored headline, situation, impact, recommendation, no-action consequence, author and
  freshness. Preserve all raw evidence behind disclosure.
- Derive review acceptance only for `outcome_review`; do not label a decision or blocker as review.

## Verification

- A locally resolvable judgement remains in the lead/Exec queue and never appears in owner Attention.
- An unprepared or stale final escalation is refused without losing the row.
- A current brief reaches Attention unchanged and attributed.
- Outcome review actions still call the existing Work review transition; conversation alone does not
  resolve any handoff.

## Risks

- **Urgent last mile waits for prose — guarded:** direct identity/CAPTCHA/MFA/legal/payment handoffs
  retain their direct route and factual fallback.
- **Admission is mistaken for deterministic relevance scoring — invariant:** the actor judges
  owner-only need; code validates only source, attribution, freshness and the presence of semantics.
