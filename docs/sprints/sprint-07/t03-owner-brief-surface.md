# S07-T3 · Owner meaning first, evidence progressively disclosed

**Layer:** Owner surface.  
**Serves:** Sprint 07 criteria 1, 6, 7, 8, 11 and 12.  
**Depends on:** S07-T2.  
**Observed friction:** implementation prose, four equal fact rows and inline evidence force the owner
to reconstruct the story before discovering the ask.  
**Makes deletable:** primary rendering of raw `prepared_state` and the equal-weight fact grid.

## Outcome

The queue is scannable and the focused item reads like a concise executive briefing: one headline,
one situation/consequence story, a recommendation, the exact next move and the effect of waiting.
Native outcomes remain first-class; technical evidence is one deliberate disclosure away.

## Scope

- Show the owner headline and short ask/state in each queue row.
- Build one raised, coloured decision folio with clear reading order and a source/author footer.
- Keep the recommendation visually distinct from observed situation without multiplying labels.
- Collapse raw notes, artifacts, gates and source references into an accessible evidence disclosure.
- Preserve keyboard focus, visible focus states, reduced motion and narrow-screen reading order.

## Verification

- Headless type/build checks pass and source-backed copy, author and action render from wire fields.
- Outcome target remains the primary review surface when live.
- Evidence content is absent from first-view prominence but remains reachable and complete.
- Queue rows remain distinguishable at their actual narrow width.

## Risks

- **Polish reads as persuasion — guarded:** observed situation and recommendation remain distinct;
  uncertainty and evidence access stay visible.
- **The card becomes generic dashboard chrome — guarded:** the chosen signature is an editorial
  decision folio with one consequential edge and next-move well, not a grid of interchangeable cards.
