# S09-T1 · Replace Authority with one stable Company shell

**Layer:** Owner surface over Authority, OrgIntel and Runtime observations.

**Observed friction:** The owner must read one dense Authority page where purpose, finance,
credentials, effects and diagnostics compete, while materially different companies would require
provider-specific UI branches.

## Outcome

Attention, Work, People and Company are the four primary areas. Company has six stable destinations:
Company charter, Authority & limits, Resources & access, External actions, Company computer and
Company doctor.

## Acceptance

- The charter is readable and read-only, includes safe legal identity when present and links current
  OrgIntel direction without merging it into the mandate.
- Limits answer independent, owner-required and prohibited authority questions.
- Resource rows render generic source metadata without provider or resource-kind branching.
- External actions show consequences and evidence, not shell/Git activity or a second approval queue.
- `/authority` redirects to the canonical Company authority destination.
- Hierarchy uses headings and space; explanations use focus-accessible tooltips rather than
  decorative eyebrows or repeated subtitles.

## Deletion

Deletes the standalone Authority destination, provider-specific Authority layout and its raw receipt
wall.
