# S09-T3 · Reuse the persistent desktop as a true Company focus mode

**Layer:** Runtime attach boundary and owner surface.

**Observed friction:** Sprint 05 proved a secure persistent desktop only from prepared Attention
handoffs. The owner still lacks a calm direct entrance and full-page reload/control continuity is not
an explicit contract.

## Outcome

The Company computer is a full-canvas threshold with one centred **Enter computer** action. Entering
opens the same private desktop transport, shows observer, owner and actor control honestly, and
returns to the exact Company location. Diagnostics and bounded recovery live on the separate Company
doctor page so they do not compete with the computer's single job.

## Acceptance

- Owner-initiated attach reuses the existing ticket, cookie, WSS proxy and controller lease.
- Before attachment, the computer fills the available surface and presents one clear, centred
  entrance rather than a diagnostic dashboard.
- Full SPA reload preserves the tab identity and a live lease; another tab remains observer-only.
- Return control does not resolve Work, approval or handoff state.
- Prepared Attention handoffs retain responsible actor and resume context; direct inspection invents
  none.
- Provider-root, finance, KYB and MFA flows remain outside the Company Runtime desktop.

## Deletion

Any copied attach/control implementation introduced by the Company route is removed; one reusable
focus component remains.
