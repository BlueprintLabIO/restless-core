# Sprint 33 — Make visual language durable and native

**Status:** Implemented and locally verified; matched live-model comparison provider-blocked

**Date:** 31 August 2026

**Programme:** [Company Identity](./company-identity-programme.md)

**Depends on:** Sprint 31. May run in parallel with Sprint 32 after the identity contract freezes.

## Why this sprint exists

Tokens and component libraries do not create art direction. Restless has repeatedly produced technically
valid pages whose graphics feel goofy, whose visual language ignores the product, or whose borrowed
effects become decoration without meaning. Approved product UI and the interaction registry already
contain stronger evidence, but there is no durable way to select and apply it across channels.

## Outcome

One released Visual Language produces a landing-page section, responsive newsletter/email header,
product-state panel and social announcement visual that are recognisably from the same company without
being the same composition. Every target is operated in its native environment and preserves product
truth, accessibility, responsiveness and reduced-motion behaviour.

## Visual-language contract

The pillar may contain:

- semantic tokens and typography roles;
- composition, density, hierarchy and whitespace principles;
- motion character, timing families and reduced-motion replacements;
- imagery, illustration, texture, icon and data-visualisation direction;
- exact rules for representing real product UI and evidence;
- approved components, effects and graphic primitives with source and licence;
- positive compositions and rejected anti-examples with judgement; and
- channel constraints for web, email, product, documents and social media.

A registry entry is a capability, not an instruction to use it. More effects are not a higher Outcome
Standard. Product UI remains the primary ground truth when public representation and decorative
inspiration conflict.

## Success contract

1. The four targets share an identifiable visual grammar without template cloning.
2. Every product depiction corresponds to the current product or is clearly abstract rather than
   uncanny pseudo-UI.
3. Motion carries meaning, state or emphasis; decorative motion that competes with comprehension loses.
4. Every motion pattern has a complete reduced-motion state, not merely a shorter duration.
5. Email and document targets degrade safely where browser effects are unavailable.
6. Typography, hierarchy, contrast, focus, responsive containment and content density pass native
   review.
7. A deliberately restrained control competes with effect-rich candidates; spectacle cannot win by
   component count.
8. Independent art direction reviews the rendered first view, scroll sequence and evidence legibility.
9. Visual evidence is bound to exact artifact identity and viewport/motion state.
10. No screenshot-only approval, theme-score service, mandatory component quota or wholesale external
    registry import is introduced.

## Slice per layer

**Authority / OrgIntel.** Promote typed Visual evidence and exceptions through the common identity
release, retaining source, licence, scope, rationale and exact artifact binding.

**Runtime.** Resolve product truth and registry resources, compile channel art direction, operate native
renderers and record viewport/motion/accessibility evidence.

**Exec / accountable lead.** Commission design engineering and independent art direction where it can
change the outcome; the lead does not reward novelty or component count by default.

**Cockpit.** Show visual evidence, versions, native targets and concrete drift without becoming a design
tool, component explorer or token editor.

## Salvage

No unverified salvage lift. Sprint 23 product-language material and Sprint 24 registry entries must be
revalidated against current product truth, framework support, licence and native behavior in T0/T2.

## Terminal decision

- **Pass:** independent reviewers identify the company across all four targets and cite product-grounded
  composition, typography, imagery or motion rather than logo repetition.
- **Revise once:** repair one bounded renderer, evidence or native-verification defect.
- **Stop negative:** if the pillar rewards component count, screenshots or visual sameness, preserve the
  evidence and do not promote it into Sprint 35.
