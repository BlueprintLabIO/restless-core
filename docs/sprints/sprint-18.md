## Why this sprint exists

The narrow experiments have done their job. They established useful priors about locally closing
work, elastic queues, non-producing leads and wide company operation. They also left two exact product
failures:

1. EXP-05's fourth owner request occupied the Exec-facing command for **69.432 seconds** while the
   new lead oriented. Durable dispatch did not return portfolio availability at the right boundary.
2. A terminal worker/gate failure did not deliver the exact causal failure to its lead. The lead could
   wait for a success-only callback even though the responsible Attempt had already ended.

The same run produced 90/90 exact units across four departments without observed cross-department
leakage, but its company-level evaluator response was malformed twice. That is an evaluation-harness
failure, not evidence that the organisation passed or failed.

More isolated coordination cells would now optimise the lab. The next informative step is to ask
whether Restless can use its own organisation to improve and operate Restless across a realistic,
multi-event product release.

## Founders' hypothesis

> Restless is ready to own one bounded release of itself: Exec remains available across simultaneous
> outcomes, accountable leads supervise rather than produce, Staff ships the exact product changes,
> material events redirect only affected responsibility, process replacement preserves truth, and the
> owner receives a runnable release candidate plus only the irreducible decisions.

This is a **company dogfood and acceptance sprint**, not a causal organisation experiment. A pass says
the whole product can sustain this operating story once. It does not prove that teams beat one strong
agent, that GLM-5.3 is the best model, or that the observed organisation generalises to every company.

## Outcome

Using Restless itself as the operating company, produce a beta-ready Restless release candidate that:

- makes Exec dispatch durably accepted and returns portfolio availability before lead model
  orientation completes;
- delivers exact terminal Attempt/gate failure evidence to the nearest accountable lead and supports
  repair or an explicit terminal judgment; and
- proves both changes in a live, prepared product scenario while a second owner outcome, a real
  provider-observed `_test` event, a material requirement change and one controlled process restart
  occur.

The owner receives the live-probed native scenario, exact evidence, release/rollback notes and any
bounded approvals. The sprint does **not** push, merge, deploy, publish or send to a real recipient.

## What is fixed and what remains intelligent

| Fixed before the run | Deliberately left to company judgment |
| --- | --- |
| Mission, success contract and current source commit | Team shape, actor count and assignment |
| GLM-5.3 model family and total spend ceiling | Decomposition, sequence and implementation design |

The scenario does not pre-create a department tour or prescribe a workflow. Organisational boundaries
must follow accountable outcomes discovered by Exec and the leads.

## Success contract

Sprint 18 passes only when the following are observed in the same versioned run.

### Useful product outcome

1. **Runnable release candidate.** The candidate is a concrete commit and a live-probed Restless stack,
   not only a diff, plan or passing unit suite. The primary ReviewTarget opens the exact operating
   scenario; commits, logs and tests are supporting evidence.
2. **Dispatch boundary repaired.** An executable owner request becomes durably owned, the caller
   returns before the commissioned lead finishes model orientation, and Exec can accept and dispatch a
   separate request while the first outcome remains active. Record request acceptance, lead launch,
   lead orientation and next-request acceptance separately.
3. **Exact failure reaches responsibility.** A controlled terminal Staff/gate failure records the
   actual check identifier, exit/terminal state and bounded diagnostic evidence; it wakes the
   accountable lead exactly once. The lead either commissions a successful repair or records an
   explicit terminal judgment. It cannot remain waiting on a success-only callback.

### Company operation

4. **One accountable organisation.** Exec delegates every executable outcome and performs no product
   work. Every accountable lead remains a supervisor and performs no planned production or silent
   artifact repair. Staff contributions and the lead that accepted them are attributable.
5. **Portfolio responsiveness.** The additional owner request is accepted while the release outcome is
   active. Its work and context do not leak into the release outcome, and ordinary status does not
   bounce through Exec.
6. **Sparse cross-outcome coordination.** Leads exchange only material facts, decisions, artifacts or
   dependencies. If continuous shared completion judgment emerges, one lead is made accountable rather
   than preserving a coordination ritual.
7. **No stale acceptance.** A material requirement change reaches the affected owner, changes or
   supersedes the affected Work/Attempt and prevents a pre-change candidate from being accepted.
   Unaffected work continues.

### Signals, recovery and authority

8. **One real inbound observation.** A provider-observed event in the dedicated `_test` world is
   authenticated/observed at its real boundary, recorded once, correlated to the nearest accountable
   responsibility and handled to an inspectable prepared result without polling or Exec-as-inbox.
   Controlled content remains test-world evidence and never becomes a live-company market/customer
   fact.
9. **Restart without operator repair.** After durable useful work exists, one exact daemon or actor
   process is stopped and replaced. The company resumes the relevant responsibility or reconstructs it
   explicitly, without duplicated Work/effects, false hot-session claims, transcript replay as new
   work or manual database/file repair.
10. **Prepared authority boundary.** Any external send, merge, push, deployment or publication remains
    prepared but unapplied unless the owner separately authorises its exact effect. Unknown outcomes
    are reconciled; they are never blindly retried.
11. **Prepared last mile.** The final owner review opens the runnable scenario and exact useful state,
    names the judgment requested and has an observable resume condition. It does not hand setup,
    agent management or ordinary debugging back to the owner.

### Evidence integrity

12. **No rescue hidden as collaboration.** The owner may supply the mandate, the planned independent
    request, the planned material change, bounded product/taste judgment, authority and final
    acceptance. Decomposition, chasing agents, repairing harness state, correcting unsupported claims
    and manually moving artifacts count as product failures.
13. **Truthful accounting.** The report records elapsed time, first-useful-output time, owner active
    minutes/intervention type, model/external spend, newly processed and cached usage where actually
    exposed, Work/Attempt attribution, duplicate/rework events and unavailable/unknown observations.
    Raw private reasoning is neither requested nor retained.
14. **Independent acceptance.** Deterministic checks judge only enumerable facts. An independent
    artifact review and the founder judge product quality from the native candidate. A malformed or
    missing evaluator decision remains evaluation-infrastructure-invalid and is not inferred from its
    prose.
15. **One honest result.** The run ends `accepted`, `rejected`, `inconclusive`, `product-invalid` or
    `evaluation-infrastructure-invalid`, with evidence for the exact classification. Partial success is
    preserved rather than promoted to a pass.

## Observation covenant

Once the run begins, founders observe rather than coach it through ordinary problems.

- Do not send status prompts merely because the company is quiet.
- Do not repair files, Work state, prompts, database rows or model output during the run.
- Do not add a worker, reroute Work or suggest an implementation unless the company asks for a genuine
  founder judgment covered by the attention envelope.
- Do record every founder intervention and its cause immediately.
- Let an ordinary failure reach its natural product state. Continue collecting evidence after a
  non-safety criterion fails when doing so is safe and still informative.

Operational liveness is event- and state-based. Missing heartbeats or an exited process may trigger
observation/reconciliation; a wall-clock timeout does not decide whether semantic work succeeded.
Spend, authority, data-integrity and founder stop boundaries remain hard governance limits.

## Problem classification

**Deterministic and enumerable:** durable dispatch acknowledgement; actor/Work attribution; process
exit; gate identifier/status; exact event identity; dedupe; supersession; approval boundaries; effect
receipts; usage fields supplied by the provider; whether the native target can be opened and probed.

**Judgment and open-ended:** product design, implementation strategy, team shape, whether an event is
material, how to repair the failure, whether the candidate is beta-ready and what the owner should
approve. Static routers, workflow stages and score thresholds must not replace these judgments.

## Risks and dispositions

| Risk | Disposition | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-dogfood corrupts the branch or runtime evaluating it | **Invariant** | Dedicated `_test` company, isolated worktrees/database, frozen starting commit and recoverable candidate branch; no merge/push |
| A test event becomes a real business fact | **Invariant** | `_test` world marker on inbound record, projection and report; real transport validates mechanism only |
| Exec or a lead repairs the product privately | **Invariant** | Attempts, commits and artifact authorship must trace production to Staff |
| A process restart is mistaken for semantic failure or success | **Invariant** | Terminal observation and semantic result remain separate; resume/reconstruct from durable facts |
| A real external effect escapes the test | **Invariant** | Owner-controlled test endpoints and existing Authority approval/effect gates |
| The broad run makes causal claims | **Guarded** | Treat it as acceptance/diagnosis; follow with a narrow experiment only for a repeated disputed mechanism |
| The company fails early on a known blocker | **Accepted** | Preserve the failure and continue only where safe; do not patch it from outside during the run |
| GLM-5.3 quality or provider capacity dominates the result | **Accepted and recorded** | Freeze the exact model/provider envelope; report provider failures separately from product behavior |
| Observation instrumentation changes behaviour | **Guarded** | Capture existing material events and evidence references; no polling/status narration or private reasoning collection |

## Non-goals

- proving a universal optimal team size, routing threshold, fan-in pattern or effort policy;
- comparing model families or claiming that GLM-5.3 is optimal;
- a universal event/signal schema, workflow engine, blackboard, shared-history primitive or new work graph;
- a production deployment, merge to `dev`/`main`, Git push, public announcement or real-recipient send;
- an exhaustive chaos suite, arbitrary multi-department pageant or benchmark leaderboard;
- automating founder taste, authority, legal attestation or final product acceptance; and
- treating test-world provider content as demand, customer feedback or strategy evidence.

## Entry, stop and exit gates

**Entry:** founders accept this v0.1 contract, the dedicated `_test` world, GLM-5.3 as the frozen model
family, a **USD 50** all-model ceiling, the real provider test endpoint to use, and the no-rescue
observation covenant. The source commit and provider/model identifiers are recorded immediately before
launch.

**Stop:** stop immediately for credential/tenant crossover, uncontrolled external recipient or
publication, possible data corruption outside the isolated candidate, spend-ceiling breach, an
irreconcilable effect outcome with risk of duplication, or founder stop. Pause for new founder
authority before any push, merge, deployment, public send or paid resource. Ordinary product failure
is evidence, not an automatic reason to intervene.

**Exit:** the run has a truthful terminal classification and complete evidence bundle. A product pass
requires every success-contract item; an informative rejection still completes the dogfood sprint if
the failure is preserved, attributed, reviewed and converted into the smallest next repair rather than
explained away or expanded into speculative machinery.
