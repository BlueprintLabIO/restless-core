# S34-T6 — Prove conduct, reject slogans and purge surveillance shapes

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Integrated evaluation

## Outcome

Run all five culture cases across restart, including one counterexample and one valid disagreement that
changes the initially favoured decision.

## Acceptance

- Reviewers cite exact conduct and decisions rather than value labels.
- Stable method survives different actors and at least one different final decision.
- Uncertainty and correction remain truthful and externally appropriate.
- Customer recovery contains both communication and bounded action.
- Hiring evidence is job-relevant and free from culture-fit/personality proxies.
- Owner interactions map only to promotion, exception, authority or irreducible judgement.
- Closing purge proves no worker scoring, sentiment surveillance, personality typing or culture-policy
  engine remains.

## Closing artifact

One casebook of exact inputs, decisions, native outputs, independent judgements and promoted/declined
observations.
