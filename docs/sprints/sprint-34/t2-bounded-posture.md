# S34-T2 — Compile relevant norms into actor and team posture

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Runtime / Staff context

## Outcome

Compile only the cultural evidence relevant to an actor's responsibility, current consequence and
decision boundary, while preserving role, Work and Authority as the actual execution controls.

## Acceptance

- Same culture release yields different bounded context for support, research, engineering and Exec.
- Context names observed conduct and counterexample rather than value slogans.
- Irrelevant company history and other employees' behavior are absent.
- Culture cannot override Work, owner judgement, safety or effect controls.
- Staff may disagree with a proposed decision and surface contradictory evidence.
- Context bytes are accounted and bounded with explicit omissions.
- No personality profile or actor score is created.

## Makes deletable

One company-wide culture monologue injected into every actor wake.
