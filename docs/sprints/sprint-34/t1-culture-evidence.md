# S34-T1 — Define promotable observed-behaviour evidence

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Company Identity / OrgIntel

## Outcome

Represent founding decisions and repeated observed conduct with situation, consequence, decision,
evidence, scope, confidence, counterexample and operational implication.

## Acceptance

- Abstract value words without behaviour are rejected.
- Observation, founding owner decision, promoted norm and exception remain distinct.
- Every norm names where it should not apply or what evidence would revise it.
- Counterexamples are first-class evidence rather than deleted embarrassment.
- Model-written interpretation cannot promote itself.
- Historical norms remain bound to the releases and outcomes that used them.
- Culture evidence cannot grant authority or suppress required escalation.

## Makes deletable

Generic values documents and prompt fragments that turn adjectives into operating rules.
