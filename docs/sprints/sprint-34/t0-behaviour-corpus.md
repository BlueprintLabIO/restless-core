# S34-T0 — Freeze decisions, dissent, incidents and counterexamples

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Product contract / Evaluation

## Outcome

Freeze observed and synthetic-safe cases for disagreement, uncertainty, customer-visible error,
quality trade-off, hiring conduct and a counterexample where an attractive slogan would cause harm.

## Acceptance

- Each case names consequence, actors, authority, evidence, decision and observed outcome.
- Existing company behavior is recorded before Culture guidance is added.
- Evaluators judge conduct without seeing value labels or the model's rationale.
- One case permits different final decisions if the same cultural method is preserved.
- No employee performance, personality, sentiment or demographic inference enters the corpus.
- External effects remain synthetic or separately authorised.

## Makes deletable

Culture-by-slogan evaluation and scenarios rewritten after seeing the implementation.
