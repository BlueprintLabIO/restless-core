# S34-T5 — Govern norms, counterexamples, exceptions and retirement

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Authority / Owner cockpit

## Outcome

Let the owner inspect the evidence behind Culture proposals, approve or narrow a norm, retain a
counterexample, create a bounded exception and retire obsolete guidance through identity releases.

## Acceptance

- Promotion presents observed behavior and consequence before proposed wording.
- Counterexamples and dissent remain visible after promotion.
- Narrowing can bind a norm to situation, role or consequence without a generic policy scope language.
- Exceptions cannot excuse safety, discrimination, deception or authority violations.
- Retirement affects new briefs and preserves historical bindings.
- Ordinary behavior observations do not create owner Attention until a consequential proposal exists.
- No employee leaderboard, sentiment panel or culture score appears.

## Makes deletable

Unreviewable lead-authored culture rules and noisy owner approval of ordinary conduct.
