# S34-T4 — Apply culture to customer recovery and scenario-based hiring

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Cross-channel native outcomes

## Outcome

Create one customer-recovery response/action package and one hiring brief whose expectations are
expressed through observable situations and conduct.

## Acceptance

- Customer communication acknowledges the concrete harm, known facts, next action and accountable owner.
- Recovery action stays within existing authority and is not replaced by empathetic prose.
- Hiring criteria use job-relevant scenarios and observable behavior.
- Personality, “culture fit”, protected traits and demographic proxies are absent.
- Both artifacts remain human, channel-appropriate and bound to Voice where Sprint 32 passed.
- Native reviewers can trace every cultural expectation to released evidence.

## Makes deletable

Poster-value hiring copy and support language that performs empathy without operational recovery.
