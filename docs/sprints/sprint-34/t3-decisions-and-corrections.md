# S34-T3 — Exercise disagreement, uncertainty and visible correction

**Serves:** [Sprint 34](../sprint-34.md) · Exec / Lead / Decision evidence

## Outcome

Run bounded cases where Staff disagree, evidence remains incomplete and an earlier claim must be
corrected. Evaluate the conduct and decision account, not agreement with a predetermined answer.

## Acceptance

- Dissent reaches the accountable decision-maker without process spam or retaliation language.
- The final record preserves material alternatives and why one was chosen.
- Unknown remains unknown; urgency does not manufacture certainty.
- Correction names what changed, consequence and current truth without erasing the original.
- Different defensible decisions may pass if evidence and cultural method are credible.
- Owner is interrupted only for authority or irreducible judgement.
- No evaluator rewards reciting culture prose.

## Makes deletable

Consensus theatre, uncertainty euphemisms and reputation-managed deletion of mistakes.
